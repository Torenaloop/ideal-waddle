### 1. The RCA trigger is clearly justified

The parent task correctly identifies that the injury required treatment beyond first aid and that restricted duty was involved. That supports the decision to run a full RCA rather than a lite review.

The task also appropriately separates the RCA investigation from parallel lanes: workers’ comp, HR/light duty, and medical/return-to-work follow-up. That is a strong governance point.

### 2. Phase 1 has useful confirmations, but some records need cleanup

Phase 1 is marked complete. The comment indicates the following were confirmed:

- Workers’ comp carrier notified.
    
- Light-duty offer documented.
    
- Medical follow-up tracking established.
    
- Safety notification criteria not met.
    
- Incident blade/PPE evidence could not be preserved because the site does not have the blade.
    

Concerns:

- The medical follow-up comment says **“skin graft surgery scheduled 5/7/25”**, which appears to be a typo and should likely be **5/7/26**.
    
- The SOSPES record number is not visible in the task comments.
    
- The workers’ comp notification date/time and owner are not visible.
    
- Light-duty documentation is confirmed, but the document owner/location is not visible.
    
- The missing cutter blade should be explicitly treated as an evidence limitation in the final RCA.
    

### 3. Phase 2 identifies important systemic facts, but should not be marked complete without more detail

Phase 2 is marked complete. The key comment states:

- **JSA/MOP does not exist.**
    
- Inventory was up to par on the day of injury.
    
- The cutter was an **improvised choice**.
    
- Investigation was still pending.
    

That is important information. The absence of a JSA/MOP and the use of an improvised cutter are likely stronger root-cause contributors than “failure to wear cut-resistant gloves” alone.

Concern: Phase 2 says the investigation was still pending, but the phase is marked complete. The exit criteria called for a completed timeline and validated evidence binder. The Asana record does not show that those items were completed.

### 4. Phase 3 is the biggest gap

Phase 3 is marked complete, but the Asana task does not show substantive interview outputs, witness statements, completed 5-Why logic, or mapped root causes. There is an empty comment dated June 3.

For a full RCA, this is a major gap. The parent task says the RCA will move beyond the immediate behavioral finding and evaluate conditions such as JSA coverage, PPE availability, tool selection, training, and pre-task briefing. That is the right approach, but the actual 5-Why conclusion needs to be documented.

At minimum, Phase 3 should show:

- Who was interviewed.
    
- Interview dates.
    
- Whether witness statements were completed.
    
- The completed 5-Why chain.
    
- Final root causes.
    
- Direct, indirect, and systemic contributing factors.
    
- Evidence supporting each root cause.

## Recommended corrective actions before final review

Before this RCA is routed for final sign-off, I would require these updates in Asana:

1. **Add the completed 5-Why output to Phase 3.**  
    Do not leave the root cause implied. Document the actual logic chain and separate direct cause, contributing factors, and systemic root causes.
    
2. **Create CAPA subtasks under the parent RCA task.**  
    Suggested CAPA families based on the current record:
    
    - Create or revise JSA/MOP for floor tape replacement.
        
    - Standardize approved cutting tools for tape work.
        
    - Evaluate safer cutter options such as shielded or auto-retracting blades.
        
    - Confirm cut-resistant glove availability at point of work.
        
    - Add or refresh task-specific training for blade/tape replacement work.
        
    - Require a documented pre-task brief for cutting-tool tasks.
        
    - Add verification/audit step after implementation.
        
3. **For each CAPA, include owner, due date, verification method, and closure evidence.**  
    A CAPA without verification is only an action item, not a preventive control.
    
4. **Resolve the Phase 2 contradiction.**  
    If the investigation was still pending, either reopen Phase 2 or add the final evidence-binder/timeline confirmation.
    
5. **Correct the medical follow-up date typo.**  
    The comment showing surgery scheduled for **5/7/25** should be corrected or clarified.
    
6. **Document missing evidence as a limitation.**  
    The cutter blade not being available should be called out in the final RCA, along with how the investigation compensated for that gap.
    
7. **Add final report and sign-off status to Phase 5.**  
    The final task should clearly show whether the STACK Incident Review Template has been completed, internally QA’d, routed to VP EHS, and linked in SOSPES.
