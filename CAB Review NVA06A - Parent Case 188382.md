## JSA / Procedure Gap Review

The procedure scope is broader than the current JSA. The MOP covers de-energizing **PDU-P4-230-02-BSW03**, replacing tap box **DD1048-1049(BLUE)**, using a **MEWP**, applying/removing **LOTO**, and re-energizing the breaker; it also identifies **arc flash risk**, **MEWP fall risk**, and an electrical incident energy of **28.3 cal/cm²**. The existing JSA only lists one task, **Opening/Closing Breakers**, with hazards of **Arc Flash** and **Electrical Shock**, and controls limited to dead fronts, standing to the side, and arc-flash PPE.

## Gap Analysis and Recommendations

- ✅ **Complete/Aligned:** The existing JSA addresses **opening/closing breakers**, **arc flash**, and **electrical shock**, which aligns with the breaker switching portions of the MOP.
    
- ✅ **Complete/Aligned:** The existing JSA includes 40 cal arc-flash PPE, safety glasses, hearing protection, FR clothing layers, and leather boots, which partially aligns with the MOP’s switching PPE.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA does not cover **LOTO application/removal**, although the MOP requires opening the breaker, placing LOTO, later removing LOTO, and closing the breaker.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA does not cover **MEWP inspection/use** or the MOP’s identified **MEWP fall risk**. The MOP specifically requires MEWP inspection and fall-arrest PPE for tap-box work.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA does not cover **tap-box removal/installation**, including actuator interlock operation, latch disengagement, supporting the tap box during release, removal, installation, and securing the replacement.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA does not address **pinch points, dropped-object exposure, struck-by hazards, cuts/lacerations, ergonomic strain, or housekeeping**, all of which are relevant to elevated tap-box replacement work.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA control language is too generic. “Proper PPE related to ARC Flash labeling” should be replaced or supplemented with the specific PPE listed in the MOP for switching and MEWP/tap-box work.
    
- ⚠️ **PPE Discrepancy:** The JSA lists **Class00 gloves**, while the MOP switching PPE lists **Class 0 rubber gloves with leather protectors**. This should be reconciled before approval.
    
- ⚠️ **Scope Mismatch:** The JSA activity is titled **“Energization of PDU output breakers for Data halls,”** but the MOP scope includes de-energization, tap-box replacement, MEWP use, and re-energization. Update the JSA activity title to reflect the full job scope.
    
- ⚠️ **Clarification Needed:** The MOP verifies tap-box breakers are open and whips are disconnected before removal, but the JSA should explicitly state how absence of hazardous electrical energy is confirmed before handling/removing the tap box. Confirm whether Operations, the Client, or another qualified party performs absence-of-voltage verification before Step 17.5–17.9.
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Revise the JSA activity to: **“De-energize PDU-P4-230-02-BSW03, replace tap box DD1048-1049(BLUE), and re-energize PDU output breaker.”**
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Add separate JSA rows for **LOTO**, **MEWP use**, **tap-box removal**, **tap-box installation**, **re-energization**, and **cleanup/housekeeping**.
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Replace vague controls with action-oriented controls such as: **“Apply LOTO and verify isolation before tap-box work,” “Inspect MEWP before use,” “Use fall arrestor while operating MEWP,” “Support tap box before releasing actuator,” “Barricade area below elevated work,” and “Remove LOTO only after personnel and tools are clear.”**
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Resolve the glove-class discrepancy and align the JSA PPE with the MOP’s task-specific PPE before supervisor approval.
