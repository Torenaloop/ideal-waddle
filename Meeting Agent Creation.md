

An agent that can read a Teams meeting agenda in Loop component format (not any text in the body of the email - our weekly agendas are in the Loop component) and, based on the agenda for the week, surface relevant content from my Microsoft ecosystem (Outlook emails and calendar, Teams content, and hopefully SharePoint) related to that week's content to help add value to the topic(s) at hand. I'd like this to be performed the morning of the day in which the meeting would take place.

Here’s a draft agent that turns your **Loop-based meeting agenda** into a **topic-by-topic prep brief** with questions, talking points, and a “what to look up” checklist you can use before the meeting.

- Built to ingest **pasted Loop agenda content** (or a share link you provide) and convert it into a structured prep pack
- Produces **discussion prompts, decision points, risks, and next steps** for each agenda item—tailored to your **Regional EHS Manager** perspective
- Includes a **copy/paste lookup plan** (search terms, people, and time windows) for finding relevant material across Outlook/Teams/SharePoint
- Notes that **automatic “run the morning of the meeting”** requires scheduled automation (available in the full Copilot Studio experience)

**Important note about accessing your Microsoft 365 content:** Your current Copilot experience doesn’t support direct access to **Outlook email, meetings, Teams messages, or SharePoint/OneDrive** for an agent. If you want the agent to actually retrieve that content (not just generate the lookup plan), you’ll need a Microsoft 365 Copilot–licensed setup that supports those knowledge connections—see [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/extensibility/prerequisites#agent-capabilities-and-licensing-models](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/extensibility/prerequisites#agent-capabilities-and-licensing-models).

Give it a spin — click and paste one of your weekly Loop agendas so it can generate a prep brief and lookup checklist for that meeting. When you're happy with the results, click to bring your agenda-based meeting prep agent to life.
