## Gap Analysis and Recommendations

- ✅ **Complete/Aligned:** The JSA generally recognizes static-bypass HMI operation, MSB breaker switching, vendor handoff/walkdown, battery-breaker restoration, transfer back to inverter, and physical cleanup.
    
- ⚠️ **Critical equipment mismatch:** The JSA identifies UPS-P1-130 and MSB-P1-130 equipment, while the procedure is for UPS-P6-240-01/-02 and MSB-P6-240-02-MOB/MIB. The existing JSA should not be used for this MOP until every equipment identifier and task is corrected.
    
- ⚠️ **Wrong operating method:** The JSA describes SKRU/key interlocks, MBB/UIB operation, and placing the lineup in maintenance bypass. None of those operations appears in the P6-240 MOP, which uses static bypass followed by direct MOB/MIB isolation of UPS-P6-240-02.
    
- ⚠️ **Irrelevant JSA content:** Li-ion Tamer testing, REI FA coordination, FACP reset, EPO/EMB operation, and restoration of the Fire Riser PA 1-9 valve are not included in the MOP. Remove these rows unless they are supported by a separate concurrent procedure.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing isolation detail:** The JSA lacks a dedicated task covering opening and applying LOTO to both the P6-240-02 MOB and MIB, confirming the unaffected UPS remains energized, and maintaining Operations LOTO through vendor work.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing Live-Dead-Live controls:** The MOP requires vendor personal LOTO and Live-Dead-Live testing, but the JSA does not identify meter rating, calibration, known-source verification before and after testing, applicable test points, stored-energy discharge, or tester PPE.
    
- ⚠️ **Breaker controls are too generic:** The JSA’s “Opening/Closing breakers on MSB” row identifies only arc flash and states “wear arc flash gear” and “utilize AMS.” Add shock, spring-charged mechanical energy, pinch points, line of fire, wrong-breaker selection, switching sequence, LOTO, load verification, two-person confirmation, and stop-work criteria.
    
- ⚠️ **Vendor work is undefined:** The MOP does not describe how the control power supply will be accessed, tested, removed, or repaired. A vendor-specific JSA/work instruction is needed before equipment handoff.
    
- ⚠️ **PPE and qualification gap:** The JSA header says only “Arc flash gear” and “S1 Qualified.” It should identify the exact MOP-listed 8 cal/cm² ensemble and distinguish personnel authorized for switching/LOTO from personnel qualified to perform absence-of-voltage testing.
    
- ⚠️ **Procedure sequencing concern:** MOP step 25 states UPS-P6-240-02 has been successfully de-energized, but Live-Dead-Live verification does not occur until step 29. Step 25 should state that the equipment is **isolated and pending absence-of-voltage verification**, with the equipment treated as energized until step 29 is complete.
    
- ⚠️ **Battery-cabinet inconsistency:** The MOP overview references “Batt Cab 3,” while the energy table and detailed steps identify only battery cabinets 0, 1, and 2.
    
- ⚠️ **Step 44 ambiguity:** The instruction says to close CPCB1 breakers for UPS-P6-240-02, but the sign-off table contains columns for both UPS-P6-240-01 and UPS-P6-240-02. The intended equipment must be unambiguous before execution.
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Replace the existing task rows with the P6-240-specific sequence above, update the activity title and equipment identifiers, remove unrelated fire-riser/EPO/SKRU content, attach or reference the vendor task-specific analysis, and complete a new approval and worker review before work begins.
