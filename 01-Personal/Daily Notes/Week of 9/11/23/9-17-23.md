Title: Rattlesnake Safety Talk for Warehouse Employees
 
Introduction:  
Good morning, everyone. Today, we're going to discuss an important topic that concerns our safety while working in and around the warehouse – rattlesnake safety. Warehouses, especially those located in areas with wildlife, may encounter rattlesnakes. Knowing the do's and don'ts when dealing with these venomous snakes is crucial to keep ourselves safe. So, let's get started with some valuable information.
 
I. Understanding Rattlesnakes:  
A. Identifying Rattlesnakes:  
1. Rattlesnakes have a distinct triangular-shaped head.  
2. They have a rattling tail, which they use as a warning sign.  
3. Rattlesnakes come in various colors, including shades of brown, gray, and green.
 
B. Rattlesnake Behavior:  
1. They are usually more active during warmer months, so be cautious in the spring and summer.  
2. Rattlesnakes are often found near rocks, shrubs, or tall grass, so be aware of these areas.  
3. They are generally not aggressive but may strike if they feel threatened.
 
II. The Do's:  
A. Stay Alert:  
1. Always be aware of your surroundings, especially in outdoor areas.  
2. Keep an eye out for snake tracks, shed skin, or the sound of rattling.
 
B. Maintain a Safe Distance:  
1. If you encounter a rattlesnake, maintain a distance of at least six feet.  
2. Do not attempt to capture or handle the snake. Leave it alone.
 
C. Wear Appropriate Clothing:  
1. When working in snake-prone areas, wear long pants, closed-toe shoes, and ankle-high boots.  
2. Consider snake gaiters or snake-proof boots for added protection.
 
D. Use a Light:  
1. When working in dimly lit areas, use a flashlight to help spot snakes.
 
III. The Don'ts:  
A. Do Not Approach or Provoke:  
1. Never try to approach or provoke a rattlesnake intentionally.  
2. Avoid attempting to kill the snake, as this can increase the risk of getting bitten.
 
B. Do Not Step Over Hidden Areas:  
1. Avoid stepping over rocks, logs, or tall grass without first inspecting the area for hidden snakes.  
2. Step onto these surfaces carefully, giving snakes a chance to sense your presence and move away.
 
C. Do Not Reach into Hidden Spaces:  
1. When reaching into tight or concealed spaces, use tools to minimize the risk of a hidden snake bite.  
2. Always inspect the area before reaching in.
 
D. Do Not Panic:  
1. If you encounter a rattlesnake, stay calm and move slowly away from it.  
2. Panic can lead to rash decisions that may put you at greater risk.
 
Conclusion:  
In conclusion, our safety in and around the warehouse is a top priority. Being aware of the do's and don'ts of rattlesnake safety is essential to reduce the risk of snake encounters and bites. Remember to stay alert, maintain a safe distance, wear appropriate clothing, and avoid provoking or approaching rattlesnakes. By following these guidelines, we can ensure a safer working environment for everyone. If you ever encounter a rattlesnake or suspect a snakebite, seek immediate medical attention. Thank you for your attention, and let's stay safe out there!
