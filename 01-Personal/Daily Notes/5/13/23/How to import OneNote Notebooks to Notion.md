Yes, it is possible to import OneNote notebooks to Notion. You can do this by exporting your OneNote notebook as an Evernote file and then importing that file into Notion. Here are the steps:
 
1. Open OneNote and select the notebook you want to export.  
2. Click on "File" in the top left corner of the screen.  
3. Click on "Export" in the drop-down menu.  
4. Select "Evernote" as the file type.  
5. Choose a location to save the exported file and click "Export."  
6. Open Notion and create a new page or database.  
7. Click on "Import" in the top right corner of the screen.  
8. Select "Evernote" as the import source.  
9. Choose the exported Evernote file and click "Import."
 
That's it! Your OneNote notebook should now be imported into Notion.
