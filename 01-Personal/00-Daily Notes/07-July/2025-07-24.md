### Electrical (Insulating) Gloves Summary of Requirements

| Requirement                     | Frequency / Notes                                                                |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dielectric lab test**         | Before issue and every 6 months                                                  |
| **Visual & air test**           | By user before each use                                                          |
| **Storage**                     | Clean, dry, and protected from damage                                            |
| **Marking & labeling**          | Class, date of test, manufacturer info                                           |
| **Use with leather protectors** | Required unless gloves are used in controlled conditions without mechanical risk |
