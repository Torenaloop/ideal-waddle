The regiment saw serious action. It fought at the Battle of McDowell in May 1862 and the Battle of Cross Keys on June 8, 1862. The regiment's total losses were significant: 5 officers and 56 enlisted men killed or mortally wounded, and 2 officers and 172 enlisted men lost to disease, for a total of 235.
