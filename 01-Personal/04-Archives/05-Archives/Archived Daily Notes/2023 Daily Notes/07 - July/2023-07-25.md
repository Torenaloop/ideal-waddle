- Synthesize FACTS and Concepts into UNIFIED presentation of information
- FACTS:
	- 22 deaths in first 6 months of 2022 compared to 15 in all of 2021 *(Alarming rise in trench....OSHA National New Release, 7/14/22, www.osha.gov) 
		- That's a 46% percent increase in fatalities in the first 6 months of 2022 alone compared to all of 2021.
	- A cubic yard of soil can weigh as much as 3,000 pounds, equal to that of a compact car *(Alarming rise in trench....OSHA National New Release, 7/14/22, www.osha.gov) 
	- Risks from trench-related activities can be mitigated or eliminated by adhering to OSHA standards.
	- OSHA implemented a compliance directive providing guidance and inspection procedures for the enforcement of Subpart P (Excavations) to all of their National, Regional, and Area Offices ([[29 CFR 1926, Subpart P]])
	- Increased inspection efforts can only do so much to protect employees. What can you do as an employer to mitigate the risks of excavation work to ensure the health and safety of your employees?
	- Value Proposition: Increase the safety of your employees and be prepared for an OSHA inspection
- SOLUTIONS: 
	- [eTool : Construction - Trenching and Excavation | Occupational Safety and Health Administration (osha.gov)](https://www.osha.gov/etools/construction/trenching)
		- 1. Ensure there's a safe way to enter and exit
		- 2. Ensure trenches that are 5 feet deep or greater have cave-in protection
			- Sloping/benching
			- Shoring
			- Shielding
		- 3. Look for standing water and test if atmospheric hazards are or may be present
		- 4. Keep materials away from the edge of the trench
		- Never enter a trench unless it has been properly inspected by a competent person *(cite OSHA definition of an Excavation Competent Person)
