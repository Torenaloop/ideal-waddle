# Three Laws of Motion

The laws of motion are three laws stated by [[Isaac Newton]], that describe the relationship between the motion of an object, and the forces acting on it.

## First Law - Law of Inertia
[[Law of Inertia]] - An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion with the same speed and direction unless acted upon by an external force.

## Second Law - F = ma
The force acting on an object is equal to its mass multiplied by its acceleration.
- Formula: F = ma

## Third Law - Action and Reaction
For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.
