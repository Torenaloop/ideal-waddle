Tom Buckman, a McLean, Virginia native, is launching his coaching career as a hitting and fielding instructor at Diamond Heroes Baseball in Merrifield. A former collegiate standout, he hit.289 at Washington College over 66 games before transferring to Division 1 Saint Peter's University, where he posted a.287 average with 25 RBIs in 50 games for the 2022 season.  
After college, Buckman played professionally with the Bakersfield Train Robbers in the Pecos League, batting.329 with a.490 OBP and.521 SLG. Now coaching, he brings his high-level playing experience to help develop young athletes both at the plate and in the field.
