Here's a **30-minute itinerary** for your son's hitting practice in the indoor batting cage that focuses on **Bat Speed**, **Contact Ability/Bat-to-Ball Skills**, and **Swing Decisions**. This plan balances efficiency and skill development while keeping it engaging for your 10-year-old.
 
**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**

- **Dynamic Stretching (2 minutes)**:
    - Arm circles, trunk twists, and wrist/shoulder mobility exercises.
    - Focus on loosening muscles to prepare for explosive movements.
- **Dry Swings (3 minutes)**:
    - Have your son take **10-15 dry swings** without a ball.
    - Emphasize proper mechanics, including grip, stance, and follow-through.
    - Focus: Reinforce muscle memory and warm up bat speed.
 
**Main Practice Drills (25 minutes)**  
**1. Bat Speed Development (8 minutes)**

- **Drill 1: Overload/Underload Swings (4 minutes)**:
    - Equipment: Use a heavier bat (overload) for 5 swings and a lighter bat (underload) for 5 swings, alternating for 3 sets.
    - Focus: Building strength (heavier bat) and quick-twitch muscles (lighter bat).
    - Key Tip: Ensure your son maintains proper swing mechanics with both bats.
- **Drill 2: Tee Work with Exit Velocity Focus (4 minutes)**:
    - Place the ball on the tee.
    - Use a radar or stopwatch if available to measure exit velocity or speed off the bat.
    - Encourage him to swing as fast as he can without sacrificing mechanics.
    - Take **10 swings**, focusing on explosive hip rotation and hand speed.
 
**2. Contact Ability/Bat-to-Ball Skills (8 minutes)**

- **Drill 3: Contact Points Drill (4 minutes)**:
    - Set the tee at different heights and locations (inside, middle, and outside).
    - Have him hit 3 balls from each position, focusing on proper contact points:
        - **Inside pitch**: Hit it out in front of the plate.
        - **Middle pitch**: Hit it directly above home plate.
        - **Outside pitch**: Let the ball travel and hit it slightly deeper in the zone.
    - Emphasize clean, solid contact on each swing.
- **Drill 4: Short Toss with Feedback (4 minutes)**:
    - Stand behind an L-screen and toss balls underhand to simulate live pitching.
    - Start with slower tosses and increase speed slightly as he gets comfortable.
    - Focus on:
        - Keeping his eyes on the ball.
        - Staying balanced and tracking the ball into the bat.
        - Consistently hitting line drives up the middle.
 
**3. Swing Decision Training (9 minutes)**

- **Drill 5: "Yes/No" Drill (4 minutes)**:
    - Soft toss underhand from behind an L-screen.
    - Mix in good pitches to hit and obvious balls out of the zone.
    - Your son must verbally call "Yes" (to swing) or "No" (to take) before each swing.
    - Focus: Developing pitch recognition and making better swing decisions.
- **Drill 6: Random Location Tosses (5 minutes)**:
    - Toss balls to various locations (inside, middle, outside, high, low).
    - He should only swing at pitches in the strike zone or the location you're working on.
    - Encourage discipline by rewarding correct decisions (e.g., hitting strikes, taking balls).
    - Focus: Reinforce understanding of the strike zone and patience.
 
**Cool Down (2 minutes)**

- **Reflect and Review**:
    - Discuss what felt good and what he learned during the session.
    - End with a couple of relaxed swings to cool down.
 
**Additional Tips**

1. **Encouragement and Fun**:
    - Keep the tone positive and celebrate small wins.
    - Rotate drills quickly to maintain engagement.
2. **Track Progress**:
    - Use a small notebook to note progress in bat speed, contact accuracy, and swing decisions.
3. **Gradual Adjustments**:
    - If any drill feels too advanced, adjust by simplifying the focus or pace.
