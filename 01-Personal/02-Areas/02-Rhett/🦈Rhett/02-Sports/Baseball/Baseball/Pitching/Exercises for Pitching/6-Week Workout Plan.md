Here’s a **6-week workout plan** tailored to a 10-year-old to strengthen their core, legs, and shoulders for pitching. The plan emphasizes gradual progression, safe exercises, and engagement to build strength and coordination.
 
**General Guidelines**

- **Frequency**: 3 days per week (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday) with rest or light activity days in between.
- **Duration**: ~20–30 minutes per session.
- **Warm-Up** (5 minutes each session):
    - Jumping jacks (30 seconds)
    - Arm circles (forward and backward, 30 seconds each)
    - High knees (30 seconds)
    - Dynamic stretches (e.g., leg swings, torso twists)
 
**Weekly Structure**  
Each week focuses on improving strength, coordination, and endurance. Start with lighter reps and progress slightly each week.
 
**Weeks 1–2: Building a Foundation**

- Focus on mastering form and developing body awareness.
- Reps and Sets: Start with 2 sets of each exercise.

**Day 1**:

- **Core**:
    - Plank: Hold for 15–20 seconds.
    - Dead Bug: 6–8 reps per side.
- **Legs**:
    - Bodyweight Squats: 10 reps.
    - Calf Raises: 12–15 reps.
- **Shoulders**:
    - Arm Circles: 30 seconds each direction.
    - Wall Angels: 6–8 reps.

**Day 2**:

- **Core**:
    - Bird Dog: 8 reps per side.
    - Russian Twists (no weight): 10 reps (5 per side).
- **Legs**:
    - Lunges: 6 reps per leg.
    - Step-Ups: 6 reps per leg.
- **Shoulders**:
    - Towel Shoulder Rotations: 6–8 reps.
    - Band Pull-Aparts: 6–8 reps (use a light band).

**Day 3**:

- **Dynamic Exercises**:
    - Ladder Drills: 3–5 minutes.
    - Medicine Ball Rotational Throws (light ball): 6 throws per side.
    - Skipping: 30 seconds.
 
**Weeks 3–4: Increasing Volume**

- Focus on adding reps or duration.
- Reps and Sets: 2–3 sets of each exercise, slightly increasing reps.

**Day 1**:

- **Core**:
    - Plank: Hold for 20–25 seconds.
    - Dead Bug: 8–10 reps per side.
- **Legs**:
    - Bodyweight Squats: 12 reps.
    - Calf Raises: 15–18 reps.
- **Shoulders**:
    - Arm Circles: 40 seconds each direction.
    - Wall Angels: 8–10 reps.

**Day 2**:

- **Core**:
    - Bird Dog: 10 reps per side.
    - Russian Twists (no weight): 12 reps (6 per side).
- **Legs**:
    - Lunges: 8 reps per leg.
    - Step-Ups: 8 reps per leg.
- **Shoulders**:
    - Towel Shoulder Rotations: 8–10 reps.
    - Band Pull-Aparts: 8–10 reps.

**Day 3**:

- **Dynamic Exercises**:
    - Ladder Drills: 4–6 minutes.
    - Medicine Ball Rotational Throws: 8 throws per side.
    - Skipping: 40 seconds.
 
**Weeks 5–6: Adding Complexity**

- Focus on endurance, slightly more challenging variations, or increased reps.
- Reps and Sets: 3 sets of each exercise.

**Day 1**:

- **Core**:
    - Plank: Hold for 30 seconds.
    - Dead Bug: 10–12 reps per side.
- **Legs**:
    - Bodyweight Squats: 15 reps.
    - Calf Raises: 18–20 reps.
- **Shoulders**:
    - Arm Circles: 50 seconds each direction.
    - Wall Angels: 10 reps.

**Day 2**:

- **Core**:
    - Bird Dog: 12 reps per side.
    - Russian Twists: 14 reps (7 per side).
- **Legs**:
    - Lunges: 10 reps per leg.
    - Step-Ups: 10 reps per leg.
- **Shoulders**:
    - Towel Shoulder Rotations: 10–12 reps.
    - Band Pull-Aparts: 10–12 reps.

**Day 3**:

- **Dynamic Exercises**:
    - Ladder Drills: 5–7 minutes.
    - Medicine Ball Rotational Throws: 10 throws per side.
    - Skipping: 50 seconds.
- **Bounding**: 6–8 exaggerated steps.
 
**Cool-Down**  
Each session should end with 3–5 minutes of stretching:

- **Hamstring stretch**
- **Quad stretch**
- **Shoulder stretch**
- **Cat-Cow stretch** (for the back)
 
**Progression**

- Monitor progress weekly and adjust based on comfort and ability.
- If exercises become too easy, add reps, hold positions longer (e.g., plank), or increase intensity slightly.
