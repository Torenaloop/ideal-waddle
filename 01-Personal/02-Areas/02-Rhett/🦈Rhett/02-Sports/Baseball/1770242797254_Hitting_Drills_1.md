# Hitting Drills - 30 Minute Practice Plan

## Overview
30-minute hitting practice itinerary focusing on:
- **Bat Speed**
- **Contact Ability/Bat-to-Ball Skills**
- **Swing Decisions**

Designed for 10-year-old player in indoor batting cage.

---

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)

### Dynamic Stretching (2 minutes)
- Arm circles
- Trunk twists
- Wrist/shoulder mobility exercises
- Focus: Loosening muscles for explosive movements

### Dry Swings (3 minutes)
- Take **10-15 dry swings** without a ball
- Emphasize proper mechanics:
  - Grip
  - Stance
  - Follow-through
- Focus: Reinforce muscle memory and warm up bat speed

---

## Main Practice Drills (25 minutes)

### 1. Bat Speed Development (8 minutes)

#### Drill 1: Overload/Underload Swings (4 minutes)
- **Equipment:** Heavier bat (overload) + lighter bat (underload)
- **Reps:** 5 swings each, alternating for 3 sets
- **Focus:** 
  - Building strength (heavier bat)
  - Quick-twitch muscles (lighter bat)
- **Key Tip:** Maintain proper swing mechanics with both bats

#### Drill 2: Tee Work with Exit Velocity Focus (4 minutes)
- Place ball on tee
- Use radar/stopwatch if available to measure exit velocity
- **Reps:** 10 swings
- **Focus:** 
  - Explosive hip rotation
  - Hand speed
  - Swing as fast as possible without sacrificing mechanics

---

### 2. Contact Ability/Bat-to-Ball Skills (8 minutes)

#### Drill 3: Contact Points Drill (4 minutes)
- Set tee at different heights and locations:
  - Inside
  - Middle
  - Outside
- **Reps:** 3 balls from each position
- **Focus on proper contact points:**
  - **Inside pitch:** Hit it out in front of the plate
  - **Middle pitch:** Hit it directly above home plate
  - **Outside pitch:** Let ball travel, hit slightly deeper in zone
- Emphasize clean, solid contact on each swing

#### Drill 4: Short Toss with Feedback (4 minutes)
- Stand behind L-screen, toss balls underhand
- Start with slower tosses, increase speed gradually
- **Focus:**
  - Keeping eyes on the ball
  - Staying balanced
  - Tracking ball into bat
  - Consistently hitting line drives up the middle

---

### 3. Swing Decision Training (9 minutes)

#### Drill 5: "Yes/No" Drill (4 minutes)
- Soft toss underhand from behind L-screen
- Mix in good pitches to hit and obvious balls out of zone
- **Must verbally call "Yes" (swing) or "No" (take) before each pitch**
- **Focus:** Developing pitch recognition and better swing decisions

#### Drill 6: Random Location Tosses (5 minutes)
- Toss balls to various locations:
  - Inside, middle, outside
  - High, low
- **Only swing at pitches in strike zone** or target location
- Encourage discipline by rewarding correct decisions
- **Focus:** 
  - Reinforce understanding of strike zone
  - Patience at the plate

---

## Cool Down (2 minutes)

### Reflect and Review
- Discuss what felt good
- Discuss what he learned during the session
- End with a couple of relaxed swings to cool down

---

## Additional Tips

### 1. Encouragement and Fun
- Keep tone positive
- Celebrate small wins
- Rotate drills quickly to maintain engagement

### 2. Track Progress
- Use small notebook to note progress in:
  - Bat speed
  - Contact accuracy
  - Swing decisions

### 3. Gradual Adjustments
- If any drill feels too advanced, simplify focus or pace
