


I found several useful emails that establish the Virginia/NVA safety fair was planned in 2024 and again in 2025, with stronger “took place” evidence for 2024 than 2025 from the correspondence surfaced so far.

## Strongest evidence found

    - Subject: **“FW: [EXTERNAL] RSVP for the First Annual STACK Virginia Safety Fair”**
        
    - Date: **Oct. 22, 2024**

2. **Location confirmation**

3. **Internal awareness/planning chain**

4. **Evidence it occurred**

    - Date: **Nov. 13, 2024**
