## Brief Life History

Charles Dabney Nuckolls was born in Grayson County, Virginia on March 2, 1805. He learned the various trades of a carpenter, cabinet-maker and wheelwright, and was considered a very fine mechanic. In the early settlement of Springfield he was engaged in the leather business as well as doing extensive work as a carpenter and builder. He became one of the leading businessmen in the area. He purchased a lot at the southeast corner of Sixth and Washington streets for twelve dollars. Several years later Charles sold it to Sangamon County for twelve hundred dollars. A court house was built on it, which was occupied as such from 1845 to 1876. Late in life Charles studied medicine and graduated in 1856 at McDowell College, St. Louis, Missouri. He practiced medicine for several years until his death on November 19, 1865. Source: EARLY SETTLERS OF SANGAMON COUNTY - 1876 by John Carroll Power
