**Your Management Leadership Rating: YELLOW**

***Recommendations for improvement:***

- Assign qualified site leadership to oversee the planning, supervision, and safety of work activities.
- Require pre- and post-job reviews for high-risk operations, and use these outcomes and lessons learned to ensure that improvements are made across projects.
- Confirm that that consistent safety metrics are defined and tracked consistently across all sites to measure proactive efforts (e.g., training participation, near-miss reporting) and reactive outcomes (e.g., recordable injuries, lost-time incidents).
- Ensure that each project budget includes dedicated allocation for safety resources (e.g., training, personal protective equipment, safety equipment)
- Confirm that contractor and subcontractor safety responsibilities are defined in contracts and verified through audits and performance reviews.
