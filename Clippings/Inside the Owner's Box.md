## About The Book

**Go inside the high-stakes world of sports ownership as *New York Times* bestselling author and owner of the Baltimore Orioles David M. Rubenstein reveals the financial strategies, leadership secrets, and billion-dollar deal-making behind championship franchises through conversations with America's most powerful owners—Robert Kraft, Jeff Lurie, Tom Ricketts, and more.**  
  
**What does it really take to own a championship franchise?**  
  
David M. Rubenstein, bestselling author and Baltimore Orioles owner, reveals the insider secrets of professional sports ownership through candid conversations with America's most powerful team owners.  
  
**Featuring moguls like:**  
  
Robert Kraft (Patriots) · Jeff Lurie (Eagles) · Tom Ricketts (Cubs) · Ted Leonsis (Wizards, Capitals) · Clara Wu Tsai (Nets) · Tom Ricketts (Cubs) and more.

· How owners negotiate billion-dollar franchise acquisitions  
  
· The financial playbook behind elite ownership—valuations, salary caps, private equity, and profit engines  
  
· Mastering media rights, sports betting, and the evolving fan economy  
  
· Leadership lessons: Building championship cultures that win on and off the field
