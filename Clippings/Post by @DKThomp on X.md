I'm going to become the joker with all of these conservatives discrediting liberal arguments about AI by spookily whispering that money might be involved.

The birth of the Tech Right involved the realization, in the 2020s, that conservative people in tech should use their money to do stuff in politics. The All In podcast hosts threw fundraisers for the president!

I don't know what the principle is at stake here other than "when conservative money seems like it'll get its way, we're a movement; but when liberal money seems like it'll get its way, it's a conspiracy." In 2024, when (per the Washington Post) about 80% of federal campaign spending by the 100 wealthiest Americans went to Republicans, you didn't hear a lot from the Tech Right back then about the danger of money in politics. But now that Trump is polling below the Mendoza Line and several upcoming IPOs will unlock billions of dollars of cash among Chamath's political enemies, these guys are suddenly Woodward and Bernstein, exposing the evils of rich ppl using their money to do stuff in politics.
