More than half of my exec team has already built personal chief-of-staff [AI](https://www.axios.com/technology/automation-and-ai) agents — connected to email, to-do lists, goals and meetings. They didn't wait for an IT rollout. They just built them.

**Why it matters:** The first-mover advantage in this [age of asymmetry](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/05/small-teams-ai-drones-geopolitics-business) is structural, not incremental. Leaders who understand agents as [a management layer](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/05/ai-agents-run-lean-business) — not a productivity tool — will architect organizations the rest of us can't compete with on headcount alone.

- The window to experiment cheaply, before this becomes table stakes, is open now. It won't stay open long.

**The big thing to understand:** An agent isn't a [chatbot](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/24/increase-your-ai-fluency). It's AI that just works. Give it a goal, not a task list. It reasons, acts, checks results, adjusts, repeats — autonomously.

- A real-world use case: At 7 a.m., before your day kicks off in earnest, it has already read your email, flagged what needs your attention and dropped it at the top of your inbox.

**What to do** on a wider scale:

- Set your company's AI governance rules now, especially for agentic use, before a security incident makes you reactive. As [I've told you before](https://www.axios.com/2026/03/29/claude-mythos-anthropic-cyberattack-ai-agents), Axios' tech team has warned me that unsupervised agentic use that touches sensitive information is the single biggest threat to our company right now.
- Start using agents ASAP. You'll soon see more clearly the work and the workforce of the future.

- Hire one or two AI-native people to tutor, inform, and build alongside you. Axios recently hired our first [AI enablement lead](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/15/axios-ai-moonshot), and we're looking for [a summer fellow](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/axios/jobs/7624537) who lives and breathes the technology with and for me.
- Sketch the [systems architect](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/27/ceo-future-ai-transition-trust-talent) role. You'll need one next to you and probably one embedded in each division.
- Align on a common AI language — mission, voice, goals, security — so agents aren't running on different playbooks.
