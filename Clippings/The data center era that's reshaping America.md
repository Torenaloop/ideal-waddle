Nothing is driving more new U.S. economic investment or political volatility than [data centers](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/19/ai-power-data-center-electricity-construction).

**Why it matters:** They're the Great Subplot of 2026, THE topic animating the [AI](https://www.axios.com/technology/automation-and-ai) race and [elections](https://www.axios.com/politics-policy/2026-midterm-elections). Everyone needs to understand the size, scope and sentiment surrounding them.

**The big picture:** It's almost impossible to wrap your brain around the scale of the cash dedicated to this buildout — now the biggest capital project in human history.

- The five biggest hyperscalers — Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Meta and Oracle — are set to spend [more than $750B](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9rbm93LmNyZWRpdHNpZ2h0cy5jb20vaW5zaWdodHMvdGVjaC1yYWlzaW5nLWh5cGVyc2NhbGVyLWNhcGV4LTIwMjYtZXN0aW1hdGVzLz91dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cB4a845f59) on capital expenditures this year. That's up 67% from last year, and roughly 75% of it is earmarked for AI infrastructure.

**The three things** about data centers that you won't be able to hide from:

1. **They're coming for everything.** Hyperscalers aren't bottlenecked by GPUs alone. Big Tech is hungry for everything physical in order to get these things built. The richest companies on earth are now your main competition for things like land, cooling systems and turbines.
2. **They're straining the grid.** Utilities [now forecast](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9ncmlkc3RyYXRlZ2llc2xsYy5jb20vd3AtY29udGVudC91cGxvYWRzL0dyaWQtU3RyYXRlZ2llcy1OYXRpb25hbC1Mb2FkLUdyb3d0aC1SZXBvcnQtMjAyNS5wZGY_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1uZXdzbGV0dGVyJnV0bV9tZWRpdW09ZW1haWwmdXRtX2NhbXBhaWduPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXJfY3N1aXRlX2JldGEmc3RyZWFtPXRvcA/586d9e571e560373298b467cB43a9770f) a sixfold jump for 2030's peak electric demand growth from predictions just three years ago. That's driven by data centers, and our aging systems can't keep up.
3. **They're a defining political issue.** Both Republicans and Democrats want to capitalize on the populist line of limiting or banning their construction. It's happening at all levels — [the federal government](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9vY2FzaW8tY29ydGV6LmhvdXNlLmdvdi9tZWRpYS9wcmVzcy1yZWxlYXNlcy9vY2FzaW8tY29ydGV6LWludHJvZHVjZXMtaG91c2UtdmVyc2lvbi1haS1kYXRhLWNlbnRlci1tb3JhdG9yaXVtLWFjdD91dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cBace2286c), [at the state level](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cucmV1dGVycy5jb20vd29ybGQvbmV3LXlvcmstYmVjb21lcy1maXJzdC1zdGF0ZS1pbXBvc2UtZGF0YS1jZW50ZXItbW9yYXRvcml1bS0yMDI2LTA3LTE0Lz91dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cB864e9fc1) and [in local communities](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cud3B0di5jb20vbmV3cy9yZWdpb24tYy1wYWxtLWJlYWNoLWNvdW50eS9sb3hhaGF0Y2hlZS1hY3JlYWdlL3BhbG0tYmVhY2gtY291bnR5LWNvbW1pc3Npb25lcnMtcmVqZWN0LXByb2plY3QtdGFuZ28tYnV0LWRhdGEtY2VudGVyLWRldmVsb3BtZW50LXN0aWxsLWFwcHJvdmVkLWZvci1zaXRlP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cB6c430290). Top Republicans tell me data centers alone could cost them control of the Senate. Josh Shapiro (D), who had [boasted](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cucGEuZ292L2dvdmVybm9yL25ld3Nyb29tLzIwMjUtcHJlc3MtcmVsZWFzZXMvZ292LWFubm91bmNlcy1hbWF6b24tdG8taW52ZXN0LS0yMGItaW4tcGEtLWxhcmdlc3QtY2FwaXRhbC1pbnZlcz91dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cB24b013d6) last year about landing a huge data center, imposed [strict new requirements](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cucGEuZ292L2dvdmVybm9yL25ld3Nyb29tLzIwMjYtcHJlc3MtcmVsZWFzZXMvZ292ZXJub3Itc2hhcGlyby1zaWducy1leGVjdXRpdmUtb3JkZXItb24tZGF0YS1jZW50ZXItZGV2ZWxvcG1lbj91dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cB2f9499fb) on new ones. Earlier this month, Texas Gov. Greg Abbott (R) [froze](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGV4YXN0cmlidW5lLm9yZy8yMDI2LzA4LzAzL3RleGFzLWRhdGEtY2VudGVyLXByb2plY3QtYXVkaXQtZ3JlZy1hYmJvdHQvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cBf3e4f90d) new data centers.

**The bottom line:** Data centers are proxy fights over AI, jobs, the environment, energy production and energy use.

## Your cheat sheet

🤔 **What they actually do:** They're giant buildings filled with chips used to train AI models or process the queries sent to them. Their size is generally measured in megawatts, the units of electrical capacity needed to run them and cool the infrastructure inside.

**📈 Total online:** About [4,000 and counting](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubWFwcy5jb20vZGF0YS1jZW50ZXItbWFwLXNob3dzLWRhdGEtY2VudGVycy1hbmQtZW5lcmd5LWluZnJhc3RydWN0dXJlLz91dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cBe4180914) across the U.S., with [580 hyperscale facilities](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuc3JncmVzZWFyY2guY29tL2FydGljbGVzL2ZvY3VzLW9mLXVzLWh5cGVyc2NhbGUtaW52ZXN0bWVudC1zaGlmdHMtZHJhbWF0aWNhbGx5LWlubGFuZD91dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cBb507673b).

**🗳️ Total delayed by politics:** At least [75 projects nationwide](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZGF0YWNlbnRlcndhdGNoLm9yZy9xMS0yMDI2P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cB844d6460) totaling roughly $130B in potential investment were impacted in Q1 2026.

**💡 Average site capacity:** [62 MW](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuc3JncmVzZWFyY2guY29tL2FydGljbGVzL3N5bmVyZ3ktcmVwb3J0cy10aGF0LXVzLWRhdGEtY2VudGVyLWNhcGFjaXR5LXdpbGwtY29udGludWUtdG8tYmFsbG9vbi1kZXNwaXRlLWluY3JlYXNpbmctaGVhZHdpbmRzP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cB893bb8b3) for large projects currently in the pipeline. (For comparison, the typical site in Virginia at the end of 2024 averaged roughly [34 MW](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9qbGFyYy52aXJnaW5pYS5nb3YvcGRmcy9yZXBvcnRzL1JwdDU5OC5wZGY_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1uZXdzbGV0dGVyJnV0bV9tZWRpdW09ZW1haWwmdXRtX2NhbXBhaWduPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXJfY3N1aXRlX2JldGEmc3RyZWFtPXRvcA/586d9e571e560373298b467cBededb98b).)

**💰 Average cost to build:** Roughly $2.1B-2.4B all in for that 62 MW project.

**🧑💻 Average employees:** [About 50](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9qbGFyYy52aXJnaW5pYS5nb3YvcGRmcy9yZXBvcnRzL1JwdDU5OC5wZGY_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1uZXdzbGV0dGVyJnV0bV9tZWRpdW09ZW1haWwmdXRtX2NhbXBhaWduPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXJfY3N1aXRlX2JldGEmc3RyZWFtPXRvcA/586d9e571e560373298b467cCededb98b) to keep the average Virginia site running. That number balloons to a peak of 1,500 workers on site during the 12-18 months of construction

## Competition, for everything

![Illustration of construction equipment lined up over an abstract vector background ](https://images.axios.com/IBaZieZNtGnO8hU2W30BSq_XTFw=/0x0:1920x1080/1920x1080/2026/08/12/1786567554803.jpeg?w=1920)

Illustration: Sarah Grillo/Axios. Stock: Getty Images

The richest companies on earth are the highest bidders for the physical components of pretty much anything needed to build.

**Why it matters:** Data centers need a hell of a lot of infrastructure to exist and operate. The hyperscalers can buy it all, with unlimited resources and endless timelines.

**The big picture:** Data centers are singlehandedly propping up private construction across America, adding more than $21B in new spending [over the past year](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuY2Vuc3VzLmdvdi9jb25zdHJ1Y3Rpb24vYzMwL3BkZi9wcml2c2EucGRmP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cB852111ea) even as the rest of the industry shrank by over $100B.

- John Deere shares [spiked](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYXhpb3MuY29tL25ld3NsZXR0ZXJzL2F4aW9zLWNsb3Nlci1jYzg2MjFhMy0zMDQ1LTRhMGQtOTU0Yy0zNjVjZDJkMmM5MDYuaHRtbD91dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cBdc4cfef3) this week after sales in its construction and forestry segment jumped 18% year over year, which the company linked directly to the data center buildout.

**Every input** you can imagine is being scrambled:

- ⚡️ **Electrical gear:** The North American transformer supply chain chief of Hitachi Energy, the world's largest transformer maker, [says](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZm9ydHVuZS5jb20vMjAyNi8wMy8wNi9haS1ncmlkLWRlZmluaW5nLWJvdHRsZW5lY2stdHJhbnNmb3JtZXJzLWhpdGFjaGktZW5lcmd5LWxlc3NvbnMvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cBaecffee4) that 44% of data center leaders report utility wait times beyond four years.
- 🪧 **Land:** Amazon [snagged 188 acres](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuY29zdGFyLmNvbS9hcnRpY2xlLzQxOTIyNzE1Ny9hbWF6b24tYnV5cy03MDAtbWlsbGlvbi1zaXRlLWluLXZpcmdpbmlhLWFzLWRhdGEtY2VudGVyLWRlbWFuZC1zdXJnZXM_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1uZXdzbGV0dGVyJnV0bV9tZWRpdW09ZW1haWwmdXRtX2NhbXBhaWduPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXJfY3N1aXRlX2JldGEmc3RyZWFtPXRvcA/586d9e571e560373298b467cB5ac3369b) in Northern Virginia last year for $700M — $3.7M an acre — for one of its data center projects. A homebuilder had originally assembled the parcel for $51M, planning roughly 500 homes on the site.
- 👷 **Labor:** The construction industry needs almost [350K more workers](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZW5yLmNvbS9hcnRpY2xlcy82MjM0OC1jb25zdHJ1Y3Rpb25zLWxhYm9yLXJlbGllZi1tYXNrcy1zdHJ1Y3R1cmFsLXJpc2stYXMtZGVtYW5kLWNvb2xzP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cBb30b35cd) to meet demand this year. Analysts say there [aren't enough specialized workers](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubnl0aW1lcy5jb20vMjAyNi8wNy8yOS9idXNpbmVzcy9lY29ub215L2RhdGEtY2VudGVyLWVsZWN0cmljaWFucy10cmFpbmluZy5odG1sP3JzcmM9Zmx0JnVubG9ja2VkX2FydGljbGVfY29kZT0xLjFWQS5TWDVELklCOVFuc0h0c2Y1ZCZzbWlkPXVybC1zaGFyZSZ1dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cBa21eb2e4) to complete cooling and electricity jobs.

**The bottom line:** Anything being built could come into competition with the best-capitalized buyers in the history of humanity.

## Water use, overhyped

![Illustration of water pipes against an abstract vector background](https://images.axios.com/wV9xAn98S4DIjId088Njf_9sHGU=/0x0:1920x1080/1920x1080/2026/08/12/1786567730083.jpeg?w=1920)

Illustration: Sarah Grillo/Axios. Stock: Getty Images

It's basically true that [a hyperscale data center](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9zdXN0YWluYWJpbGl0eS5hdG1ldGEuY29tL3dwLWNvbnRlbnQvdXBsb2Fkcy8yMDI1LzEwL01ldGFfMjAyNS1FbnZpcm9ubWVudGFsLURhdGEtSW5kZXgucGRmP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cB3476b9d1) directly soaks up roughly as much water as one of the most famous [golf courses](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubmN3YXRlci5vcmcvV1VEQy9hcHAvV1dBVFIvcmVwb3J0L3ZpZXcvMDE1MC0wMDAyLzIwMjU_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1uZXdzbGV0dGVyJnV0bV9tZWRpdW09ZW1haWwmdXRtX2NhbXBhaWduPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXJfY3N1aXRlX2JldGEmc3RyZWFtPXRvcA/586d9e571e560373298b467cBcd3175cf) in America.

**🚜 Between the lines:** Even when you take into account that data centers indirectly use [roughly 12x](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9lc2Nob2xhcnNoaXAub3JnL3VjL2l0ZW0vMzJkNm0wZDE_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1uZXdzbGV0dGVyJnV0bV9tZWRpdW09ZW1haWwmdXRtX2NhbXBhaWduPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXJfY3N1aXRlX2JldGEmc3RyZWFtPXRvcA/586d9e571e560373298b467cB9b509ee2) more water to generate the power to run them, the numbers don't change on a macro level.

- The water use of every data center in America in 2023 equaled less than 1% of the water used to irrigate the nation's agriculture that year.

**⚠️ Smart way to look at this:** Though their water use is a rounding error nationally, individual data centers can still have massively outsized impacts on local watersheds. That's where the backlash will live.

## Electricity use, underhyped

![Illustration of a power line against an abstract vector background](https://images.axios.com/N11LcE9f6Ma7jFlllffR5ZGX_d4=/0x0:1920x1080/1920x1080/2026/08/12/1786567818485.jpeg?w=1920)

Illustration: Sarah Grillo/Axios. Stock: Getty Images

On the flip side, data centers' electricity use is likely more massive than you imagine — and demand is surging.

- Data centers used 4.4% of U.S. electricity in 2023. It's [forecast](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9ldGEtcHVibGljYXRpb25zLmxibC5nb3YvcHVibGljYXRpb25zL3VuaXRlZC1zdGF0ZXMtZGF0YS1jZW50ZXItZW5lcmd5LTIwMjU_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1uZXdzbGV0dGVyJnV0bV9tZWRpdW09ZW1haWwmdXRtX2NhbXBhaWduPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXJfY3N1aXRlX2JldGEmc3RyZWFtPXRvcA/586d9e571e560373298b467cB6f9ca2d4) to be almost 12% by 2030.

🏭 **Smart way to look at this:** Energy is rapidly shifting from an operating cost to an open competition and strategic constraint.

- Our grid is running out of room faster than it can build more. Utilities simply can't add the power plants, transmission lines and equipment needed fast enough.
- It's getting so fraught that PJM, America's largest grid operator, [proposed](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9pbnNpZGVsaW5lcy5wam0uY29tL3BqbS1wcm9wb3Nlcy1mcmFtZXdvcmstdG8tY29ubmVjdC1kYXRhLWNlbnRlcnMtd2l0aG91dC1jb21wcm9taXNpbmctcmVsaWFiaWxpdHktYWZmb3JkYWJpbGl0eS8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1uZXdzbGV0dGVyJnV0bV9tZWRpdW09ZW1haWwmdXRtX2NhbXBhaWduPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXJfY3N1aXRlX2JldGEmc3RyZWFtPXRvcA/586d9e571e560373298b467cBbe94c8fd) that data centers without their own power plants get cut from the grid first in times of high stress.
- OpenAI's job board tells you where this is headed. They want a [power trading lead](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9vcGVuYWkuY29tL2NhcmVlcnMvcG93ZXItdHJhZGluZy1sZWFkLXVzLXJlbW90ZS8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1uZXdzbGV0dGVyJnV0bV9tZWRpdW09ZW1haWwmdXRtX2NhbXBhaWduPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXJfY3N1aXRlX2JldGEmc3RyZWFtPXRvcA/586d9e571e560373298b467cBa68b8db2) for their data center portfolio.

💸 **That capacity shortage** is exactly what's driving up electricity costs for everyone. PJM saw [its capacity prices](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9pbnNpZGVsaW5lcy5wam0uY29tL3BqbS1jYXBhY2l0eS1hdWN0aW9uLXByb2N1cmVzLTEzODMxOC1tdy1vZi1nZW5lcmF0aW9uLXJlc291cmNlcy1hcy13b3JrLWNvbnRpbnVlcy10by1hZGRyZXNzLWdyb3dpbmctZWxlY3RyaWNpdHktZGVtYW5kLz91dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cB97458449) spike by 11x in just a few years — and utilities pass that on to monthly bills.

## Meta's massive play

Meta is committing [more than $50B](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9kYXRhY2VudGVycy5hdG1ldGEuY29tLzIwMjYvMDcvZGVlcGVuaW5nLW91ci1pbnZlc3RtZW50LWluLXJpY2hsYW5kLXBhcmlzaC1sb3Vpc2lhbmEvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cB589cd215) to build Hyperion, one of the largest data centers in America, in rural northeastern Louisiana.

**⚡️ The size:** Its capacity upon completion sometime around 2032 will be 5 GW (roughly 80x the average project mentioned above), [powered](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubHBzYy5sb3Vpc2lhbmEuZ292L2RvY3MvdHJhbnNjcmlwdHMvQXVndXN0LTIwLTIwMjUtQkUucGRmP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cBcfcf8f1d) [by](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZW50ZXJneS5jb20vbmV3cy9lbnRlcmd5LWxvdWlzaWFuYS1hbm5vdW5jZXMtYS1uZXctYWdyZWVtZW50LXdpdGgtbWV0YS10aGF0LXdpbGwtZGVsaXZlci1hbi1hZGRpdGlvbmFsLTJiLWluLWN1c3RvbWVyLXNhdmluZ3M_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1uZXdzbGV0dGVyJnV0bV9tZWRpdW09ZW1haWwmdXRtX2NhbXBhaWduPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXJfY3N1aXRlX2JldGEmc3RyZWFtPXRvcA/586d9e571e560373298b467cB9e6fc89b) 10 new natural gas plants paid for by Meta. The construction site [is massive](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9zaGVyd29vZC5uZXdzL3RlY2gvc2VlLWZvci15b3Vyc2VsZi1qdXN0LWhvdy1tYXNzaXZlLW1ldGFzLWh5cGVyaW9uLWRhdGEtY2VudGVyLWlzLz91dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cB9dbed810) — five miles long and a mile wide.

## A new kind of politics

A chart shows U.S. opinions on local AI data center construction, based on a Gallup survey. Overall, 7% strongly favor, 20% somewhat favor, 23% somewhat oppose, and 48% strongly oppose the construction. Among Democrats, 23% favor, while 75% oppose; Independents show 24% favor and 74% oppose, and Republicans have 34% favoring and 63% opposing.

Reproduced from [Gallup](https://news.gallup.com/poll/709772/americans-oppose-data-centers-area.aspx). Chart: Axios Visuals

The politics of data centers only runs one way right now: against them, almost astonishingly so in our divided America.

**How people feel:** More than 70% of Americans oppose the construction of a data center in their area, [according to Gallup](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9uZXdzLmdhbGx1cC5jb20vcG9sbC83MDk3NzIvYW1lcmljYW5zLW9wcG9zZS1kYXRhLWNlbnRlcnMtYXJlYS5hc3B4P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cC3d7b044a) — more than a nuclear power plant.

- In Virginia, America's data center heartland, support [has halved](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cud2FzaGluZ3RvbnBvc3QuY29tL2J1c2luZXNzLzIwMjYvMDQvMTUvZGF0YS1jZW50ZXJzLXBvbGwtdmlyZ2luaWEvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cB0e82acd6) in just three years.

**The big picture:** You can be sure this will spill over into the midterms. A majority of competitive House districts this fall [have data centers under construction](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYnVzaW5lc3NpbnNpZGVyLmNvbS90aGUtZGF0YS1jZW50ZXItYm9vbS1pcy1jb2xsaWRpbmctd2l0aC10aGUtbWlkdGVybXMtMjAyNi02P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cBdcd9d460) or in the planning stages.

- All of the congressional and gubernatorial ads tracked by AdImpact that mention data centers are against them.

**Between the lines:** This brutal bipartisan backlash means the era of states and localities paying AI and other tech companies to show up and break ground might be ending.

- For two decades, the calculus has been pretty simple: arrive with capital and jobs and get tax breaks and quick permits.
- Now people [are pissed off](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly9qYXNtaS5uZXdzL3Avbm8tZGF0YS1jZW50ZXJzLWluLW15LWJhY2t5YXJkP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cB7d38e8c7) about these monstrous buildings and worried their local government is getting fleeced by a Big Tech company that doesn't care about them.

**The other side:** Most [positive](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuc3Bva2VzbWFuLmNvbS9zdG9yaWVzLzIwMjYvanVsLzI2L3doZXJlLWFncmljdWx0dXJlLW1lZXRzLXRlY2hub2xvZ3ktaW5zaWRlLXRoZS1zbWFsP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9bmV3c2xldHRlciZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1uZXdzbGV0dGVyX2NzdWl0ZV9iZXRhJnN0cmVhbT10b3A/586d9e571e560373298b467cB60d8b680) [stories](https://link.axios.com/click/47156589.6232/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuYmVuZHNvdXJjZS5jb20vbmV3cy9sb2NhbG5ld3MvcHJpbmV2aWxsZS13YW50cy1tb3JlLW91dC1vZi1pdHMtbmV4dC1kYXRhLWNlbnRlci1kZWFsLz91dG1fc291cmNlPW5ld3NsZXR0ZXImdXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bmV3c2xldHRlcl9jc3VpdGVfYmV0YSZzdHJlYW09dG9w/586d9e571e560373298b467cBecac06d2) you'll find from communities with data centers got them well before the AI boom, and there's been enough time for civic benefits from tax revenue to become visible.
