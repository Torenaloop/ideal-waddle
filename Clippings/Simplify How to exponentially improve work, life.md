Nothing has improved my work and life more than my new operating philosophy: *[Simplify](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://www.smartbrevity.com/simplify*about__;Iw!!Al82Z4c!wzXvnpVtZL1vvx45-uPwR7nWvtwazlfsYohPnhJbkVcr66687DaBWiBc7K5o8h1pmTSasQ$).*

- I've seen such a dramatic improvement at Axios, and in my personal life, that I co-wrote a new book explaining step by step how it works. It's called *" [Simplify](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://www.smartbrevity.com/simplify*about__;Iw!!Al82Z4c!wzXvnpVtZL1vvx45-uPwR7nWvtwazlfsYohPnhJbkVcr66687DaBWiBc7K5o8h1pmTSasQ$): Do 50% More with 50% Less."*

**Why it matters:** Axios co-founders Jim VandeHei, Roy Schwartz and I dig deep into real-world experience and science to show you why most people waste years of their lives doing needless stuff out of habit or inertia.

- You'll learn the three-step *Simplify* operating system we've personally stress-tested: *Confront* what you do, *Delete* what you shouldn't do, and *Amplify* what brings you joy and achievement.

**💡 *Simplify* is a method and a mindset** — a set of power tools to help you kill complexity in your life and work so you can be happier and more productive.

- ***[Smart Brevity](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://www.axioshq.com/smart-brevity-book__;!!Al82Z4c!wzXvnpVtZL1vvx45-uPwR7nWvtwazlfsYohPnhJbkVcr66687DaBWiBc7K5o8h3magQDGQ$),*** our first book, was about simplifying how you communicate. *Simplify* extends that superpower to how you lead, manage, work and live.

**Why this?** *Simplify* is a modern survival guide written for this wild, high-velocity AI moment.

- We'll show you how to "say no"... to delete... to skip.

**Why now?** AI turns out to be the greatest complexity-generation machine ever invented. It mass-produces drafts, dashboards and action items, adding to the noise and distraction that engulf us.
