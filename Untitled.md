**Why portfolios change the game:** Right now, each site's audit lives as its own project. Without portfolios, leadership would need to click into 51+ individual projects to understand the state of the program. With an Asana portfolio, all site audit projects roll up into a single view where you can see at a glance which sites are on track, at risk, or off track. Portfolio dashboards automatically aggregate data across projects — so a chart showing "incomplete High-priority tasks" or "Compliance vs. Site completion rate" isn't just for one facility, it's across the entire program. Instead of manually collecting and summarizing data across projects, the dashboard feature automatically creates custom charts and visualizes how initiatives are performing.

Specific portfolio capabilities that matter for your use case:

- **Portfolio dashboards** pull custom field data (like your 6 fields) across all contained projects, so you get program-wide views of priority distribution, verification method breakdowns, and regulatory coverage without exporting anything to Excel.
- **Portfolio status updates** let you synthesize cross-project progress into a single shareable update — you can build the update with a few clicks and share it with every member of your portfolio. This replaces the manual reporting cadence.
- **Nested portfolios** allow hierarchical organization — for example, a top-level "EHS Program" portfolio containing regional sub-portfolios (Southeast, Mid-Atlantic, etc.), each containing site projects. You can create portfolios within portfolios to manage complex work structures.
- **Portfolio timeline** shows every site project's schedule in one pane, so you can see where audits overlap, stagger site visits, and spot scheduling conflicts across regions.
