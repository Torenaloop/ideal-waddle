This map focuses only on [The Nuckolls Report](https://homepages.rootsweb.com/~ahopkins/nuckdocs/the.htm). Circle sizes show relative concentration in the report—not exact record counts—and lines summarize the principal migrations it describes. Select a circle for details.

The dominant pattern is colonial and central Virginia, followed by movement into southwestern Virginia, the Carolinas, Tennessee, Illinois, and eventually the Plains and West.
