## EHS audit questions for data center operations:
 
Here are 10 audit questions related to environmental, health, and safety that can be added to a more comprehensive checklist for data center operations:
 
- [Are the data center’s environmental, health, and safety (EH&S) policies and procedures documented and communicated to all employees and contractors?](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx)1 - [Does the data center have a designated EH&S manager or coordinator who is responsible for overseeing the implementation and monitoring of the EH&S program?](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx)1 - [Does the data center conduct regular risk assessments to identify and mitigate potential hazards such as fire, electrical, chemical, biological, ergonomic, and physical risks?](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx)[2](https://pcidssguide.com/data-center-audit-checklist/)[1](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx) - [Does the data center have adequate fire detection and suppression systems, emergency lighting, exit signs, alarms, and sprinklers in place?](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx)[2](https://pcidssguide.com/data-center-audit-checklist/)[1](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx) - [Does the data center have proper ventilation, temperature, and humidity control systems to ensure optimal performance and prevent overheating, condensation, and corrosion of equipment?](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx)[2](https://pcidssguide.com/data-center-audit-checklist/)[1](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx) - [Does the data center have appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) such as gloves, goggles, masks, ear plugs, and safety shoes available for employees and contractors who work in hazardous areas or handle hazardous materials?](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx)[3](https://hsseworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/22-Safety-checklist.pdf)[1](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx) - [Does the data center have a comprehensive training program that covers topics such as EH&S policies and procedures, emergency response, incident reporting, hazard identification and control, safe work practices, and PPE use?](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx)[4](https://safetyculture.com/checklists/iso-45001/)[3](https://hsseworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/22-Safety-checklist.pdf)[1](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx) - [Does the data center have a system for recording, investigating, and analyzing EH&S incidents, accidents, near misses, and complaints?](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx)[4](https://safetyculture.com/checklists/iso-45001/)[3](https://hsseworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/22-Safety-checklist.pdf)[1](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx) - [Does the data center have a process for conducting internal and external audits to evaluate the effectiveness and compliance of the EH&S program?](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx)[5](https://www.safeopedia.com/the-proper-way-to-conduct-a-safety-audit/2/4787)[4](https://safetyculture.com/checklists/iso-45001/)[3](https://hsseworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/22-Safety-checklist.pdf) - [Does the data center have a process for reviewing and updating the EH&S program based on audit findings, feedback, changes in regulations, best practices, and lessons learned?](https://ohsonline.com/Articles/2016/04/01/Data-Center-EHS-101.aspx)[5](https://www.safeopedia.com/the-proper-way-to-conduct-a-safety-audit/2/4787)[4](https://safetyculture.com/checklists/iso-45001/)[3](https://hsseworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/22-Safety-checklist.pdf)
