Qualified Persons Oral Board Questionnaire   
 

5. What are the different electrical activities a qualified persons and affected worker can perform. ✅

Mainly looking for LOTO. Qualified persons can perform LOTO activities while affected workers cant.    
What are the three elements required to complete a Job Hazard Analysis prior to working with hazards. ✅  
Task, hazards, controls 

7. What are the five hierarchy of controls when completing a risk assessment. ✅

Elimination, substitution, engineering, administrative, PPE 

9. When is retraining required for qualified persons? ✅

Every 3 years, if the qualified person has deviated from procedures indicated noncompliance, new equipment (new hazards, policy changes, NFPA70E changes 

11. What is an electrically safe work condition (ESWC)? ✅

The equipment has be isolated, deenergized, LOTO’d, LDL’s, tested, boundaries established 

13. What are the two exceptions for an electrically safe work condition? 

Infeasible (equipment designed in such a way that it cannot be deenergized), supports life safety systems 

15. What is Simple (individual) Lockout Tagout. ✅

One person, one lock, one source of isolation 

17. What is Complex (group) Lockout Tagout. 
18. How do you determine an arc flash boundary. 

Arc flash assessment, sticker on equipment 

20. How do you determine Incident Energy at the working distance. 

Arc flash assessment, sticker on equipment 

22. How do you determine proper PPE based on incident energy.  
23. What are the four PPE categories and their Cal/cm2 rating. 

1 is 4cal, 2 is 8cal, 3 is 25cal, 4 is 40cal 

25. What are the two conditions to be exempt from completing Lockout Tagout? 
26. What is an energy isolating device. 

Device that removes energy from equipment such as a circuit breaker or valve (I like to ask if EPO is, which it is not since it is typically wired into controls and simply removes start sequence but not energy – also indicated in NFPA70E) 

28. What information needs to be on a Lockout Tag? 
29. What are the steps in a typical energy control procedure. 

Risk assessment to identify hazards, notifications sent out for hazard awareness, preparations made (PPE and tools), boundaries established, equipment shutdown, equipment isolated, equipment LOTOd, equipment LDL’s, equipment attempt to start 

31. How do you perform a verification of hazardous energy isolation. 
32. What are the three alerting techniques that can be used keep unqualified persons out of approach boundaries? 

Signs, barricades, attendants 

34. What is a normal operating condition for electrical equipment . 

Installed properly, maintained properly, use in accordance with mfg instructions, panels and doors closed/secured, no evidence of impending failure 

36. What is Two-Person Verification (3-way Communication).  

Controller requests operator to perform a step, operator repeats and visually indicates step and equipment to be operated, Controller confirms, Operator performs the step
