- Chemical Inventory is what we'll be focused on today
    
    - What is EPCRA?
        
        - Reg passed by EPA in response to Bhopal disaster in India
        - Purpose of EPCRA
            
            - Develop emergency plans to protect people and environment from chemical accidents
        - Sections of EPCRA
            
            - Focus on 312 - Tier II Reporting - must report, to state and local officials and local fire departments, inventories of all on-site chemicals for which SDS's exist that are stored above the threshold
    - Does my facility have to report
        
        - Federally -
        - State level (and sometimes at the city level)
    - Section 302 - EHS Threshold Planning Quantities
    - Get past report to determine if facility has previously reported #important
    - Determine reporting thresholds for State
        
        - EPA Website has a State Tier II Reporting Requirements and Procedures
    - Inventory records
    - Purchase records
    - Estimates
    - Compare the max amount to the reporting threshold for that material
    - If you do not exceed any thresholds, then you do not need to file a Tier II report AND document the evaluation.
    - We have to report - Now what?
        
        - Tier II prep and form completion (6 steps shown in PPT)
