**Environmental Health & Safety:**

1. **Audits & Inspections Conducted:** Proactive, third-party or in-house audits, not including regulatory inspections or audits conducted. These include those of significance performed no more frequently than monthly. This does not include HRA assessments, or risk specific audits. The intention is to capture more comprehensive audits conducted on a monthly, quarterly or annual basis.
2. **% of Compliance Findings Closed:** A percentage of compliance findings closed from all audits reported on.
3. **% of Employees Submitting EHS Observations:**  A percentage of STACK employees that have submitted observations during the reporting period. 
    - **Data source:** SOSPES
4. **% of Positive Observations and Suggestions​:** The percentage of positive observations or actionable suggestions for safety improvements reported during the reporting period.
    - **Data source:** SOSPES
5. **% of EHS Trainings Completed:** The percentage of EHS trainings completed by employees.
    - **Data source:** Learning Management System (LMS)
