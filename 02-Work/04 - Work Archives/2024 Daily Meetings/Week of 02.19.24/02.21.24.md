- Donna reached out to me last Wednesday when she was reviewing the dashboard and she thought the number of "recommendations" for CHI01A was too many and looked out of place
- In looking at the "source of truth" spreadsheet for CHI01A, we noticed that what was being reflected on the dashboard were the total number of recommendations, regardless of whether or not they were tied to N/A, Met, Partially Met, or Not Met
- Recommendations associated with audit criteria/line items that were N/A or Met were still showing on the dashboard and could be misinterpreted as audit findings.
- Short term work around - each EHS Manager should make sure their respective "source of truth" spreadsheets do not have verbiage in the recommendations field if they are not actually findings from the audit. If this situation is found, cut/copy/paste the recommendation into the "notes" field. Doing so will more accurately reflect how many findings there were and how many of those findings are still open or have been closed.
- Long term fix - adjust Dashboard code to pull actual findings (partially or not met items) vs. pulling total number of recommendations.
