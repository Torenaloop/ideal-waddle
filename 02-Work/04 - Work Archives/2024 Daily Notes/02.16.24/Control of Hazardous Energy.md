## Class exercises (Control of Hazardous Energy)
   

1. **Slide 17 - Review Control of Hazardous Energy Program (15 minutes)**
    1. Material needed:
        1. STACK AMER AP - LOTO AP
        2. ==STACK Site Specific LOTO Template AP==
    2. Have attendees go up to the white board to capture what STACK requires over/above the OSHA requirements/exceptions discussed in previous slides.
2. **Slide 26 - Shot various LOTO devices (5 minutes)**
    1. Material needed:
        1. LOTO devices
    2. Hands on demo of LOTO devices
3. **Slide 56 - LOTO Procedure Review (10 minutes)**
    1. Material needed:
        1. 2 MOPS: 1 "GOOD" and 1 "BAD" handouts for each attendee
    2. Review the 2 MOPS with the class
4. **Slide 57 - Portable Test Meter Selection (10 minutes)**
    1. Material needed:
        1. ==Portable Test Equipment==
        2. ==Portable Test Equipment Manual==
    2. Demo how to find voltage/category settings
    3. Visually confirm attendees can find the info on the meter and in the manual
5. **After last slide** - ==Level 1 Course Survey==
