## Class exercises (Electrical Safety)
   

1. **Slide 20 - Review Electrical Safety Program (15 minutes)**
    1. Material needed:
        1. STACK Electrical Safety AP
        2. NFPA 70E
    2. Have attendees go up to the white board to capture what STACK requires over/above NFPA 70e….?
2. **Slide 24 - Job Safety Analysis (15 minutes)**
    1. Material needed:
        1. "Good" JSA
        2. JSA template
    2. Have attendees practice developing a JSA for an electrical task
3. **Slide 56 - Activity: Energized Electrical Work Permit Review (10 minutes)**
    1. Material needed:
        1. EEWP handouts for each attendee
    2. Review the EEWP with the class
4. ==Familiarize yourself with the content of== **slide 58 & 59** ==in order to be able to speak intelligently on the topic==
5. **Slide 66 - Review Arc Flash Label Information (10 minutes)**
    1. Material needed:
        1. ==Arc Flash Label Information "course handout"==
    2. Review the elements on the information found on the arc flash labels
6. **Slide 85 - Inspecting Rubber Insulating Gloves (10 minutes)**
    1. Material needed:
        1. Rubber Insulating Gloves
    2. 1 course attendee will perform inspection and 1 course attendee will coach on conducting an inspection
7. **After last slide** - ==Level 1 Course Survey==
