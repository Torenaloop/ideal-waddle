As we discussed today, we’ve discovered that some of our client racks are pushing voltage (35V DC)through the neutral line on de-energized RPPs and to the neutral bar. The only way to truly isolate this voltage would be disconnecting the client racks, which they have refused to do. Our mitigation plan for this work moving forward is; if voltage is detected on the neutral bar after the RPP has been de-energized, a grounding strap will be attached between the neutral bar and the ground bar, grounding out the circuit and allowing work to be performed with no hazard.
