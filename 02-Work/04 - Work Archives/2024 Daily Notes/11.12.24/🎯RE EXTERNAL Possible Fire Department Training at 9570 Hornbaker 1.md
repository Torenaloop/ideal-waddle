Joe,  
   
We are looking at February 17, 2025 (Presidents Day) for the training. Our hope is that by picking a holiday, it might lessen the impact of our presence to your facility and company. Obviously, we are open to moving the dates around to whatever would work best for your facility.  
   
We also picked 9570 Hornbaker with the information you still had some data halls that are finished but without customers. If you think a different facility would be better, we are open to that as well.  
   
We are hoping for two sessions of 28-30 firefighters each. The morning session would be from 0900-1100, and the afternoon session would be from 1300-1500. We feel that this is the perfect number of firefighters to be able to simulate a full response to your facilities, and still try to keep our footprint smallish and not disturb your facility. We are planning on having 2-3 controllers to manage the scenario and evaluate the crews once inside the facility. If needed, we can provide a roster as crews arrive to provide accountability to you and your customers.   
   
The scenario **WILL NOT** involve any water, nor any hose lines being dragged into your facilities so there is no chance of any water damage. We are really aiming to get our crews to focus on communications aspect, as well them developing a plan with your personnel to remove the smoke and secure the power to the sections we will be working in.  
   
For the scenarios, our crews will be breathing with their air bottles and their lens will be covered with a smoke layer, that way we can simulate low visibility conditions and a dangerous atmosphere without doing any possible damage to your facility.   
   
The only things we would like to ask for from your facility is if we could have the fire alarms going off just like they would be in a real world event, as well as whatever door alarms might be going off as our guys enter the “secure areas”, and the HVAC to be working at its normal capacity to fully simulate the issues we anticipate for communicating.  
   
Thank you for considering letting us come to your facility and practice this scenario.    
   
Technician II Kevin Fagga  
Prince William County Department of Fire and Rescue  
Engine 525-C
