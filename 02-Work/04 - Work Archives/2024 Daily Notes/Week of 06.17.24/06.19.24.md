**Subject:** Permit for A-Frame Ladder TSG
 
There are two locations in the hall ways where TSG must access the drop ceiling to take measurements and core holes for the conduit installations. Theses areas can not be reached above the drop ceiling with a lift or a platform ladder unless a portion of the ceiling grid is removed. Operations does not want remove the grid for such a small scope of work.  For these two locations I have authorized TSG to use a permitted ( by operations) A-Frame ladder with the understanding that it is a two man operation. We discussed the safe use of a ladder and there must be a spotter to secure the ladder while in use. David and I have discussed this and are in agreement that with the safety spotter in place it will make this job task quicker and easier.  Please contact me if you have any concerns or questions.
