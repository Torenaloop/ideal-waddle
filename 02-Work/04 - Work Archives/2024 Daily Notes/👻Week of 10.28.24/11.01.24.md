 Hey Tom. I noticed thought I'd pass along some info on additional features in the table being used to track action items/deliverables. It won't hurt my feelings if no one is interested at this moment, but in the spirit of information sharing, I wanted to share the following:
 
1. I've mocked up a similar action tracker table
2. Hover your cursor over this guy and click
3. It will open up the "card"
4. You can see all the other "properties" in this view also
5. Consider adding your daily notes as follows
    1. Go to lower half of the card
    2. Hit forward slash "/"
    3. Type "collapsable heading" (1, 2, or 3)
    4. Type "Notes"
    5. Hit enter
    6. Type forward slash "/"
    7. Type "date"
    8. Choose the applicable day from the calendar that populates
    9. Once the date is chosen, you can then type any/all the notes you'd like to add for that particular day for that particular task (or sub-tasks)
