Hi Ted,  
   
   
Thanks for sending the materials, I have read through them and attached the UAP governing SVY for comparison.  
   
A few things to call our attention to in reviewing the DIG version.  
 

1. The **Excavation Tolerance Zone** is 24”, for SVY **6 feet** is the established envelope in which “soft digging” (vac truck or hand)  needs to occur. From a STACK risk mitigation perspective, 24 inches is very aggressive.
2. The DIG plan addresses **Locates** and Potholing but does not define the radius around area of work that they be performed. For comparison, the SVY plan establishes 50 feet around the proposed excavation site.
3. The concept of an **Excavation Observer** or “Spotter” but it only loosely defines the conditions where one is required. Stating that “_When excavation equipment is operating near known underground utilities, an observer must assist the operator”_ . The SVY plan establishes Spotters are needed within 15 feet of known utilities.
4. The DIG plan does not seem to address **excavation around active roadways**,. The SVY plan establishes hard barricades are required when digging 10 feet in proximity.

   
   
These all deserve some conversation but the biggest concern we’ll want to revisit before bringing associated work to CAB will be the tolerance zone. Spencer is on PTO tomorrow, but I believe we should both be available on Thursday the 2nd. Would you setup a meeting please?
