- What Happened: With the information available at this time: This afternoon at approximately 3:00 PM Foster fuel was onsite to refuel (1) of the temporary generators, according to the Foster Fuel Technician, there was a fuel line malfunction that resulted in diesel fuel filling the interior compartment of the generator and then overflowing and filling the exterior containment with diesel fuel. After further discussion, Foster fuel was able to get their vac truck mobilized this evening (1/23) and will be onsite within the next 30 minutes to remove the excess diesel and clean the containment. An investigation is underway with a full report to follow on 01.24.25
- Were Corrective/Preventative Actions taken: Yes
- Corrective Action Description: STACK, HITT and Sunbelt called a third -party company to remove the excess fuel. Foster fuel onsite with a vacuum truck to remove the excess fuel and properly dispose of it and clean the containment. Sunbelt will remain onsite overnight to monitor the plant. This incident is still under investigation.
