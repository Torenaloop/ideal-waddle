## Manager/Direct Planner Relationship
 
- ==PROJECT RELATED WORK== - Manager and direct open up the **Operations EHS Planner OR Construction EHS Planner** and check the status of applicable assignments related to 2025 Goals
- ==NON-PROJECT RELATED WORK== - Manager and direct open up the directs **My Task** views in Planner to discuss **work being done, but outside of the 2025 Goals Planner (either for Ops or Construction)**

A people manager can add a task and post to "a" channel about the task | better than email because

- More targeted
- Easier to find/re-find
- Context for the task can be added in the "add note" field or by posting said content in "Channel"
