Hey Stephen,  
   
To recap our call earlier.. We as STACK are taking over the ownership of Canada and any country and provincial training requirements.  
   
From the limited amount of research I did on this to prep for the call, here is what I could find on the CCOHS website regarding required training for employee’s.  
 

- General Workplace Orientation
- WHMIS (Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System)
- Emergency Preparedness (ERP)
- Equipment Operation Training (Specific training for using machinery, tools, and vehicles safely.)
- Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Ergonomics and Safe Lifting Techniques
- Fall Protection
- Violence and Harassment Prevention

This is not provincial specific as I still need to do some research in that realm to make sure nothing is missing. But out of this list about, we currently have something in the LMS for everything except for the Emergency Preparedness, however that is in works and we do have the document already completed, which the team at TOR01A should be trained on already. Everything thing else is either currently in the General Safety Learning Paths, or Site-Specific learning path for TOR01A.  We can create new learning paths for provinces or just overall all of Canada, and assign those trainings as needed for Remote workers.  
   
After the new year, I will sync up with Jesse and Donna about this to make sure we are not missing anything else and then get anything missing into action.
