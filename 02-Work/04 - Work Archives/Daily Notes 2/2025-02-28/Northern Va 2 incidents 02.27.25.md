Subject: READ: Initial information : 2 incidents  
   
Two incidents occurred yesterday in Northern Virginia that need immediate awareness through the portfolio, while we await the formal RCAs.   Please share with your teams.   
   
 Both may initiate regulator response.  In each case there are actions that site teams can take immediately to reduce risks.   Check spill containments and spill kits, review the logistics plans for equipment parking, walk your projects with a critical eye.     
   
Brief narratives:   
 

1. A generator supplying power to the project trailers was leaking diesel fuel.  Initial reports were that 30 gallons were released, this number was updated to 200+ gallons of diesel, into the containment and onto the dirt surrounding containment. Holder is the GC.   Virginia DEQ will be on site today. 

1. An employee operating a dump truck parked the truck on a slope near a building pad and assumed the parking brake was fully engaged.  The employee exited the equipment and noticed that it had begun to roll. They attempted to regain control of the truck but the attempt was unsuccessful.  The truck ran over the employee's foot and crashed through a gate and fence, coming to a stop in a ditch.  This employee sustained fractures to the metatarsal bones of the foot and may require surgery.  EE Reed is the GC.
