Thanks Kate,  
Team, here’s where my head is related to this data.  
   
I look at the Violence line. We did roll out a violence in the workplace prevention program last year. How well do you think we’ve implemented? How much are we evaluating our controls and confident in their effectiveness?  We have programs for Fall and Struck By, both of which we’ve had serious incidents and near misses in the past; yet from this data we know those SIFs are happening, likely to Companies that also felt they have/had good programs for prevention. We think we have methods and procedures that should prevent SIF in these areas, yet we have incidents.  
   
What about Violence? Are we running on luck? I say luck because I do believe this is a real threat over the last decade or more that employers haven’t fully figured out how to effectively prevent and manage. I think the statistics here show that.  
   
Keep that in mind as we roll into 2025, work that program and manage that risk of violence in the same way you do these other hazards and risks. Leverage our security team as well and ensure we are raising concerns when we see them, feel them, or our gut is telling us there is something there.  
   
Donna
