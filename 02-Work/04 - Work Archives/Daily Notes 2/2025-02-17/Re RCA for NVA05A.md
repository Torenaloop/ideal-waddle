EHS commencing 2025 site safety audits. (EHS-Jesse Kesner)

Incorporated as a deliverable as part of the Site Integration process. (TechOps-Ryan Arthur)  
   
R4. Critical Operations leadership and Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) conduct random spot checks of work execution to ensure adherence to procedures and policies, verifying that tasks are performed by qualified workers. Take corrective action when discrepancies are identified. Increased attention and oversight are particularly necessary following changes in operational methods, policies, or staffing due to the higher risk of non-compliance. Implement methods to maintain transparency of spot check outcomes.  
Not Started-No plan or organizational goal in place around this.

Incorporated as a deliverable as part of COM Onboarding and certification. (TechOps-Tony Badolato)  
   
R6. Evaluate the current Change Control policy and standardize workflow across regions to meet industry best practices such as: clear roles and responsibilities, a predictive approval workflow, defined timeline, gating requirements, document control, and success monitoring.  
Incorporated as a deliverable in CAB Process Improvement Initiative (TechOps-Ryan Arthur)  
   
R7. Develop and implement an Operations Onboarding Program (COM/ACOM/COT/ACOT) that sets clear expectations of roles and responsibilities, verifies acknowledgement of Administrative Policies, and teaches company best practices for risk assessment and risk management. Incorporate relevant KPIs to increase transparency, and foster ownership and accountability.  
Incorporated as a deliverable as part of COM Onboarding and certification. (TechOps-Tony Badolato)  
   
R8. Any Change Notification impacting Critical Operations' daily tasks or expectations must be sent as a formal notice, followed by monitoring and feedback to ensure understanding and adoption.
