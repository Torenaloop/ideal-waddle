Do we need to add solutions to the build plan going forward - no
 
==Main takeaways:==  
==Team was fine using "temporary guardrail systems" tens of feet off the edge of the affected parapet wall that were not close to the edge, where no equipment would be and there were no walkways (in other words, there wouldn't be a business need to travel past where the temp guard rails system would be erected). This solution in these areas satisfied Brian Medina's desire to maintain aesthetics for the client and to minimize the amount of that would be put in the roof.==
 
==Team was aligned on erecting a permanent guard rail system on the remainder of the perimeter where the parapet is not high enough as the paths of travel/proximity to equipment necessitated it and the view(s) of the guardrail system would not cause an aesthetic issue.==
