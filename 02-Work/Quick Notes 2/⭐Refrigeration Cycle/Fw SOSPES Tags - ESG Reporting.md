There are 4 new observation tags labeled "EHS Only" for tracking SOSPES reports with more specificity. The SOSPES Closeout Checklist has been updated to reflect this. (All of this will be added to a SOSPES AP).
 ![Observation Tags 3 CONFINED SPACES 3 ELECTRICAL 3 ...](Files/Exported%20image%2020260127090255-1.png)  

**Lagging/Leading Indicators**

- Proactive: the observation is indicating the "potential" of a future consequence, often submitted as a suggestion.
    - _Example: low lighting on the roof increases the potential for slips, trips, falls_
-   
    

_OR_
 - Reactive: the observation is being submitted after a consequence has been discovered.
    - _Example: expired fire extinguisher needs replaced or recharged_
       
**Corrective Measures**

- Actionable - Level 1: can be considered "low hanging fruit" from an Ops perspective, something that requires minimal effort to resolve with low impact (_low caliber observations_).
    - _Example: ladder found leaning up against the wall was placed on ground_
-   
    

_OR_
 - Actionable - Level 2: significant thought and effort required to close out with high impact ("_real" observations_).
    - _Example: Plumbed chemical showers installed in the 90's has a 15-year life span and needs CAPEX retrofit_
       
_Obviously the two Actionable examples given are on opposite sides of the spectrum, we just need a way to filter the varying impact/caliber of reports._
