- **Why It Matters**:
    - Cooling systems, like chillers or computer room air conditioners (CRACs), are complex and rely on the refrigeration cycle.
    - Common issues include:
        - Loss of refrigerant.
        - Compressor inefficiency.
        - Blocked condenser or evaporator coils.
        - Faulty expansion valves.
- **Refrigeration Cycle Relevance**:
    - Technicians who understand the cycle can pinpoint the root cause of cooling system malfunctions and work effectively with HVAC specialists or independently resolve minor issues.

- **Why It Matters**:
    - Data centers often have redundant cooling systems to ensure uninterrupted operations even during failures.
