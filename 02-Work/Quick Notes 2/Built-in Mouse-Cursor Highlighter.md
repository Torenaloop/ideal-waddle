You can definitely add a highlighter to your mouse cursor to make it easier for viewers to follow along during your screen recordings. Here are a few methods you can try:  
**1. Built-in Windows Settings**  
Windows has some built-in options to change the appearance of your mouse cursor:

1. **Open Settings**: Press Win + I to open the Settings app.
2. **Ease of Access**: Go to Ease of Access \> Mouse pointer.
3. **Change Pointer Color**: Select a color that stands out on your screen1.

**2. Using Third-Party Software**  
There are several third-party applications that can add a highlighter effect to your cursor:

- **Mouse Pointer Highlight by Catnip5**: This free app from the Microsoft Store allows you to customize the highlight size, color, and opacity[2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gk70xEOoPmY).
- **AutoHotKey**: You can use AutoHotKey scripts to create a customizable halo around your cursor[3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwSqtNvT7to).

**3. Screen Recording Software**  
Some screen recording software includes built-in options to highlight the mouse cursor:

- **EaseUS RecExperts**: This software allows you to add mouse click effects and highlight the cursor during recording[4](https://recorder.easeus.com/screen-recording-tips/capture-mouse-cursor-in-recording.html).
- **OBS Studio**: OBS Studio can capture the mouse highlight if you use an app like Mouse Pointer Highlight[2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gk70xEOoPmY).

**Steps for OBS Studio**

1. **Install Mouse Pointer Highlight**: Download and install the app from the Microsoft Store.
2. **Customize Highlight**: Open the app and adjust the highlight settings to your preference.
