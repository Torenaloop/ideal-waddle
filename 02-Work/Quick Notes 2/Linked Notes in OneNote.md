Certainly! Linked Notes in OneNote allow you to dock OneNote to one side of your computer screen while viewing websites or other apps on the other side. Here’s how to use it:

1. **Internet Explorer**:
    - Make sure the Command bar is visible (if not, right-click the title bar and choose Command Bar).
    - In the menu bar, select “OneNote Linked Notes.”
    - Move the cursor in the docked OneNote window to start taking notes.
    - Notes are automatically linked to the content you’re researching.
2. **Word, PowerPoint, or OneNote**:
    - Choose “Review” \> “Linked Notes.”
    - In the Select Location dialog, pick a location for the new notes page.
    - Notes will be added to an existing page or create a new one.
    - Start taking notes as usual.
3. **During Linked Notes**:
    - OneNote stores a thumbnail image of the page, a text excerpt, and a link to the source content.
    - To stop taking linked notes, click the chain link icon in the upper-right corner of the docked OneNote window.
4. **Accessing Linked Content**:
    - Hover over individual notes to see details of the linked file.
    - Click a thumbnail image to open the associated file.
    - [To view all linked documents or remove unnecessary links, click the chain link icon on the page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/take-linked-notes-in-onenote-8e9e53ce-b2f1-4c4c-9745-fff878eec0b7)1.

Feel free to explore this feature—it’s a handy way to keep your notes connected to your research! 😊
