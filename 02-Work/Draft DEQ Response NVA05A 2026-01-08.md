**Subject:** Response to Stack Testing Observations – December 16, 2025

Dear [DEQ Representative Name],

STACK Americas is providing this response in follow‑up to the Virginia DEQ observations and subsequent discussions regarding the stack testing conducted on **December 16, 2025**.

### Summary of Findings

Following the December testing, Rehlko (Kohler) and Fidelity technicians reviewed engine performance data, operational logs, and diagnostic information collected while the units were operating under load. Based on this review, the manufacturer concluded the engines were operating normally and **within manufacturer specifications**, with no mechanical abnormalities identified. These findings were further reviewed by Rehlko engineering support.

It is important to note that “within specification” refers to normal operational specifications and diagnostic parameters observed during testing and does not diminish STACK’s responsibility to address the opacity observations identified during the DEQ‑requested testing.

### Evaluation of Testing Methods

- A **Method 22 visual observation** was conducted during the initial testing event.
- A **Method 9 opacity test** was not performed during the December site visit, as it was not included in the original scope and requires specific run durations, certified personnel, observation positioning, and sun angle requirements.
- STACK has coordinated with ALL4, who has confirmed the availability of a **Method 9‑certified inspector**. Stack intends to conduct Method 9 testing after completion of corrective and mitigation measures described below.

### Mitigation Measures (Completed / Planned)

Based on manufacturer recommendations and third‑party consultation, the following mitigation steps are being implemented or planned:

1. **Valve lash inspection and adjustment** to confirm proper combustion performance.
2. **Fuel sampling and laboratory analysis** to verify fuel quality and rule out contamination. This activity was delayed due to the unavailability of an advanced fuel sampling kit during the December visit and holiday laboratory closures. Procurement and scheduling are in progress.
3. **Replacement of fuel filters** to ensure clean and unrestricted fuel delivery.
4. **Addition of a fuel conditioner** (Lubrizol) to clean injectors and combustion chambers and reduce the potential for coking.
5. **Post‑mitigation validation run** consisting of operating the units at full load for approximately four hours to confirm stable engine performance and exhaust opacity under sustained operating conditions.

### Planned Follow‑Up Testing

Upon completion of the above measures, STACK plans to:

- Conduct **Method 9 opacity testing** using a certified observer under conditions consistent with DEQ requirements.
- Provide DEQ with the Method 9 results, fuel analysis data (once available), and confirmation of corrective actions completed.

### Current Status

Fuel sampling and results remain pending due to holiday scheduling delays. STACK is actively coordinating with the manufacturer and testing laboratories and will provide updated timelines once confirmed.

STACK Americas remains committed to full compliance with applicable air permit requirements and to working collaboratively with VA DEQ to resolve this matter. Please let us know if DEQ has any additional questions or if further clarification is required while these actions are underway.
