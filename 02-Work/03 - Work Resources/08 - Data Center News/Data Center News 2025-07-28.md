- Concerns included lack of public input and environmental impact; the setback may complicate future proposals. ([Virginia Mercury](https://virginiamercury.com/2025/07/28/amazon-pulls-louisa-county-data-center-proposal-after-strong-resistance/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Amazon pulls Louisa County data center proposal after strong resistance"))
    

### 🔹 Public Backlash and Growth Pressure

- **Virginia** remains the world’s data center capital, hosting nearly 600 facilities, including over 150 hyperscale centers.
    
- Residents in **Chesapeake and beyond** are organizing a growing “Not In My Backyard” (NIMBY) movement due to concerns over power, water usage, noise, and local quality of life.
    
- Dominion Energy is expected to build an additional **40 GW of power capacity** specifically to support data center expansion. ### 🔹 Local Zoning Actions and Regulatory Shifts
