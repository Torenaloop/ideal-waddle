**Why Conduct Investigations?**

1. Show commitment to employees
2. Examine the facts and circumstances of an accident
3. Learn to prevent future accidents
4. Analyze and learn about all the system in your organization #important
 
Not just to determine what changes need to be made to correct the immediate problems that appeared to have caused the accident - but to learn more about all aspects of your organization!
 
**Common Issues**

- We find only a single cause, often the final triggering event
- We find only immediate causes and do not look for weaknesses in the management system
- We list human error as a cause w/tout saying what is needed to prevent errors or mitigate for them
 
**A Systems Approach Learns More**  
Concluding that carelessness or failure
 
**Systems Approach**

- Reinforce a systems approach by stressing it in written programs as well as investigation procedures.
- Identifying and correcting as many causes as possible should always be the key objective
 
**Four Components of a Workplace Incident Investigation Program**

1. Formal/written incident investigation policy
2. Reporting
3. Investigating
4. Training in emergency response
