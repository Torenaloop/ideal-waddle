## Joe Meeting Summary

*All,*

*Here’s a summary from today’s meeting with DEQ.*

*Jim Hubert, David Nuckolls, and I met with Paul Koxwell, Sr. Air Compliance Inspector with the VA DEQ. Paul was following up on a complaint filed by our neighbor, the VA Department of Forensic Science (building to the southwest), on 9/10. They reported that air quality inside their building was compromised by generator operation at NVA05A.*

*Paul clarified that DEQ does not regulate indoor air quality but, since our generators may have contributed, they wanted to confirm our compliance with our site air permit. Based on his review, Paul believes we are in compliance but has requested a formal letter/email from STACK documenting:*

- *The reason for the generator run on 9/10*
- *Confirmation of compliance with our air permit*
- *Supporting correspondence with NOVEC explaining the outage*

*Outage details (9/10/2025):*

- *On 9/8, NOVEC informed us that their permanent circuit/substation would be ready for energization on 9/10. Timing had been fluid leading up to this due to substation construction/commissioning. NOVEC requested we transition as soon as possible to free capacity on the temporary circuit for other sites.*
- *On 9/10, NVA05A Phase 1 was placed on generator at ~6:30 AM. Phases 2 & 3 were allowed to go dead since they had not yet been turned over to the client.*
- *The MV gear was reconfigured to tie into the new circuit. During re-energization, issues with MV settings in the main switch caused extended generator runtime.*
- *Generators were taken offline at ~7:30 PM after the issues were resolved.*

*Attached for reference:*

- *Generator log (day and year)*
- *Site air permit*
- *NOVEC correspondence*

*Next step: Paul has requested the formal letter/email outlined above. Is this something that Legal will draft, or should we take the lead with Legal’s review?*
