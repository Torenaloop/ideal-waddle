## Gap Analysis and Recommendations

- ✅ **Complete/Aligned:** The JSA activity title generally matches part of the MOP scope by referencing energization of PDUs, UODP, STSDP, and STS breakers.
    
- ✅ **Complete/Aligned:** The JSA identifies required training as **S1 Qualification** and **Electrical Safety**, which is directionally appropriate for the MOP’s energized electrical switching scope.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA has only one listed task, **“Energize UODP/STSDP Breakers,”** but the MOP includes multiple additional physical work evolutions: UPS transfer to maintenance bypass, MSB MBB/UIB switching, STS feeder energization, STS bypass operation, PDU main energization, return of STS units to normal, and return of UPS to inverter.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA does not clearly list hazards in the hazard column for the task row. Electrical shock, arc flash, burns, pressure blast, wrong-equipment operation, unexpected energization, and loss of critical load should be explicitly identified.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA required PPE lists **40 cal arc-flash suit** only, but the MOP specifies different PPE levels at different stages, including **8 cal/cm²**, **40 cal/cm²**, and **4 cal/cm²** arc-rated PPE. The JSA should reflect PPE by task or state “per equipment arc-flash label and MOP step-specific PPE.”
    
- ⚠️ **Significant Safety Concern:** The MOP lists **MSB-P5-240-MBB** with an incident energy of **59.2 cal/cm²**, while switching steps later identify **40 cal/cm²** PPE. This needs verification before execution because 40 cal PPE may not be adequate for equipment labeled above 40 cal/cm².
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA does not address LOTO removal controls for UODP-P5-240-MCB and STSDP-P5-240-MCB. It should include authorized LOTO removal, affected-personnel clearance, verification of downstream status, and controlled energization.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA does not address SKRU/interlock key operation hazards. The MOP uses SKRU key sequencing for MSB breaker operations, which introduces wrong-key operation and interlock-sequence hazards.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA does not address STS mechanical interlock bar operation. Add pinch-point controls, “do not force interlock” language, and sequence-verification controls.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA does not address PDU main breaker energization for PDU-P5-240-01 through PDU-P5-240-05, despite the MOP requiring sequential PDU main breaker closures.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA does not address return-to-normal activities, including STS return from bypass and UPS return from maintenance bypass to inverter. These are separate energized switching evolutions and should have their own hazards and controls.
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Expand the JSA from one broad task row into multiple task rows matching the major physical MOP evolutions listed in the table above.
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Replace generic controls such as “ensure personnel are trained” and “wear PPE” with action-based controls such as “positively identify equipment,” “remove LOTO only under authorized release,” “stand clear during breaker operation,” “verify expected status before each state change,” and “stop work on abnormal alarm or failed verification.”
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Revise the PPE section to either list PPE by task or state: **“Wear arc-rated PPE, shock protection, and supporting PPE as specified in the MOP and equipment arc-flash labels for each step.”**
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Confirm whether MSB-P5-240-MBB switching is intended to be performed with 40 cal PPE despite the MOP’s 59.2 cal/cm² listing. If the label is accurate, revise the procedure/JSA to require appropriate controls before switching, such as reassessing PPE, establishing an expanded arc-flash boundary, using remote operation if available, or obtaining site safety/engineering approval.
    

**Clarification needed before final field use:** Can you confirm whether the **59.2 cal/cm²** value for **MSB-P5-240-MBB** is the active arc-flash label for the breaker being operated in this MOP, or whether there is another label/condition that supports the listed 40 cal PPE?
