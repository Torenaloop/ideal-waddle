**2. Case 165743 – ATL 01A Annual “Pull the Plug” Test**

**Presenters:** Jack Gillian & Bleau Bryant  
**Key Points:**

- Procedure unchanged since 2018; template updates triggered CAB re-review.
- Georgia Power will operate external equipment; STAC monitors internal system behavior.
- First transfer is utility-to-utility (open transition within 8 seconds); second transfer is utility-to-generator.
- Multiple verification sequences discussed: CRAC/CRAH performance, UPS loads, SPDUs, battery support, breaker conditions, and fire alarm behavior.
- Need to explicitly validate load before/after transitions and confirm system stability.
- Restoration is automatic after ~1600 seconds; team confirmed load behavior must be tracked graphically.
