## Overall Finding

**Significant gaps identified. The current JSA is not aligned to this MOP and should not be used as-is.** The JSA is titled for placing the lineup in maintenance bypass for **Li-on Tamer testing** and contains only one task—opening/closing MSB breakers—with arc flash as the sole hazard. The MOP covers complete UPS isolation and restoration for **battery replacement of Cabinet 2, Jar 213**, including HMI transfers, SKRU interlocks, multiple breaker operations, LOTO, battery-string isolation, Live-Dead-Live testing, vendor handoff, re-energization, and return to inverter.

**Assumption:** The table below covers STACK personnel performing the physical isolation, switching, LOTO, handoff, and restoration. Administrative notifications, approvals, emails, and EPMS checks are intentionally excluded. The vendor’s physical battery replacement requires a separate task-specific JSA unless this JSA is intended to cover that work.

## Gap Analysis and Recommendations

- ✅ **Complete/Aligned:** The existing JSA correctly identifies electrical energy and arc flash as hazards during MSB breaker operation. Arc flash is relevant to this MOP.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete — Scope:** The JSA states “Maintenance Bypass for Li-on Tamer Testing,” while the MOP is for isolation and restoration supporting battery replacement of Cabinet 2, Jar 213. The activity description, date, equipment identifiers, and approval basis should be updated for this specific work.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete — Tasks:** The JSA omits the UPS HMI transfers, SKRU/key-interlock operations, MBB/UIB switching, MOB/MIB isolation, LOTO, battery isolation, CPCB1 operation, Live-Dead-Live test, vendor handoff, re-energization, converter restart, and final return to inverter.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete — Hazards:** The JSA does not address electrical shock, stored battery energy, backfeed, spring-charged mechanisms, pinch points, line-of-fire exposure, incorrect switching sequence, critical-load interruption, battery thermal events, or unexpected energization.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete — Controls:** “Wear arc flash gear” and “utilize AMS” are too generic. The JSA should identify qualified-person requirements, equipment verification, electrical boundaries, two-person switching verification, LOTO ownership, zero-energy verification, meter validation, breaker sequencing, stop-work criteria, and vendor handoff controls.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete — PPE:** The MOP’s incident-energy table lists exposures as high as **59.2 cal/cm²**, while the detailed PPE tables name cut-resistant gloves, hard hat, safety glasses/goggles, hearing protection, and leather work shoes. The current JSA only states “arc flash gear.” The exact label-based arc-rated ensemble, shock protection, and acceptable work method must be reconciled before work.
    
- ⚠️ **Potential Sequence Conflict:** MOP Step 36 directs personnel to doff PPE immediately before Step 37, where the vendor performs Live-Dead-Live testing. This is ambiguous and potentially exposes the tester unless the vendor remains in appropriate electrical PPE throughout the test.
    
- ⚠️ **Equipment Identification:** The MOP uses differing MOB/MIB prefixes between the isolation and restoration steps and includes “M0B” in the incident-energy table. All equipment IDs should be reconciled against the single-line and field labels to prevent operation of the wrong breaker.
    
- ⚠️ **Vendor Work Not Assessed:** The actual removal and replacement of Jar 213 is not described in the detailed procedure. The supplied JSA does not cover battery handling or vendor task hazards. A separate vendor JSA and method are required unless the procedure is expanded.
    
- ⚠️ **Potentially Unsupported Control:** The JSA requires the AMS arc-reduction switch, but the MOP does not identify which breakers have AMS, when it is to be enabled, or how its status is verified. Confirm applicability or revise the control.
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Replace the single JSA task row with the scope-specific task section above. Update the activity title to reflect UPS-P4-220-01/-02 isolation and restoration for Cabinet 2, Jar 213 battery replacement; list authorized LOTO and qualified-electrical-person requirements; and add exact PPE based on each equipment label.
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Revise the MOP or provide a formal clarification for PPE during Live-Dead-Live, the correct MOB/MIB equipment identifiers, and the approved use of AMS or other arc-reduction methods.
