# Cases Reviewed

## 1. Case 180388 – ATL 01A

### DHCP Migration

**Decision:** Approved

### Summary

- Previously held due to concerns regarding potential impact to critical systems.
- Follow-up validation confirmed:
    - No impact to **BMS / EPMS**
    - No impact to **chillers or HVAC systems**
- Scope limited to **end-user devices (Wi-Fi, phones, workstations)**.

### Key Outcome

- Strong example of proper CAB lifecycle:
    - Risk identified → validated → approved

### Action Items

- Add **planned start date in NetSuite**
- Ensure **onsite monitoring during execution (~9:30 AM)**

---

## 2. Case 179944 – NAL 01A / 01B

### Potholing (Hydrovac Utility Verification)

**Decision:** Approved

### Summary

- Hydrovac excavation to verify **fiber and power routing** prior to construction.
- Non-destructive method reduces risk of utility strike.
- Work performed at:
    - 25 ft intervals (fiber)
    - 50 ft intervals (power)

### Key Risks Identified

- Potential **fiber damage → carrier outage**
- **Trip hazard from open excavations**
- Limited initial contractor oversight

### Action Items

- Add step to **verify all potholes are properly backfilled**
- Perform **initial oversight of contractor work (first phase)**
- Maintain **daily awareness of excavation locations**
