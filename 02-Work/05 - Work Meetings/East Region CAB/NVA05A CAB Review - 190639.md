## Gap Analysis and Recommendations

- ✅ **Complete/Aligned:** The current JSA identifies the principal switching activities—MBB, UIB, battery MCBs, MIBs, MOBs, and live-dead-live—and its overall order generally follows the MOP.
    
- ✅ **Complete/Aligned:** The JSA PPE list broadly matches the MOP’s detailed switching PPE: 40-cal suit and hood, electrical gloves with leather protectors, eye protection, hearing protection, and safety-toe boots.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete — arc-flash data:** Multiple JSA rows contain “XXX cal/cm².” MOP page 2 identifies 29 cal/cm² for listed UPS and battery equipment, but it does not establish that MBB, UIB, MIB, and MOB have the same value. Enter the current incident energy, working distance, and arc-flash boundary from each equipment label or study; do not substitute 29 cal/cm² by assumption.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete — shock data:** The JSA assigns “480 VAC” to battery MCB work. Battery strings are a DC source, so the actual DC nominal voltage and applicable test points must be stated separately from the 480-VAC portions of the UPS.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete — controls:** “Only having authorized personnel in the work area” is not an adequate control by itself. It does not establish qualification, arc-flash and shock boundaries, barricades, equipment-condition checks, line-of-fire positioning, interlock requirements, or stop-work criteria.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete — LOTO:** The MOP applies LOTO to two MOBs and two MIBs, but the JSA does not identify applying, maintaining, verifying, and removing LOTO as a task. The MOP also opens twelve battery MCBs and locks cabinet doors without explicitly showing personal or group LOTO on those battery sources. Confirm that this method satisfies the site LOTO policy or add approved lockable isolation points.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete — live-dead-live:** The JSA lists PPE but no testing method. Add meter inspection and rating, known-live verification before and after the test, all required AC and DC test points, stored-energy discharge time, probe control, and recorded readings. MOP pages 12–13 contain the voltage table but do not fully state these controls.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete — switching sequence:** The JSA combines opening MIBs and MOBs and also combines their restoration. The MOP requires MOBs to be opened before MIBs; restoration requires MIBs to be closed, the inverters to be started, and only then the MOBs to be closed. Separate these into distinct rows to prevent an incorrect sequence.
    
- ⚠️ **MOP steps not covered in the JSA:** SKRU/key-interlock manipulation, load-sharing verification, BCU power-switch operation, battery-door locking, tool and fastener accountability, pre-energization area clearance, and restoration of barriers and covers are absent or only implied.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing hazard:** MOP page 2 expressly lists slip, trip, and fall risk, but the JSA contains no corresponding task, hazard, or housekeeping control.
    
- ⚠️ **Vendor work scope:** The MOP releases the vendor to perform scheduled work but does not describe the maintenance task, and the JSA covers only the vendor’s live-dead-live test. The maintenance itself requires a separate task-specific JSA unless it is explicitly outside the scope of this JSA.
    
- ⚠️ **PPE and tool classification:** The insulated flat-head screwdriver is listed as PPE in the JSA and MOP overview; it should be listed as a voltage-rated tool. “Electrical Gloves” should be written as the specified glove class with leather protectors and should include pre-use inspection and test-date verification.
    
- ⚠️ **Training:** The JSA lists S1 and UPS shutdown/restoration training, but the MOP
- requires qualified personnel and coordinated LOTO. Add electrically qualified-person, arc-flash/shock, and authorized-employee LOTO training where applicable.
    
- ✅ **No clearly irrelevant JSA task:** Arc flash and shock are relevant throughout the switching process. The concern is inaccurate or incomplete hazard data—particularly “480 VAC” for battery work—not an unnecessary hazard category.
    
- ⚠️ **Procedure inconsistency requiring correction:** MOP step 71 identifies “P1-120-01-E Door 6” and closes the E MCB again, although the isolation sequence identifies Door 6 as P1-120-01-F. This appears to omit restoration of the 01-F cabinet and must be resolved before execution.
