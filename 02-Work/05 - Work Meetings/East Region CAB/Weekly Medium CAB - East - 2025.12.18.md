**3. Case 168631 – Generator House Load Bank Validation (NVA 06A)**

**Presenter:** Kaitlyn Lail  
**Risk Level:** Medium

**Discussion Summary**

High-level operational review of the work activity:

**Scope & Purpose**

- Required per air permit for **Output Control Device (OCD) validation**.
- Using existing on-site **1500 kW load bank** connected to the generator house load bank breaker.
- Generator must be placed in **unavailable** state during testing—output breaker opened and load directed only between alternator and load bank.

**Sequence Highlights (High-Level Review Approach Requested by James)**

- Apply **ATS isolation** (ASCO 7000): bypass to normal, isolate to prevent backfeed.
- Apply **Lotto** to generator output breaker and load bank breaker; unplug HMI and EPO as secondary safeguards.
- Vendors involved:

- **Fidelity/Kohler** – operate load bank and HMI data pull
- **REI** – cable installation & IR (infrared) scanning
- **GCA** – environmental oversight
- **DEQ POC (David Perry)** – must witness test per air permit

- Load bank run plan:

- Incremental ramp-up in 15% steps
- Hold at ~92% (≈1100 kW) for 5 minutes
- Increase to 1300 kW to validate overload trip >1200 kW

- After completion, reconnect shore power, remove load bank safely (live–dead–live), and return generator house to normal configuration.
- Clarified that temporary generator remains onsite but de-terminated until Friday; full demobilization scheduled later.

**Key Discussion Points**

- Rain cannot be present during testing; load bank connection door must remain open.
- Pre-checks (generator and house UPS) should occur **before** any switching to avoid discovering issues mid-isolation.
- DEQ may not stay for full event; readings may still be required before generator returns to service.
- Several corrections required in the MOP (steps 27–28, 51, and restoration order).
