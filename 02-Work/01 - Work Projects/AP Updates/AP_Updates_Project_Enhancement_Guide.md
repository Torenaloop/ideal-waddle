# ERP vs BCP Gap Analysis

## Overview
Systematic comparison of the organization's Emergency Response Plan (ERP) and Business Continuity Plan (BCP) to identify gaps, overlaps, inconsistencies, and opportunities for alignment.

**Project:** [[Project_Summary]] - Administrative Procedures Updates  
**Related:** [[ERP_Revamp]] - Will use findings to update ERP

---

## Purpose

### Why This Analysis Matters
**Problem:** ERP and BCP may have:
- **Gaps:** Important elements missing from one or both
- **Overlaps:** Duplicated content creating confusion
- **Inconsistencies:** Conflicting information between plans
- **Unclear boundaries:** When to use ERP vs BCP

**Solution:** Systematic gap analysis to:
- Identify all gaps, overlaps, and inconsistencies
- Provide recommendations for alignment
- Inform [[ERP_Revamp]] updates
- Ensure seamless transition from emergency response to business continuity

---

## Analysis Framework

### Dimensions to Analyze

#### 1. Scope & Purpose
**Questions:**
- What scenarios does ERP cover?
- What scenarios does BCP cover?
- Is there clear boundary between them?
- Are there gaps in scenario coverage?

**Assess:**
- Scope definition in each plan
- Trigger events for each plan
- Handoff points between plans

#### 2. Roles & Responsibilities
**Questions:**
- Are emergency response roles clear?
- Are business continuity roles clear?
- Is there overlap in responsibilities?
- Are there gaps in coverage?

**Assess:**
- Organizational structure in each plan
- Role definitions and authorities
- Chain of command
- Decision-making processes

#### 3. Procedures & Protocols
**Questions:**
- What procedures are in ERP?
- What procedures are in BCP?
- Are there duplicates?
- Are there gaps?
- Do procedures conflict?

**Assess:**
- Step-by-step procedures
- Decision criteria
- Communication protocols
- Resource allocation processes

#### 4. Resources & Assets
**Questions:**
- What resources does ERP identify?
- What resources does BCP identify?
- Are lists consistent and complete?
- Are there redundancies or gaps?

**Assess:**
- Emergency contact lists
- Equipment and supplies
- Facilities and locations
- External resources

#### 5. Communication
**Questions:**
- How does ERP handle communication?
- How does BCP handle communication?
- Are communication plans aligned?
- Are there gaps or inconsistencies?

**Assess:**
- Internal communication protocols
- External communication procedures
- Stakeholder notification processes
- Media/public relations approach

#### 6. Training & Testing
**Questions:**
- What training does ERP require?
- What training does BCP require?
- How are drills/exercises conducted?
- Are requirements aligned?

**Assess:**
- Training requirements
- Drill frequencies
- Exercise scenarios
- Documentation practices

#### 7. Maintenance & Updates
**Questions:**
- How is ERP maintained?
- How is BCP maintained?
- Who owns updates?
- How often are plans reviewed?

**Assess:**
- Update processes
- Ownership and accountability
