# ERP vs BCP Gap Analysis

## Overview
Systematic comparison of the organization's Emergency Response Plan (ERP) and Business Continuity Plan (BCP) to identify gaps, overlaps, inconsistencies, and opportunities for alignment.

**Project:** [[Project_Summary]]  
**Related:** [[ERP_Revamp]] - Will use findings to update ERP

---

## Purpose

### Why This Analysis Matters
**Problem:** ERP and BCP may have:
- **Gaps:** Important elements missing
- **Overlaps:** Duplicated content creating confusion
- **Inconsistencies:** Conflicting information
- **Unclear boundaries:** When to use ERP vs BCP

**Solution:** Systematic gap analysis to:
- Identify all gaps, overlaps, and inconsistencies
- Provide recommendations for alignment
- Inform [[ERP_Revamp]] updates
- Ensure seamless transition from emergency response to business continuity

---

## Analysis Framework

### Dimensions to Analyze

1. **Scope & Purpose** - What scenarios each plan covers
2. **Roles & Responsibilities** - Emergency response and continuity roles
3. **Procedures & Protocols** - Step-by-step processes
4. **Resources & Assets** - Contact lists, equipment, facilities
5. **Communication** - Internal and external protocols
6. **Training & Testing** - Requirements and drills
7. **Maintenance & Updates** - Update processes and ownership

---

## Analysis Methodology

### Step 1: Document Review
Review current ERP and BCP, extract key elements, create comparison matrix.

### Step 2: Gap Identification
Compare element by element, identify gaps, overlaps, and inconsistencies.

### Step 3: Stakeholder Input
Interview key stakeholders, validate findings, prioritize issues.

### Step 4: Recommendations
Develop recommendations for each gap, propose alignment strategy.

### Step 5: Executive Review
Present findings to [[Donna]] and [[Jesse]], get approval to proceed.
