# Administrative Procedures (AP) Updates - Project Summary

## Quick Overview

### What
Comprehensive review and update of organizational Administrative Procedures (APs), with focus on:
- Emergency Response Plan (ERP) revamp
- Business Continuity Plan (BCP) alignment
- Elimination of duplicate procedures
- ISO compliance

### Why
- **Eliminate confusion:** Multiple duplicate APs create inconsistency
- **Ensure compliance:** Align with ISO standards
- **Improve effectiveness:** Streamlined, clear procedures
- **Support operations:** Better emergency and business continuity planning

### Who
- **Sponsor:** [[Donna]] - EHS Organizational Leader
- **Project Lead:** [[Jesse]] - Construction EHS Executive
- **Stakeholders:** EHS leadership, operations teams, compliance
