## ERP Revamp Scope

### What We're Updating
1. **Current ERP Content**
   - Review existing Emergency Response Plan
   - Identify gaps from [[ERP_vs_BCP_Gap_Analysis]]
   - Update based on gap analysis recommendations

2. **Duplicate Procedure Consolidation**
   - Eliminate duplicate emergency response procedures
   - Consolidate into single, authoritative ERP
   - Clear references from deprecated procedures

3. **BCP Alignment**
   - Ensure ERP aligns with Business Continuity Plan
   - Address gaps and overlaps
   - Create clear handoff between emergency response and continuity

4. **ISO Compliance**
   - Reference: [[ISO_Clause-Go-by]]
   - Ensure ERP meets all applicable ISO requirements
   - Document compliance mapping
