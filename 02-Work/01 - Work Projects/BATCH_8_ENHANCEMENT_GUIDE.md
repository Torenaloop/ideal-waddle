## PARA Classification Decisions

### Why "Areas" for EHS Goals?
These are **ongoing responsibilities**, not temporary projects:
- No specific end date (annual cycle, but continues yearly)
- Standard of performance to maintain indefinitely
- Part of role definition, not special initiative
- Reviewed and updated annually but never "complete"

Alternative considered: "Resources" (reference documents)
**Rejected because**: These aren't just reference—they're active performance management tools used in reviews, 1:1s, and planning.

### Why "Resources" for Ecosystem Replication?
This is **reference material** about a system:
- Documents methodology, not work in progress
- Explains completed migration and ongoing practices
- Referenced when onboarding others or refining system
- No deadline or completion state (ongoing maintenance)

Alternative considered: "Projects" (migration work)
**Rejected because**: The migration is complete; this documents the result and ongoing use.

## Goal Cascading Structure

### Organizational Hierarchy
```
Leadership (SVP/VP/Sr.Dir/Director)
    ↓ Sets vision, standards, allocates resources
Critical Operations Managers (COMs)
    ↓ Implements at sites, manages teams
ACOM & COT (Individual Contributors)
    ↓ Executes safely, participates actively
```

### How Same Goals Cascade Differently

**Example: Incident Notification**

| Level | "Meets" Standard | Accountability |
|---|---|---|
| **Leadership** | Ensure 24-hour notification | Systems work across organization |
| **COMs** | 24-hour notification + clinic referral | Team follows procedure |
| **ACOM/COT** | 24-hour notification + clinic visit | Personal compliance |

**Example: SOSPES Observations**

| Level | "Outstanding" Performance |
|---|---|
| **Leadership** | Monthly observations + team participation |
| **COMs** | Monthly observations + team participation |
| **ACOM/COT** | Monthly observations |

**Note**: Same "Meets" standard (2 observations) for all levels demonstrates "safety is everyone's job." Outstanding requires monthly cadence, showing cultural leadership.

## Key Metrics & Definitions

### EHS Acronyms Explained
- **EHS**: Environmental, Health, and Safety
- **COM**: Critical Operations Manager (site-level leadership)
- **ACOM**: Assistant Critical Operations Manager
- **COT**: Critical Operations Technician
- **NOV**: Notice of Violation (regulatory enforcement action)
- **SOSPES**: STACK's Safety Observation Program and Engagement System
- **EQW**: Electrical Qualified Worker (specialized training for data center electrical work)
- **LMS**: Learning Management System
- **Occ Health Clinic**: Occupational Health Clinic (designated facility for work injuries)
- **Q1**: First Quarter (January-March)

### Performance Scale
1. **Below**: Significantly below expectations; requires immediate improvement
2. **Nearly Met**: Close to expectations but key gaps remain
3. **Meets**: Fully meets role expectations and performance standards
4. **Exceeded**: Consistently exceeds expectations; demonstrates excellence
5. **Outstanding**: Far exceeds expectations; best-in-class; models for others

## Strategic Context

### Bridge Construction-Ops EHS Gap Initiative
**The Challenge**: 
STACK's construction EHS has historically been strong; operations EHS is developing but not yet at same maturity.

**How EHS Goals Address This**:
1. **Codify Excellence**: Define what "good" looks like at each level
2. **Consistent Standards**: Same criteria across all sites (no variability)
3. **Cultural Emphasis**: Beyond compliance to engagement (observations, reporting, participation)
4. **Accountability**: Clear ownership at each level
5. **Measurement**: Objective tracking of progress

**Goal**: Elevate Operations EHS to match Construction EHS excellence.

---

### 2025 Environmental Audit Project
**Direct Connection**:
- **Pre-Audit**: Goals drive preparation (training, procedures, documentation)
- **During Audit**: Goals establish expectations for participation
- **Post-Audit**: Goals require rapid closure of findings

**Performance Metrics**:
- COMs: 25-50% Q1 closure = Below; 100% Q1 closure = Outstanding
- Leadership: 100% Q1 closure required for Outstanding rating
- ACOM/COT: Follow corrective actions from audit in daily work

**The Audit Enables Goal Achievement**: Findings become the work that demonstrates Outstanding performance.

## Implementation Guidance

### For Performance Reviews

**Quarterly Check-Ins**:
1. Self-assess against relevant scorecard
2. Identify 2-3 focus areas for improvement
3. Develop action plans with specific steps
4. Track progress on actions
5. Celebrate wins ("Exceeded" or "Outstanding" areas)

**Annual Reviews**:
1. Complete comprehensive self-assessment
2. Gather supporting evidence:
   - Observation records from SOSPES
   - Training completion certificates
   - Incident reports and investigations
   - Environmental audit closure documentation
3. Calculate overall performance rating
4. Meet with supervisor to calibrate
5. Set goals for next year

---

### For Goal Setting

**Top-Down Cascade**:
1. **Leadership**: Set strategic EHS vision and direction
2. **Translation**: Goals translate vision to measurable outcomes
3. **COMs**: Develop site-specific action plans
4. **ACOM/COT**: Understand personal contribution

**Example Cascade**:
- **Vision**: "Safety is our #1 priority; everyone goes home safely"
- **Goal**: "Zero NOVs + controls integrated into procedures"
- **COM Plan**: "Review 2024 NOVs, embed controls in SOPs, train team Q1"
- **Individual**: "Complete EHS Learning Path, follow updated SOPs, report gaps"

## Common Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge: "Goals feel overwhelming"
**Symptom**: Unclear where to start
**Solution**: Focus on "Meets" level first, not "Outstanding"
- **Year 1**: Establish "Meets" performance
- **Year 2**: Target "Exceeded" in 2-3 areas
- **Year 3+**: Pursue "Outstanding" selectively

---

### Challenge: "How do I track all this?"
**Symptom**: Manual tracking burden
**Solution**: Build into existing workflows
- Monthly EHS report already captures many metrics
- SOSPES system tracks observations automatically
- LMS tracks training completion
- Incident system tracks notification timing
- Only need to aggregate and review

---

### Challenge: "Too much in my Obsidian vault"
**Symptom**: Can't find anything, feels cluttered
**Solution**: Leverage ecosystem replication principles
- Use tags to filter (`#active` vs `#complete`)
- Create MOCs (Maps of Content) for major areas
- Use daily notes as action dashboard
- Archive completed projects regularly
- Trust search - don't over-organize

---

### Challenge: "Goals don't reflect my actual work"
**Symptom**: Disconnect between goals and daily reality
**Solution**: Integrate goals into regular rhythms
- Reference in 1:1 meetings
- Include in monthly EHS site reviews
- Tie corrective actions to goal criteria
- Celebrate when team demonstrates goal behaviors
- Make goals visible (dashboards, posters, meetings)
