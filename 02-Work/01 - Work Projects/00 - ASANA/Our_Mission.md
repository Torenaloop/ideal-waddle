# Our Mission

## Overview
Our organizational mission is built on four core values that guide everything we do across both Operations and Construction EHS. These values shape our culture, inform our decisions, and define how we work together.

---

## Our Four Core Values

### 1. Partnership
**Core Value:** [[Partnership]]

**Focus:** Collaboration, teamwork, breaking down silos  
**Key Principle:** Strong partnerships enable organizational success

**2025 Strategic Alignment:**
- **Organizational Goal:** [[2025_EHS_Org_Goal_4]] - Improve Internal Communications
- **Strategic Theme:** [[Improve_Internal_Communications]]
- **Executive Leads:** Jesse (Construction) & Kate (Operations)

**Why It Matters:**
Partnership is critical for bridging Construction and Operations EHS teams, enabling cross-functional collaboration, and building trust across the organization.

---

### 2. Visible
**Core Value:** [[Visible]]

**Focus:** Leadership presence, accessibility, authentic engagement  
**Key Principle:** Visibility builds trust and connection

**2025 Strategic Alignment:**
- **Organizational Goal:** [[2025_EHS_Org_Goal_2]] - Employee Engagement
- **Strategic Theme:** [[Employee_Engagement]]
- **Executive Lead:** Kate

**Why It Matters:**
Visible leadership creates cultures where employees feel seen, valued, and connected to the organization and its mission.

---

### 3. Culture Carrier
**Core Value:** [[Culture_Carrier]]

**Focus:** Embodying and transmitting organizational culture  
**Key Principle:** Every person carries and shapes our culture

**2025 Strategic Alignment:**
- **Organizational Goal:** [[2025_EHS_Org_Goal_1]] - Vendor Management
- **Strategic Theme:** [[Vendor_Management]]

**Why It Matters:**
Culture carriers ensure that our values extend beyond our organization to partners, vendors, and everyone we work with.

---

### 4. Be of Service to Others
**Core Value:** [[Be_of_Service_to_Others]]

**Focus:** Servant leadership, supporting teams, creating value for others  
**Key Principle:** We exist to serve others and make their work better

**2025 Strategic Alignment:**
- **Organizational Goal:** [[2025_EHS_Org_Goal_3]] - Leveraging Technology for Continuous Improvement
- **Strategic Theme:** [[Leveraging_Tech_for_Continuous_Improvement]]

**Why It Matters:**
A service mindset ensures that all our work - especially technology implementations - genuinely helps teams rather than creating burden.

---

## How These Values Work Together

### Interconnected System
Our four values don't stand alone - they reinforce and strengthen each other:

- **Partnership + Visible** = Leaders who are present and collaborative
- **Visible + Be of Service** = Accessible leaders who support teams
- **Be of Service + Culture Carrier** = Service-minded culture throughout organization
- **Culture Carrier + Partnership** = Cultural values extend to all partnerships
- **Partnership + Be of Service** = Collaborative approach to serving others
- **Visible + Culture Carrier** = Leadership that models cultural values

### Living All Four Values
Strong EHS organizations demonstrate all four values simultaneously:
- **Partnership** in how we work together
- **Visible** in how leaders engage
- **Culture Carrier** in how we model values
- **Be of Service** in why we do the work
