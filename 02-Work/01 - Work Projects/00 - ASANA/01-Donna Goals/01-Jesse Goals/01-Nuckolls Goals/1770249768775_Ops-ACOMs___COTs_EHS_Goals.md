# 2025 Operations EHS Goals - ACOM & COT

## Overview
Detailed EHS goals and objectives for Assistant Critical Operations Managers (ACOMs) and Critical Operations Technicians (COTs) for 2025.

## Parent Goal
[[Nuckolls 2025 Goal 1]] - EHS Goals for ACOM & COT staff

## Target Roles

### ACOMs - Assistant Critical Operations Managers
- Site leadership support role
- Tactical operations focus
- Direct supervision of COTs
- Safety culture champions

### COTs - Critical Operations Technicians
- Frontline technical staff
- Equipment maintenance and monitoring
- Daily safety observations
- First responders for incidents

## 2025 Goals Framework

### Goal Category 1: Safety Performance
**Objectives:**
- [ ] Zero recordable injuries
- [ ] Increase near-miss reporting by 25%
- [ ] Complete 100% of safety observations
- [ ] Achieve 95%+ safety training completion

**Metrics:**
- TRIR (Target: 0.0)
- Near-miss reports per month
- Safety observation completion rate
- Training compliance percentage

### Goal Category 2: Environmental Compliance
**Objectives:**
- [ ] Maintain all environmental permits
- [ ] Complete monthly environmental inspections
- [ ] Zero environmental violations
- [ ] Proper waste segregation and documentation

**Metrics:**
- Permit compliance rate (Target: 100%)
- Inspection completion (Target: 100%)
- Violations (Target: 0)
- Waste audit scores

### Goal Category 3: Training & Development
**Objectives:**
- [ ] Complete required EHS training on time
- [ ] Attend quarterly safety meetings
- [ ] Participate in emergency drills
- [ ] Complete role-specific certifications

**Metrics:**
- Training completion rate
- Meeting attendance
- Drill participation
- Certification status

### Goal Category 4: Communication & Reporting
**Objectives:**
- [ ] Report incidents within 1 hour
- [ ] Submit weekly safety reports
- [ ] Escalate concerns promptly
- [ ] Participate in safety committees

**Metrics:**
- Incident reporting timeliness
- Report submission rate
- Issue escalation tracking
- Committee participation

### Goal Category 5: Housekeeping & Organization
**Objectives:**
- [ ] Maintain clean work areas
- [ ] Proper equipment storage
- [ ] Tool and equipment inspections
- [ ] Follow 5S principles

**Metrics:**
- Housekeeping audit scores
- Storage compliance
- Inspection completion
- 5S audit results

## Performance Expectations

### ACOM-Specific
- Leadership in safety culture
- Mentoring COTs on EHS practices
- Conducting site inspections
- Investigating incidents
- Coordinating training

### COT-Specific
- Following all safety procedures
- Completing daily safety checks
- Reporting hazards immediately
- Using PPE correctly
- Maintaining work area cleanliness
