- **Date:** July 22, 2025
- **Meeting Type:** Microsoft Teams
- **Project:** NVA06A
- **Participants:**
  - Lauren Dalton
  - Terry DeBell

## Purpose
Thorough review of:
1. **NVA06A-EXP-DEV-1166_D2D Checklist.xlsx**
2. **STACK Facility and Client Guide (NVA06A)-2025.07.11.docx**

**Goal:** Evaluate whether the Word document is redundant to the Excel file.

---

## Key Purpose Comparison

| **Aspect**       | **Excel Checklist (D2D)**                                   | **Word Facility & Client Guide**                                     |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Format**       | Structured checklist with tasks and status columns          | Narrative guide with policies, procedures, and contacts              |
| **Intended Use** | Internal site readiness or deployment checklist for staff   | External-facing client orientation document                          |
| **Scope**        | Focused on operational tasks and facility prep              | Focused on rules, safety, security, contacts, ERP, client onboarding |
| **Audience**     | Internal operations, project, or critical systems teams     | Clients, vendors, security, and admin teams                          |
| **Content Type** | Line items for validation (e.g., signage, cable management) | Policy details, procedures, emergency contacts, badge protocols      |

---

## Content Overlap Analysis

### Finding: Some Overlap Exists
There is **some overlap** in topics (e.g., safety, access control, emergency contacts), but:

- **Excel file:** More **action/task-oriented** 
  - Checklist confirming readiness of physical or procedural items for the facility
  
- **Word guide:** **Reference/resource document**
  - Educates and informs clients about facility use, protocols, and expectations

---

## Conclusion 1: Not Redundant, But Complementary

### Assessment
The **Word document might appear redundant** due to topical overlap (security, emergency, access), but in function and audience, they **serve distinct purposes**.

### Recommendations

**If evaluating for consolidation or simplification:**
The documents could be **linked**:
- Example: Have the Excel checklist reference applicable sections of the Word document for guidance
- E.g., "Refer to ERP map in Client Guide – Appendix C"

**Important Note:**
One **should not replace** the other without losing important operational or client-facing context.

## EHS Tab Deep Dive

### Evaluation: EHS Tab vs. Word Guide

| **EHS Excel Deliverable** | **Overlap in Word Guide?** | **Details from Word Document** |
|---|---|---|
| **Air Permit** | ❌ Not Present | No mention of air permits or environmental permitting responsibilities |
| **State Specific OSHA Plan Requirements** | 🟨 Partial | General safety policies mentioned (PPE, signage, STOP Work authority) but no reference to specific state OSHA |
| **Tier II Reporting** | ❌ Not Present | No reference to Tier II, EPCRA, or hazardous chemical reporting obligations |
| **Chemical Inventory** | ❌ Not Present | No inventory procedures or management expectations outlined in the guide |
| **Confined Space Inventory and Signage** | 🟨 Partial | Confined space entry must be approved by Critical Ops, but no inventory or signage requirements are stated |
| **Fall Hazard Assessment** | ❌ Not Present | No explicit mention of fall protection or assessments in the Word guide |
| **Emergency Response Plan and Maps** | ✅ Present | The ERP and shelter/muster points are referenced, and maps are included in Appendix C |

---

## Key Observations

- **Only 1 out of 7** deliverables from the EHS tab (**Emergency Response Plan and Maps**) is fully represented in the Word document

- **Partial overlaps** exist for OSHA, confined space, and general safety responsibilities, but the Excel file demands a higher level of specificity (e.g., compliance validation, inventories, due dates)

- The **Excel checklist is compliance-driven and action-oriented**
- The **Word guide is policy- and communication-driven**

---

## Conclusion 2: Low Redundancy in EHS Tab

### Finding
The **EHS tab in the Excel checklist is not redundant** to the content in the Word document.

### Purpose of EHS Tab
- Serves as a **compliance tracker** for regulatory requirements and internal audits
- Addresses specific environmental deliverables not covered in the Word doc
- Should remain a standalone document or be integrated into a broader compliance management system (e.g., Smartsheet, Asana)
