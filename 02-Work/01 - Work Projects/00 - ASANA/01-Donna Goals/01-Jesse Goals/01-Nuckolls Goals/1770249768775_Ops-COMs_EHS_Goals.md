# 2025 Operations EHS Goals - COMs

## Overview
Detailed EHS leadership goals and objectives for Critical Operations Managers (COMs) for 2025.

## Parent Goal
[[Nuckolls 2025 Goal 2]] - EHS Goals for COM leadership

## Target Role

### COMs - Critical Operations Managers
- Site leadership position
- Strategic operations oversight
- Team management and development
- Client relationship management
- Financial and operational accountability

## 2025 Leadership Goals Framework

### Goal Category 1: Safety Leadership
**Objectives:**
- [ ] Establish strong safety culture at site
- [ ] Conduct monthly safety walks
- [ ] Lead incident investigations
- [ ] Champion behavioral-based safety
- [ ] Achieve site TRIR target

**Metrics:**
- Site TRIR (Target: <0.5)
- Safety walk completion (Target: 12/year)
- Investigation completion timeliness
- Safety culture survey scores
- Leading indicator trends

**Leadership Behaviors:**
- Visible commitment to safety
- Stop work authority demonstration
- Recognition of safe behaviors
- Accountability for performance

### Goal Category 2: Environmental Stewardship
**Objectives:**
- [ ] Ensure all permits current and compliant
- [ ] Oversee environmental audit preparations
- [ ] Zero environmental violations
- [ ] Implement sustainability initiatives
- [ ] Manage environmental risks

**Metrics:**
- Permit compliance (Target: 100%)
- Audit findings (Target: 0 critical)
- Violations (Target: 0)
- Sustainability KPIs
- Risk assessment completion

**Leadership Behaviors:**
- Environmental compliance oversight
- Resource allocation for compliance
- Stakeholder communication
- Continuous improvement mindset

### Goal Category 3: Team Development
**Objectives:**
- [ ] Ensure team EHS training compliance
- [ ] Develop safety competency in staff
- [ ] Mentor ACOMs on EHS leadership
- [ ] Build high-performing safety culture
- [ ] Succession planning for key roles

**Metrics:**
- Team training compliance (Target: 100%)
- ACOM development progress
- Safety competency assessments
- Culture survey improvements
- Succession plan completion

**Leadership Behaviors:**
- Coaching and mentoring
- Performance feedback
- Career development support
- Recognition and accountability

### Goal Category 4: Strategic EHS Planning
**Objectives:**
- [ ] Develop site-specific EHS plan
- [ ] Allocate budget for EHS initiatives
- [ ] Integrate EHS into operations
- [ ] Participate in company EHS initiatives
- [ ] Benchmark against industry standards

**Metrics:**
- EHS plan completion
- Budget utilization
- Initiative implementation rate
- Program participation
- Benchmark comparisons

**Leadership Behaviors:**
- Strategic thinking
- Resource management
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Innovation and improvement

### Goal Category 5: Communication & Engagement
**Objectives:**
- [ ] Monthly safety communications to team
- [ ] Quarterly EHS updates to leadership
- [ ] Transparent incident reporting
- [ ] Active participation in safety committees
- [ ] Client EHS engagement

**Metrics:**
- Communication frequency
- Leadership update completion
- Reporting timeliness and accuracy
- Committee participation
- Client satisfaction with EHS

**Leadership Behaviors:**
- Clear and frequent communication
- Transparency and honesty
- Stakeholder engagement
- Collaborative problem-solving

## Performance Expectations

### Strategic Responsibilities
- Setting site EHS vision and strategy
- Allocating resources for EHS
- Holding team accountable
- Managing EHS risks
- Representing site to corporate EHS

### Operational Responsibilities
- Overseeing daily EHS compliance
- Ensuring adequate staffing and training
- Managing incidents and emergencies
- Coordinating with corporate EHS team
- Implementing EHS programs

## Differentiation from ACOM/COT Goals
- **COMs:** Strategic leadership, resource allocation, culture setting
- **ACOMs:** Tactical leadership, daily operations, mentoring COTs
- **COTs:** Execution, compliance, reporting

## Notes
- COM goals are higher-level and strategic
- COMs are accountable for their entire site's EHS performance
- Goals cascade down to ACOM and COT levels
- Integration with performance management and compensation
