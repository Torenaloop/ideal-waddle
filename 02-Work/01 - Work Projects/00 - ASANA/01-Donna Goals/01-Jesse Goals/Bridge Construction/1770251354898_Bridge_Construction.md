## Why This Matters

### The Problem
- **Handoff Challenges:** Information gaps during Construction-to-Operations transition
- **Communication Breakdown:** Siloed teams, different priorities
- **Compliance Risks:** Safety and environmental gaps post-handover
- **Inefficiencies:** Rework, delays, quality issues

### The Solution
- **Systematic Bridge:** Structured communication and process integration
- **Technology Enablement:** Shared systems and visibility
- **Cultural Change:** Collaboration over silos
- **Process Excellence:** D2D methodology

### The Impact
- Safer facilities
- Smoother operations
- Better client experience
- Team collaboration
- Reduced costs
