# Bridge Construction-Ops EHS Gap

## Strategic Goal Overview
Close the gap between Construction EHS and Operations EHS teams to ensure smooth project handovers, improve communication, and enhance overall safety and compliance.

## Parent Company Goal
[[Improve Internal Communications]] - This goal is a key component of the broader internal communication initiative.

## Strategic Importance
- **Problem:** Handoff challenges between Construction and Operations phases
- **Impact:** Safety risks, compliance gaps, operational inefficiencies
- **Solution:** Systematic bridge-building through multiple initiatives
- **Outcome:** Seamless transitions, better collaboration, improved performance

## Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge 1: Different Cultures
**Issue:** Construction vs Operations mindset differences
**Solution:** Joint training, shared goals, cross-team experiences

### Challenge 2: Timeline Pressures
**Issue:** Construction deadline pressure affects handover quality
**Solution:** Quality gates, executive accountability, D2D checklist

### Challenge 3: Information Silos
**Issue:** Documentation gaps, knowledge loss
**Solution:** Shared systems (Goal 5), communication protocols (Goal 3)

### Challenge 4: Role Clarity
**Issue:** Unclear responsibilities during transition
**Solution:** RACI matrices, documented handover process
