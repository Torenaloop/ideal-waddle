# Asana Training

## Overview
Comprehensive learning path for Asana adoption and mastery.

**Hub:** [[00_-_ASANA]] - Main Asana resource center

## Training Philosophy

### Focus Areas
Per Asana Academy, prioritize **non-AI courses** initially since AI features may not be immediately available. AI-related training should wait until closer to adoption of that functionality.

**Priority:** Foundations first, then specialized certifications.

## Core Certification (FREE)

### Asana Foundations Skill Badge
**Resource:** [[Asana_Foundations_Skill_Badge]]

**Details:**
- **Time Commitment:** 2-3 hours
- **Cost:** FREE
- **Format:** Lessons + quiz
- **Audience:** All Asana users (essential foundation)

**What You'll Learn:**
- Asana basics and hierarchy
- Project planning essentials
- Team communication strategies
- Personal productivity techniques

**Status:** Recommended immediate priority  
**Value:** Required foundation for effective Asana use

---

## Advanced Certification (Paid - Currently FREE)

### Workflow Specialist Certificate
**Resource:** [[Workflow_Specialist_Certificate]]

**Details:**
- **Time Commitment:** 8-10 hours
- **Cost:** $299 USD (Currently FREE for limited time!)
- **Format:** 2 courses + hands-on projects + 60-question exam (80% passing)
- **Completion Window:** 90 days after registration
- **Audience:** Team leaders, project/program managers, workflow builders

**What You'll Learn:**
- Design impactful workflows
- Apply automations
- Create dashboards and measure success
- Research-backed change management strategies
- Drive team adoption

**Components:**
1. **Course 1:** Lessons - Workflow Specialist Certificate (5 hrs)
2. **Course 2:** Exam - Workflow Specialist Certificate (3 hrs)
   - 3 hands-on projects
   - 60-question final exam

**Prerequisites:** [[Asana_Foundations_Skill_Badge]] recommended first

**Status:** Future goal - currently FREE offer available  
**Strategic Value:** Critical for organizational workflow adoption
