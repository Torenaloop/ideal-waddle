## What Asana AI Can Do

**Your Role:** Regional EHS Manager  
**Focus:** Project management and EHS coordination

Asana AI serves as your intelligent assistant to help manage work more efficiently.

---

## Relevant Use Cases for EHS Management

### 1. Project and Portfolio Management
Track and organize your EHS projects with AI assistance.

**Example Query:**  
*"Show me all my overdue tasks in my safety compliance projects."*

**Value:**
- Quick project status overview
- Identify at-risk items
- Prioritize attention

### 2. Status Reporting and Workload Management
Get project updates and balance workloads across team.

**Example Query:**  
*"What's the status of our current safety training initiatives?"*

**Value:**
- Real-time status visibility
- Workload balancing
- Resource allocation insights

### 3. Resource Planning and Goal Tracking
Assist with resource allocation and monitoring progress toward goals.

**Example Query:**  
*"Help me create a task for our quarterly safety audit."*

**Value:**
- Efficient task creation
- Resource planning support
- Goal progress tracking

## Using Asana AI Effectively

### Important Tip: @-Mentions
When asking about specific tasks, projects, or people, use [@-mentions](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/14142327738779) to help AI find exactly what you're looking for.

**Example:**  
- *"What's the status of @Project: Safety Training Program?"*
- *"Show me all tasks assigned to @John Smith that are overdue"*

**Value:** More precise, accurate results
