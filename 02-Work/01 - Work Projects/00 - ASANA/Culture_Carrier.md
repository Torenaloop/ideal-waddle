# Culture Carrier

## Value Statement
**Culture Carrier** means that every person embodies, transmits, and reinforces our organizational culture. Culture carriers understand that culture isn't just what happens inside our organization - it extends to every interaction, partner, and relationship.

---

## Why Culture Carrier Matters

### Organizational Impact
- **Extends culture** beyond organizational boundaries
- **Reinforces values** through consistent behavior
- **Builds reputation** through every interaction
- **Attracts talent** who align with values
- **Strengthens partnerships** with culturally-aligned vendors and partners

### Strategic Context
In EHS work, being a culture carrier is essential because:
- Safety culture must be consistent everywhere
- Partners and vendors reflect on our organization
- Every interaction shapes how others see us
- Cultural alignment with external partners matters for safety
- Strong culture is competitive advantage

## What Culture Carrier Looks Like

### In Every Interaction
**Culture Carriers:**
- **Model values consistently** - Not just when convenient
- **Represent organization well** - Every interaction matters
- **Set expectations clearly** - Help others understand what we value
- **Reinforce culture** - Recognize and reward cultural alignment
- **Correct misalignment** - Address behaviors that conflict with values
- **Tell culture stories** - Share examples of values in action

**Example Behaviors:**
- Treating all people with respect, regardless of role
- Making safe choices even when no one is watching
- Speaking about the organization positively
- Helping new people understand "how we do things"
- Calling out behaviors that conflict with values

### With Vendors & Partners
**Culture Carriers:**
- **Share our values** - Help vendors understand what matters to us
- **Set clear expectations** - Culture is part of the contract
- **Evaluate alignment** - Assess vendors on cultural fit, not just capability
- **Provide feedback** - Help vendors align with our culture
- **Recognize alignment** - Appreciate vendors who embody our values
- **Address gaps** - Work with or remove vendors who don't fit

**Example Behaviors:**
- Including culture in vendor selection criteria
- Onboarding vendors to our values and expectations
- Regular feedback on cultural alignment
- Recognition of vendors who model our values
- Decisions that prioritize cultural fit

### With New Team Members
**Culture Carriers:**
- **Welcome and orient** - Help new people understand culture
- **Model behaviors** - Show what our values look like in practice
- **Share stories** - Illustrate culture through examples
- **Answer questions** - Help new people navigate cultural norms
- **Provide feedback** - Help new people align with culture
- **Be patient** - Cultural learning takes time

## Culture Carrying in Practice

### Vendor Cultural Alignment
**The Practice:** Ensuring vendors understand and embody our values

**What It Includes:**
- **Selection:** Cultural fit is part of vendor evaluation
- **Onboarding:** New vendors learn our values and expectations
- **Performance:** Cultural alignment is measured and discussed
- **Recognition:** Vendors who embody values are appreciated
- **Addressing gaps:** Vendors who don't fit are coached or replaced

**2025 Focus:** [[2025_EHS_Org_Goal_1]] - Vendor Management initiative

### Daily Culture Reinforcement
**The Practice:** Everyday actions that strengthen culture

**What It Includes:**
- **Recognition:** Calling out values-aligned behaviors
- **Storytelling:** Sharing examples of culture in action
- **Feedback:** Addressing behaviors that conflict with values
- **Decision-making:** Using values as decision criteria
- **Consistency:** Modeling values even when difficult
