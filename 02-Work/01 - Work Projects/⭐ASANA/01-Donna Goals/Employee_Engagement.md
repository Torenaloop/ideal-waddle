# Employee Engagement

## Overview
Employee Engagement is one of our four organizational strategic themes for 2025, focused on creating meaningful connection and visibility across the EHS organization.

## Core Value
**Points to Value:** [[Visible]]

Leadership and teams are visible, accessible, and authentically engaged with employees at all levels.

## 2025 Strategic Goal
**Organizational Goal:** [[2025_EHS_Org_Goal_2]]

This theme drives our organization-wide effort to increase employee engagement through visible leadership and meaningful connection.

## Strategic Framework

### Why It Matters
- Engaged employees are safer employees
- Visibility builds trust and connection
- Strong engagement drives performance and retention
- Culture of care and connection

### Key Focus Areas
- Leadership visibility and accessibility
- Two-way communication
- Recognition and appreciation
- Team member voice and input
- Cross-functional connections
