# Improve Internal Communications

## Overview
Internal Communications is one of our four organizational strategic themes for 2025, focused on building partnership and breaking down silos between Construction and Operations EHS.

## Core Value
**Points to Value:** [[Partnership]]

Strong partnerships require clear, consistent, and collaborative communication across teams and functions.

## 2025 Strategic Goal
**Organizational Goal:** [[2025_EHS_Org_Goal_4]]

This theme drives our organization-wide effort to enhance communication and build genuine partnership between Construction and Operations EHS teams.

## Strategic Framework

### Why It Matters
- Siloed teams miss opportunities for collaboration
- Poor communication creates inefficiencies and gaps
- Strong internal communication drives safety performance
- Partnership enables organizational agility

### Key Focus Areas
- Cross-functional information sharing
- Breaking down Construction-Operations silos
- Consistent messaging and alignment
- Collaborative problem-solving
- Technology-enabled communication
