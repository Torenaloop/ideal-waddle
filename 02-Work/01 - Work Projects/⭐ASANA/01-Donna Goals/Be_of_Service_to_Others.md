# Be of Service to Others

## Overview
**Be of Service to Others** is a core organizational value that guides how we approach our work, our teams, and our stakeholders.

## Value Statement
We exist to serve others - our teams, our organization, our communities. Our work has meaning when it makes others safer, more effective, and more successful.

## 2025 Strategic Alignment

### Organizational Goal
**Primary Goal:** [[2025_EHS_Org_Goal_3]] - Leveraging Technology for Continuous Improvement

**Strategic Theme:** [[Leveraging_Tech_for_Continuous_Improvement]]

This value drives our approach to technology: tools and systems should serve people, not the other way around.

## Living This Value

### What It Means
- Put others' needs before bureaucracy
- Design processes that serve field teams
- Lead with a servant leadership mindset
- Measure success by the value we provide others
- Ask "How does this serve our teams?"

### What It Looks Like
- Technology that makes work easier and safer
- Responsive support when teams need help
- Solutions designed with end-users in mind
- Leaders who remove obstacles for their teams
- Continuous improvement based on team feedback
