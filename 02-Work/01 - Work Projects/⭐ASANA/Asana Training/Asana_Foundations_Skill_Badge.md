# Asana Foundations Skill Badge

## Overview
Essential foundation certification for all Asana users. FREE certification covering Asana basics, project planning, team communication, and personal productivity.

**Hub:** [[00_-_ASANA]]  
**Learning Path:** [[Asana_Training]]

## Certification Details

### Time & Cost
- **Time Commitment:** 2-3 hours
- **Cost:** FREE
- **Format:** Self-paced lessons + quiz
- **Certificate:** Asana Foundations Skill Badge upon completion

### Prerequisites
- None (entry-level)
- Recommended: Complete [[Getting_Started_with_Asana]] and [[Personal_Productivity_in_Asana]] first

### Audience
- **All Asana users** - This is foundational for everyone
- New collaborative work management (CWM) users
- Anyone planning to use Asana for project or task management

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Enable Asana AI (if available)
1. Follow [Asana Help Center steps](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/17035682875291)
2. Features available immediately after enabling
3. Can use AI while taking course

### Step 2: Register for Course
1. Visit [Asana Academy](https://academy.asana.com)
2. Find "Asana Foundations Skill Badge"
3. Register (FREE)
4. Begin self-paced learning

### Step 3: Complete Lessons
- Work through all modules at your own pace
- Estimated 2-3 hours total
- Take notes on key concepts
- Practice in your workspace as you learn

### Step 4: Pass Quiz
- Complete assessment quiz
- Demonstrate understanding of foundations
- Earn Asana Foundations Skill Badge
