## What is Asana?

### Collaborative Work Management (CWM) Platform
Asana is a collaborative work management platform designed to help teams organize, track, and manage their work.

**Key Concept:** Asana connects tools, doesn't replace them.

### Why CWM Matters

#### Problems CWM Solves
- **LACK OF CLARITY** - Teams don't know what to work on or why
- **WORK IN SILOS** - No visibility into what others are doing
- **WASTED TIME** - Searching for information, duplicated efforts

#### The CWM Solution
Implementing a CWM platform means that **data flows directly from the tasks of individual contributors to the goals of leadership** to inform real-time decision making and risk management.

**Value:** Single source of truth connecting individual work to organizational goals.

## Training Highlights

### Problem Statement & Value Proposition
**Key Message:** Asana connects tools, not replace them.

### Core Concepts

#### Task Management Basics
- Each line is a task
- Additional details associated with each task:
  - Properties
  - Links
  - Attachments
- Commenting and '@-ing' people
- For larger tasks, use sub-tasks

#### Multi-Select & Bulk Actions
**How to multi-select:**
1. Select the 6 dots on left side of task
2. Press and hold Shift
3. Drag down to select multiple tasks

**Use for:** Bulk editing, moving, assigning tasks

#### Assign Copy Function
Copy tasks with all details and settings to quickly duplicate work.

**Use case:** Templates, recurring work patterns

---

## Customization Features

### Custom Fields
Add custom properties to tasks for your specific needs.

**Example:** Priority, Status, Department, etc.

**Where to find:** Look for "Add custom field" button

**Value:** Tailor Asana to your workflow

### Views: Sort, Group, Filter

#### Sort
Sort tasks by any field (e.g., priority, due date, assignee)

#### Group
Group tasks into sections based on field values (e.g., group by status)

#### Filter
Show only tasks meeting specific criteria (e.g., "only assigned to me")

**Strategic Use:** Customize view for your specific needs at any moment

---

## Advanced Task Features

### Multi-Homing Tasks
Tasks can appear in multiple projects simultaneously.

**How:** Add task to additional project  
**Benefit:** Task appears in multiple places, updated everywhere  
**Advanced:** Can delineate into specific section of a project

**Use case:** Cross-functional work, tasks relevant to multiple initiatives

- List view

### Overview Tab
Project dashboard with key information, status updates, and resources.

## Inbox Management

### Three Actions for Notifications

#### 1. Read & Archive
Simple FYI notifications - acknowledge and archive

#### 2. Additional Work Required
- Select task detail pane
- Add detail or give a like
- Archive notification

#### 3. Create Follow-Up Task
- Select notification
- Click 3 dots in right corner
- Can edit due date and name
- Create task
- Archive notification

### Bookmark vs. Archive
- **Bookmark:** Important notifications to revisit
- **Archive:** Remove from inbox (doesn't delete anything)

## Using Asana AI Effectively

### Important Tip: @-Mentions
When asking about specific tasks, projects, or people, use [@-mentions](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/14142327738779-Text-formatting-in-Asana#h_01HEJ8EKNEEYRQMXVPD6FYERBY) to help AI find exactly what you're looking for.

**Example:**  
- *"What's the status of @Project: Safety Training Program?"*
- *"Show me all tasks assigned to @John Smith that are overdue"*

**Value:** More precise, accurate results
