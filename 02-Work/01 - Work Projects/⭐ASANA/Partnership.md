# Partnership

## Value Statement
**Partnership** means working collaboratively across boundaries, breaking down silos, and building genuine relationships based on trust and mutual respect. Strong partnerships enable organizational success and make work more effective and fulfilling.

---

## Why Partnership Matters

### Organizational Impact
- **Breaks down silos** between Construction and Operations EHS
- **Enables collaboration** across functions and teams
- **Builds trust** throughout the organization
- **Accelerates problem-solving** through shared expertise
- **Creates synergy** - combined efforts exceed individual contributions

### Strategic Context
In EHS work, partnership is essential because:
- Safety requires coordination across teams
- No single person or team has all the answers
- Cross-functional issues require cross-functional solutions
- Organizational agility depends on collaboration
- Best practices emerge from shared learning

## What Partnership Looks Like

### In Leadership
**Partnership-Focused Leaders:**
- Seek input before making decisions
- Build relationships across organizational boundaries
- Share information openly and proactively
- Give credit generously
- View success as collective, not individual
- Remove barriers to collaboration
- Model collaborative behaviors

### In Teams
**Partnership-Focused Teams:**
- Reach out to other teams proactively
- Share knowledge and best practices
- Support other teams' initiatives
- Celebrate cross-functional wins
- View other teams as partners, not competitors
- Communicate regularly across boundaries

### In Individuals
**Partnership-Focused Individuals:**
- Build relationships across organizational boundaries
- Seek to understand others' perspectives
- Offer help without being asked
- Share information that helps others
- Give credit to partners
- View collaboration as strength, not weakness
