## Technical Concerns Addressed

### David's Original Concern
The "legacy dashboard and spreadsheet" were difficult to reverse-engineer. David wanted to understand:

- What's the holistic view of audit scope?

### Resolution

## Lessons for STACK Team

### Critical Understanding
1. **Don't reverse-engineer from the dashboard** - it won't show you the questions
2. **Focus on findings format** - that's what feeds the dashboard
3. **Protect the spreadsheet structure** - it's the critical link
4. **Use picklists everywhere possible** - ensures data quality

- Regional COMs who will be preparing sites
