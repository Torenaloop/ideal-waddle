## Scheduling Strategy & Notes

### Coordination Approach
1. **David initiates** with proposed dates from Antea Group
2. **COMs respond** with preferred week
3. **Dates confirmed** with ✅ status
4. **Antea finalizes** auditor assignments and travel

### Key Constraints
- **2-week lead time**: Sites need documents uploaded 10 business days before audit
- **Travel optimization**: Multiple sites in same region scheduled together when possible
- **Auditor availability**: Some auditors (Shannon Lonergan) note Monday travel days
- **Site operations**: Cannot disrupt critical operations

### Communication Pattern
David's standard message template:
> Hey [COM Name] - there is another round of 3rd party Environmental Audits forthcoming and I wanted to get your take on proposed dates from the consulting firm (Antea-same folks who did the last Env Audit in '23). I just received the audit questions/protocol that I can forward to you once you've given me your time preference. The proposed dates for [SITE] are: [DATES]. If you could let me know which week would work best by tomorrow morning, it would be greatly appreciated.
