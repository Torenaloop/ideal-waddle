## Document Requirements
Per Becky Matich's guidance (Feb 26, 2025):
- Operations teams should focus on **topics** (air, waste, etc.) rather than specific questions
- Auditor questions will vary by site based on permits and local regulations
- Document request list provided separately to show what should be prepared

## Timeline Requirements
- **10 business days** (2 weeks) prior to audit: All documents must be uploaded to external drive
- Antea Group auditors need this time to review materials before on-site visit
