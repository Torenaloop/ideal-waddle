# 2025 EHS Performance Goals Hub

## Overview

This hub connects STACK Infrastructure's **2025 Operations EHS Performance Goals** across all organizational levels. These goals establish clear accountability for environmental, health, and safety excellence from individual contributors to executive leadership.

---

## Goal Documents by Role Level

### 📊 Leadership Level
**[[2025 EHS Goals - Leadership]]**
- **Roles**: SVP, VP, Sr. Director, Director
- **Focus**: Strategic oversight, culture building, organizational systems
- **Accountability**: Portfolio/region-wide performance
- **Key Emphasis**: "Champion culture of safety excellence"

### 🏢 Site Management Level
**[[2025 EHS Goals - COMs]]**
- **Role**: Critical Operations Manager (COM)
- **Focus**: Site-level implementation, team management, daily operations
- **Accountability**: Individual site EHS outcomes
- **Key Emphasis**: "Implement comprehensive safety procedures"

### 👷 Individual Contributor Level
**[[2025 EHS Goals - ACOM & COT]]**
- **Roles**: Assistant COM (ACOM), Critical Operations Technician (COT)
- **Focus**: Personal execution, safe work practices, participation
- **Accountability**: Individual performance and peer leadership
- **Key Emphasis**: "Promote culture through personal actions"

## Common Performance Criteria Across All Levels

### 🚨 Incident Response Timeline
**Expectation**: Faster response at each level demonstrates excellence

| Level | Meets Standard | Outstanding Performance |
|---|---|---|
| **Leadership** | Ensure 24-hour notification | Corrective action in 30 days |
| **COMs** | 24-hour notification + clinic referral | Corrective action in 30 days |
| **ACOM/COT** | 24-hour notification + clinic visit | Help develop corrective actions |

**Why Cascading**: Each level builds on the previous—leaders ensure systems work, COMs execute systems, individuals follow procedures.

---

### 👁️ Safety Observations (SOSPES Program)
**Expectation**: Higher frequency demonstrates stronger safety culture

| Level | Meets Standard | Outstanding Performance |
|---|---|---|
| **Leadership** | 2 observations + team participation | Monthly observations + team |
| **COMs** | 2 observations + team participation | Monthly observations + team |
| **ACOM/COT** | 2 observations + team participation | Monthly observations |

**Aligned Expectations**: All levels have same "Meets" standard (2+ observations), demonstrating "safety is everyone's job." Outstanding performance requires monthly cadence.

---

### ⚖️ Regulatory Compliance (NOVs)
**Expectation**: Zero violations with escalating accountability by level

| Level | Acceptable | Outstanding |
|---|---|---|
| **Leadership** | Zero NOVs with financial penalties | Zero NOVs + controls in procedures |
| **COMs** | 1 NOV without financial penalty | Zero NOVs + controls in procedures |

**Leadership Difference**: COMs have site-level accountability (1 NOV acceptable at "Meets"). Leadership has portfolio accountability (zero financial penalties required at "Meets").

**Definition**: **NOV** = Notice of Violation (environmental or safety regulatory enforcement action)

---

### 📚 Training Requirements
**Expectation**: 100% completion with personal accountability

| Level | Core Requirements |
|---|---|
| **Leadership** | • Complete EQW training<br>• Promote toolbox talks<br>• Ensure team training systems work |
| **COMs** | • All team members complete EQW within 60 days<br>• All team members complete EHS Learning Path<br>• Sites participate in annual safety audit |
| **ACOM/COT** | • Self complete EQW within 60 days<br>• Self complete EHS Learning Path |

**Language Shift**: Leadership "ensures," COMs "manage team completion," ACOM/COT "self complete." Same outcome, different accountability level.

**Key Terms**:
- **EQW**: Electrical Qualified Worker training (critical for data center electrical safety)
- **LMS**: Learning Management System (training platform)
- **EHS Learning Path**: Comprehensive safety curriculum

---

### 🌱 Environmental Compliance
**Expectation**: Timely closure of audit findings

| Level | Performance Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Leadership** | Organizational systems support audit readiness |
| **COMs** | • 25-50% Q1 closure = Below<br>• 75% Q1 closure = Nearly Met<br>• 100% Q1 closure = Outstanding |
| **ACOM/COT** | Follow environmental procedures from audit findings |

**Connection**: These goals directly support [[2025 Environmental Audit Project Hub]]. The March-May audits will generate findings that must be closed per these performance standards.

## Performance Rating Scale Explained

All three documents use a **5-point performance scale**:

| Rating | Level | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Below | Significantly below expectations; requires immediate improvement |
| **2** | Nearly Met | Close to expectations but key gaps remain |
| **3** | Meets | Fully meets role expectations and performance standards |
| **4** | Exceeded | Consistently exceeds expectations; demonstrates excellence |
| **5** | Outstanding | Far exceeds expectations; best-in-class; sets example for others |

**Calibration Across Levels**:
- Same rating scale, different expectations based on role
- "Meets" at ACOM/COT level is foundational; "Meets" at Leadership level is strategic
- Outstanding at any level means you're modeling best practices for your peers

## Strategic Context & Alignment

### Bridge Construction-Ops EHS Gap ([[Bridge Construction-Ops EHS Gap]])

**The Challenge**:
- Construction EHS has historically been strong at STACK
- Operations EHS has been developing but not yet at same maturity level
- Gap creates inconsistent customer experience and risk exposure

**How These Goals Address Gap**:
1. **Clear Expectations**: Codify what "good" looks like at each level
2. **Consistent Standards**: Same criteria across all sites (no variation)
3. **Cultural Focus**: Emphasis on observations, engagement, not just compliance
4. **Accountability**: Each level knows exactly what they're responsible for
5. **Measurement**: Objective criteria enable tracking progress

**Goal**: Elevate Operations EHS to same excellence level as Construction EHS.

## How to Use This Framework

### For Performance Reviews
**Quarterly Check-Ins**:
1. Review relevant scorecard for your role level
2. Self-assess against each criterion
3. Identify "Below" or "Nearly Met" areas for improvement
4. Develop action plans with supervisor
5. Track progress on action plans

**Annual Reviews**:
1. Complete full self-assessment
2. Gather supporting evidence (observation records, training certificates, incident reports)
3. Calculate overall performance rating
4. Meet with supervisor to calibrate
5. Set goals for next year

---

### For Goal Setting
**Top-Down Cascade**:
1. Leadership sets strategic EHS vision (qualitative)
2. Goals translate vision to measurable outcomes (quantitative)
3. COMs develop site-specific action plans to achieve goals
4. ACOM/COT understand personal contribution to site goals

**Example**:
- **Leadership Vision**: "Safety is our #1 priority; everyone goes home safely every day"
- **Goal Translation**: "Zero NOVs + controls integrated into procedures"
- **COM Action Plan**: "Review all NOVs from 2024, embed controls in site SOPs, train team Q1"
- **ACOM/COT Contribution**: "Complete EHS Learning Path, follow updated SOPs, report any gaps"

---

### For Continuous Improvement
**Monthly EHS Meetings**:
- Review progress against goals
- Celebrate achievements ("Exceeded" or "Outstanding" performance)
- Problem-solve challenges ("Below" or "Nearly Met" areas)
- Share best practices across sites
- Adjust strategies as needed

**Trend Analysis**:
- Track performance over quarters
- Identify leading vs. lagging indicators
- Spot systemic issues requiring leadership attention
- Benchmark against industry standards

## Common Performance Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge: "Too busy with operations to do observations"
**Symptom**: Zero or low SOSPES observations
**Root Cause**: EHS seen as separate from operations, not integrated
**Solution**:
- Make observations part of daily routine (e.g., one per week during rounds)
- Leadership models behavior (when they visit sites, they do observations)
- Recognize observation participation in team meetings
- Simplify observation process (mobile app, quick forms)

---

### Challenge: "Don't know what to observe"
**Symptom**: Low participation, poor quality observations
**Root Cause**: Lack of training on observation process
**Solution**:
- Provide observation training in onboarding
- Share examples of good observations
- Create observation checklists for different areas
- Pair new observers with experienced ones
- David Nuckolls provides technical support

---

### Challenge: "Audit items not getting closed"
**Symptom**: Low Q1 completion percentages
**Root Cause**: Competing priorities, unclear ownership, lack of resources
**Solution**:
- Leadership clarifies expectations and deadlines
- COMs assign specific owners to each finding
- Track progress in weekly/monthly operational reviews
- Allocate resources (time, budget, people) for closure
- Escalate barriers to leadership for removal

---

### Challenge: "Near misses not being reported"
**Symptom**: Zero near miss reports (which might be under-reporting, not safety excellence)
**Root Cause**: Fear of punishment, lack of trust, unclear process
**Solution**:
- Leadership emphasizes "just culture" (learning, not blaming)
- Recognize and thank people who report near misses
- Show how near miss reports prevent future incidents
- Simplify reporting process
- Follow up publicly on corrective actions from near miss reports

## Summary

These 2025 EHS Performance Goals establish **clear, cascading accountability** for environmental, health, and safety excellence across all levels of STACK's operations organization. By aligning expectations from executives to front-line technicians, STACK creates a unified culture of safety where everyone knows their role and how they contribute to organizational EHS success.

**The goals are not just about compliance**—they're about building a culture where safety and environmental stewardship are core values, demonstrated through daily behaviors at every level of the organization.
