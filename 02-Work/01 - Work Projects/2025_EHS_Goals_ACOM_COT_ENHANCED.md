# 2025 Operations EHS Goals - ACOM & COT

## Role Overview

**Positions**: 
- **ACOM**: Assistant Critical Operations Manager
- **COT**: Critical Operations Technician

**Level**: Individual contributor and junior management
**Scope**: Personal EHS performance and team participation
**Reports to**: Critical Operations Manager (COM)

---

## Goal Statement

> [!ACOMS + COT]
> **Adhere to all STACK EHS policies** to prevent operator error, lost time safety incidents, and ensure timely reporting of incidents and follow-up actions. Report incidents, near misses, unsafe acts or conditions, positive behaviors, and injuries in a timely manner.
> 
> **Advocate for personnel well-being** and safeguard the physical environment in the data center by implementing comprehensive safety procedures. This includes workplace safety protocols, provision of personal protective equipment, and continuous safety training.
> 
> **Promote a culture of safety awareness and engagement**, work to create a secure work environment in the data center, mitigate risks, and ensure the health and safety of co-workers and vendors.

---

## Key Differences from COM/Leadership Goals

### ACOM & COT Focus (This Document)
- **Personal**: "Self" language - individual accountability
- **Participation**: Engage in programs, don't necessarily lead them
- **Execution**: Follow procedures, report issues, work safely
- **Learning**: Complete training, develop skills
- **Peer Influence**: Model safe behavior for coworkers

### COM Focus ([[2025 EHS Goals - COMs]])
- **Team**: Ensure entire site team performs
- **Management**: Lead programs, conduct investigations
- **Systems**: Implement procedures, track metrics
- **Oversight**: Monitor performance, coach team members
- **Accountability**: Responsible for site EHS outcomes

### Leadership Focus ([[2025 EHS Goals - Leadership]])
- **Strategic**: Set direction across sites/regions
- **Resources**: Allocate budget and personnel
- **Culture**: Drive organizational transformation
- **Systems**: Ensure processes work across organization
- **Accountability**: Aggregate performance across portfolio

## Performance Matrix Overview

ACOM & COT are evaluated on a **5-point scale** focused on personal execution and participation:

| Scale | Performance Level | Individual Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Below | Not following procedures; creating risk for self/others |
| **2** | Nearly Met | Mostly compliant but key gaps in participation or follow-through |
| **3** | Meets | Fully meets expectations for safe work and EHS engagement |
| **4** | Exceeded | Goes beyond requirements; models excellence for peers |
| **5** | Outstanding | Exemplary safety leader; recognized as peer champion |

## Performance Criteria by Category

### 🚨 Incident Response & Reporting (Personal Accountability)

| Performance Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Below (1)** | [[Late notification of incidents resulting in more severe medical needs]] |
| **Nearly Met (2)** | [[Incident notification within 48 Hours]] |
| **Meets (3)** | [[in 24-hrs & EE directed to designated occ. health clinic]] |
| **Exceeded (4)** | [[Draft investigation Report completed within 3 days]] |
| **Outstanding (5)** | [[Corrective action plan in motion within 30 days]] |

**Individual Accountability**:
- **Below**: Delayed reporting of injury to self or witnessed incident
- **Meets**: Immediately report incidents, cooperate with investigation, seek medical care
- **Exceeded**: Participate in investigation, provide detailed information
- **Outstanding**: Help develop corrective actions, implement preventive measures

**Why This Matters**: As individual contributors, you are often the first to witness or experience incidents. Your timely reporting can prevent injuries from worsening and enables quick corrective action.

---

### 👁️ Safety Observations (Personal Participation)

| Performance Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Below (1)** | [[Zero SOSPES observations]] |
| **Nearly Met (2)** | [[1 SOSPES observation]] |
| **Meets (3)** | [[2 SOSPES observations + team observations]] |
| **Exceeded (4)** | [[Quarterly SOSPES observations + team observations.]] |
| **Outstanding (5)** | [[Monthly SOSPES observations]] |

**Individual Participation**:
- **Observations**: Conducting structured safety observations of work areas and coworkers
- **Team Observations**: Participating when COM or others conduct observations
- **Monthly**: 12+ per year demonstrates proactive safety engagement
- **Quarterly**: 4+ per year shows consistent participation
- **Below**: Zero observations indicates lack of engagement in safety culture

**How to Excel**:
- Look for both hazards (unsafe conditions) and safe behaviors (recognition)
- Document observations in SOSPES system
- Follow up on corrective actions
- Share learnings with team
- Encourage peers to participate

### 📚 Training & Development (Personal Capability)

**Meets Level (3) Requirements**:
- [[All new team members have completed EQW w/in 60 days of onboarding]]
  - *For ACOM/COT*: "Self have completed Electrical Qualified Persons Training within 60 days"
- [[All team members completed EHS Learning path in LMS]]
  - *For ACOM/COT*: "Self completed EHS Learning path in LMS"

**Individual Responsibility**:
- **EQW Training**: Critical for anyone working near electrical systems in data centers
- **60-Day Window**: Must be completed before independent work on electrical equipment
- **EHS Learning Path**: Comprehensive safety curriculum covering all aspects of data center EHS
- **LMS Completion**: Track your own progress, meet deadlines, maintain certifications

**Career Development**: Training completion is not just about compliance—it builds your capability for advancement to COM or technical specialist roles.

---

### 🛡️ Safe Work Practices (Daily Execution)

**Meets Level (3) Requirements**:
- [[Zero Safety Near Misses]]

**What This Means**:
- Work safely every day without creating near miss situations
- Follow all procedures and safety protocols
- Use PPE correctly every time
- Stop work when unsafe conditions exist
- Report hazards before they become near misses

**Note on "Zero Near Misses"**: 
The goal is zero near misses **happening** (not zero near miss **reporting**). However, if you observe or experience a near miss, you should still report it—that's part of "promote a culture of safety awareness."

---

### 🌟 Safety Champion (Excellence Level)

**Exceeded Level (4) Requirement**:
- [[Participate as Safety Champion]]

**What is a Safety Champion?**
- Peer leader in safety at your site
- Models safe behavior consistently
- Mentors new team members on safety
- Leads toolbox talks or safety meetings
- Represents team in safety initiatives
- Recognized formally or informally as safety advocate

**How to Become a Safety Champion**:
- Demonstrate consistent safe work practices
- Complete all training ahead of schedule
- Conduct observations regularly
- Volunteer for safety initiatives
- Share ideas for safety improvements
- Express interest to your COM

## Goal Cascading in Practice

### Example: Incident Notification

**Leadership Goal** ([[2025 EHS Goals - Leadership]]):
> "Ensure incident notification within 24 hours" (strategic oversight)

**COM Goal** ([[2025 EHS Goals - COMs]]):
> "Incident notification within 24 hours and EE directed to designated occ health clinic" (manage team compliance)

**ACOM/COT Goal** (This Document):
> "Incident notification within 24 hours and directed to designated occ health clinic" (personal action)

**What This Means for You**:
- If injured: Report to COM/supervisor immediately, seek medical care at designated clinic
- If witness incident: Report to COM/supervisor immediately with details
- If near miss: Report through SOSPES or to COM within 24 hours
- Follow-up: Cooperate with investigation, provide honest account

---

### Example: Safety Observations

**Leadership Goal**:
> "Quarterly SOSPES observations + team observations" (model behavior, ensure program works)

**COM Goal**:
> "Monthly SOSPES observations + team observations" (lead by example, manage team participation)

**ACOM/COT Goal**:
> "2 SOSPES observations + team observations" (participate actively, meet minimum)

**How to Excel**:
- **Meets (3)**: Conduct 2 observations per year, participate when others observe
- **Exceeded (4)**: Conduct quarterly (4+ per year), actively engage in team observations
- **Outstanding (5)**: Conduct monthly (12+ per year), become go-to person for observations

## Real-World Scenarios

### Scenario 1: New Employee (First 60 Days)
**Your Goals**:
- Complete EQW training ✓ (within 60 days)
- Complete EHS Learning Path in LMS ✓
- Learn SOSPES observation process
- Understand incident reporting procedure
- Complete job-specific safety training

**Success Looks Like**: By day 60, you're fully qualified to work independently with all safety certifications current.

---

### Scenario 2: Experienced ACOM
**Your Goals**:
- Conduct 2+ SOSPES observations per year (Meets)
- Zero near misses in your work
- Help train new team members on EHS procedures
- Participate as Safety Champion (reach Exceeded)

**Success Looks Like**: You're recognized as peer leader, consulted by COM on safety initiatives, considered for promotion.

---

### Scenario 3: Incident Occurs
**Your Immediate Actions**:
1. Ensure scene is safe / provide first aid if trained
2. Notify COM/supervisor immediately (don't wait for end of shift)
3. If injury, seek care at designated occ health clinic
4. Document what you saw/experienced while fresh in memory
5. Cooperate fully with investigation
6. Help identify corrective actions
7. Follow up to ensure actions implemented

**Outstanding Performance**: You notice the precursor conditions and report them **before** incident occurs, preventing injury.

---

### Scenario 4: Witnessing Unsafe Behavior
**Your Options**:
- **Immediate**: Stop the unsafe work if imminent danger
- **Peer-to-Peer**: Politely point out the unsafe behavior to coworker
- **Supervisor**: Report to COM if pattern of unsafe behavior
- **SOSPES**: Document observation in system
- **Near Miss**: If near miss occurred, report immediately

**Culture Building**: Approaching peers with care and respect about safety builds trust and improves overall safety culture.

## Common Questions

### "What if my COM isn't doing observations?"
**Answer**: You can still conduct your observations. Model the behavior you want to see. If persistent issue, you can raise to David Nuckolls (EHS Manager) or regional leadership.

### "I'm new and don't know what to observe"
**Answer**: Start with basics:
- Are people using PPE correctly?
- Are walkways clear of obstacles?
- Are emergency exits accessible?
- Are chemicals stored properly?
- Are tools in good condition?

Ask your COM for observation training—it's their responsibility to support you.

### "How do I become a Safety Champion?"
**Answer**: 
1. Consistently model safe behavior
2. Complete all training on time
3. Conduct regular observations
4. Volunteer for safety initiatives
5. Express interest to your COM
6. Attend safety meetings actively

### "What if I report a near miss—will I get in trouble?"
**Answer**: No. STACK promotes near miss reporting as proactive safety. Reporting prevents future incidents and demonstrates safety culture engagement. You should be recognized, not penalized.
