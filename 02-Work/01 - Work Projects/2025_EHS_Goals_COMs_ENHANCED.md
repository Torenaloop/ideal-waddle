# 2025 Operations EHS Goals - Critical Operations Managers

## Role Overview

**Position**: Critical Operations Manager (COM)
**Level**: Site-level operational leadership
**Scope**: Direct responsibility for data center EHS performance
**Reports to**: Regional Directors / Senior Directors

---

## Goal Statement

> [!COMs]
> **Adhere to all STACK EHS policies** to prevent operator error, lost time safety incidents, and ensure timely reporting of incidents and follow-up actions. Report incidents, near misses, unsafe acts or conditions, positive behaviors, and injuries in a timely manner.
> 
> **Advocate for personnel well-being** and safeguard the physical environment in the data center by implementing comprehensive safety procedures. This includes workplace safety protocols, provision of personal protective equipment, and continuous safety training.
> 
> **Promote a culture of safety awareness and engagement**, work to create a secure work environment in the data center, mitigate risks, and ensure the health and safety of co-workers and vendors.

---

## Performance Matrix Overview

COMs are evaluated on a **5-point scale** across multiple EHS dimensions:

| Scale | Performance Level | Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Below | Performance significantly below expectations; requires immediate improvement |
| **2** | Nearly Met | Close to meeting expectations but key gaps remain |
| **3** | Meets | Fully meets role expectations and performance standards |
| **4** | Exceeded | Consistently exceeds expectations; demonstrates excellence |
| **5** | Outstanding | Far exceeds expectations; sets example for others; best-in-class |

## Performance Criteria by Category

### 🚨 Incident Response & Investigation

| Performance Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Below (1)** | [[Late notification of incidents resulting in more severe medical needs]] |
| **Nearly Met (2)** | [[Incident notification within 48 Hours]] |
| **Meets (3)** | [[in 24-hrs & EE directed to designated occ. health clinic]] |
| **Exceeded (4)** | [[Draft investigation Report completed within 3 days]] |
| **Outstanding (5)** | [[Corrective action plan in motion within 30 days]] |

**Why This Matters**: Timely incident response prevents escalation of injuries, demonstrates care for employee wellbeing, and enables faster root cause analysis. Late notification can result in more serious medical outcomes and regulatory issues.

---

### 🌱 Environmental Compliance & Audit Performance

| Performance Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Below (1)** | [[25-50% Environmental Audit items complete in Q1]] |
| **Nearly Met (2)** | [[75% Environmental Audit items complete in Q1]] |
| **Meets (3)** | [[Complete all open items from environmental audits]] |
| **Outstanding (5)** | [[100% Environmental Audit items complete in Q1, or extension requested]] |

**Connection to 2025 Audit**: These goals directly support the [[2025 Environmental Audit Project Hub]]. Sites that complete audit items quickly demonstrate operational excellence and regulatory commitment.

---

### ⚖️ Regulatory Compliance (NOVs)

| Performance Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Below (1)** | [[2 or more NOV's]] |
| **Nearly Met (2)** | [[1 NOV]] |
| **Meets (3)** | [[1 NOV w/o financial penalty]] |
| **Exceeded (4)** | [[Zero NOV's]] |
| **Outstanding (5)** | [[Zero NOV's and controls integrated into procedures]] |

**Definition**: **NOV** = Notice of Violation (environmental or safety regulatory finding)

**Why This Matters**: NOVs represent regulatory non-compliance, can result in fines, damage STACK's reputation, and indicate systemic control failures. Outstanding performance means not only avoiding violations but building them into standard procedures.

---

### 👁️ Safety Observations (SOSPES Program)

| Performance Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Below (1)** | [[Zero SOSPES observations]] |
| **Nearly Met (2)** | [[1 SOSPES observation]] |
| **Meets (3)** | [[2 SOSPES observations + team observations]] |
| **Exceeded (4)** | [[Quarterly SOSPES observations + team observations.]] |
| **Outstanding (5)** | [[Monthly SOSPES observations + team observations]] |

**What is SOSPES?**: STACK's **Safety Observation Program** where managers and team members conduct structured safety observations to proactively identify hazards and safe behaviors.

**Why This Matters**: Proactive observations prevent incidents before they occur. Zero observations indicates lack of engagement; monthly observations demonstrate active safety culture leadership.

---

### 📚 Training & Development

**Meets Level (3) Requirements**:
- [[All new team members have completed EQW w/in 60 days of onboarding]]
- [[All team members completed EHS Learning path in LMS]]
- [[Sites have actively participated in annual safety audit & acted on remediation needs]]

**Key Terms**:
- **EQW**: Electrical Qualified Worker training (critical for data center electrical safety)
- **LMS**: Learning Management System
- **EHS Learning Path**: Comprehensive safety and environmental training curriculum

**Why This Matters**: Well-trained teams prevent incidents. 60-day window ensures new hires are qualified before independent work on electrical systems. LMS completion ensures consistent safety knowledge.

---

### 🛡️ Safety Culture & Engagement

**Meets Level (3) Requirements**:
- [[Zero Safety Near Misses]]
- [[Keep all environmental & compliance reporting current]]
- [[Meet Permit Obligations]]

**Note on "Zero Near Misses"**: This may seem counterintuitive (shouldn't we want near miss reporting?), but in context likely means "zero unreported near misses" or refers to a specific classification.

**Why This Matters**: Current reporting and permit compliance demonstrate operational control and prevent regulatory surprises. Engaged teams report issues before they become incidents.

## How to Use This Scorecard

### For COMs (Self-Assessment)
1. **Monthly**: Review your performance against each criterion
2. **Quarterly**: Conduct formal self-assessment for your site
3. **Annually**: Use for performance review preparation
4. **Action Planning**: Focus improvement efforts on "Below" or "Nearly Met" areas

### For Regional Directors (Performance Reviews)
1. **Track**: Monitor COM performance across all sites in region
2. **Coach**: Provide targeted support for areas below "Meets"
3. **Recognize**: Celebrate "Exceeded" and "Outstanding" performance
4. **Compare**: Benchmark performance across sites

### For David Nuckolls (EHS Manager)
1. **Aggregate**: Roll up performance across all COMs
2. **Identify Trends**: Spot systemic issues vs. site-specific challenges
3. **Resource Allocation**: Direct training/support where most needed
4. **Report**: Provide leadership visibility into EHS performance
