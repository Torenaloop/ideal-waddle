# 2025 Operations EHS Goals - Leadership

## Role Overview

**Positions**: SVP (Senior Vice President), VP (Vice President), Sr. Director, Director
**Level**: Regional and organizational leadership
**Scope**: Strategic EHS oversight across multiple sites/regions
**Accountability**: Setting culture, ensuring compliance, driving continuous improvement

---

## Goal Statement

> [!Note] Summary
> **Ensure strict adherence** to all STACK EHS policies and standards across all levels of the organization to prevent operator errors, minimize lost time safety incidents, and ensure prompt reporting and follow-up actions. Champion a culture of safety excellence and advocate for the well-being of personnel, prioritizing the safeguarding of the physical environment in the data center.
> 
> **Provide strategic leadership** in the development and implementation of comprehensive safety procedures, including workplace protocols, provision of personal protective equipment, and ongoing safety training initiatives. Oversee the implementation of environmental safety measures such as fire prevention, chemical handling, and compliance with electrical safety standards, ensuring full regulatory compliance and operational resilience.
> 
> **Drive initiatives** to foster a culture of safety awareness and engagement throughout the organization, promoting proactive risk mitigation strategies and continuous improvement efforts. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to create a secure work environment, mitigate risks effectively, and ensure the health and safety of employees and stakeholders remains paramount in all operational endeavors.

---

## Leadership vs. COM Distinction

### Leadership Focus (This Document)
- **Strategic**: Setting direction, culture, and standards
- **Oversight**: Ensuring systems and processes work across sites
- **Resources**: Allocating budget, personnel, training
- **Cross-Functional**: Collaborating with other departments
- **Accountability**: Aggregate performance across multiple sites/regions

### COM Focus ([[2025 EHS Goals - COMs]])
- **Tactical**: Day-to-day site EHS execution
- **Direct**: Managing site team and vendor safety
- **Hands-On**: Conducting observations, investigations
- **Site-Specific**: Individual facility performance
- **Accountability**: Single site EHS outcomes

## Performance Matrix Overview

Leadership is evaluated on a **5-point scale** with emphasis on strategic impact and organizational culture:

| Scale | Performance Level | Leadership Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Below | Systemic issues; organizational culture or compliance gaps |
| **2** | Nearly Met | Making progress but key strategic objectives incomplete |
| **3** | Meets | Full strategic execution; standards met across organization |
| **4** | Exceeded | Proactive leadership; organization exceeds standards |
| **5** | Outstanding | Industry-leading; creates best practices; cultural transformation |

## Performance Criteria by Category

### 🚨 Incident Response & Investigation (Strategic Oversight)

| Performance Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Below (1)** | [[Late notification of incidents resulting in more severe medical needs]] |
| **Nearly Met (2)** | [[Track incident notifications within 48 hours]] |
| **Meets (3)** | Ensure incident notification within 24 hours |
| **Exceeded (4)** | [[Draft investigation Report completed within 3 days]] |
| **Outstanding (5)** | [[Corrective action plan in motion within 30 days]] |

**Leadership Accountability**: 
- Ensure systems in place for timely notification across all sites
- Monitor aggregate incident response times
- Provide resources for rapid investigation
- Track corrective action implementation
- **Not** personally investigating (unless critical incident), but ensuring process works

---

### ⚖️ Regulatory Compliance (Organizational Level)

| Performance Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Below (1)** | [[3 or More NOVs]] |
| **Nearly Met (2)** | 2 NOVs |
| **Meets (3)** | [[Zero NOV's with Financial Penalties]] |
| **Exceeded (4)** | [[Zero NOV's]] |
| **Outstanding (5)** | [[Zero NOV's and controls integrated into procedures]] + [[100% Environmental Audit items complete in Q1, or extension requested]] |

**Leadership Accountability**:
- **Below**: Multiple sites with NOVs indicates systemic issues
- **Meets**: Organization avoids financial penalties (may have minor violations without fines)
- **Outstanding**: Not only zero violations but controls embedded in standard procedures organization-wide

**Connection to [[2025 Environmental Audit Project Hub]]**: Leadership ensures audit findings drive procedural improvements, not just site-by-site fixes.

---

### 👁️ Safety Observations (Cultural Leadership)

| Performance Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Below (1)** | Zero SOSPES observations (indicates leadership not modeling behavior) |
| **Nearly Met (2)** | [[1 SOSPES observation]] |
| **Meets (3)** | [[2 SOSPES observations + team observations]] |
| **Exceeded (4)** | [[Quarterly SOSPES observations + team observations.]] |
| **Outstanding (5)** | [[Monthly SOSPES observations + team observations]] |

**Leadership Accountability**:
- **Personal Participation**: Leaders must model observation behavior
- **Team Engagement**: Ensure direct reports are conducting observations
- **Cultural Signal**: Leadership participation demonstrates "safety is everyone's job"
- **Visibility**: Leaders being visible on floors conducting observations builds trust

---

### 📚 Training & Development (Strategic Enablement)

**Meets Level (3) Requirements**:
- [[All new team members have completed EQW w/in 60 days of onboarding]]
- [[All team members completed EHS Learning path in LMS]]
- [[Sites have actively participated in annual safety audit & acted on remediation needs]]
- Complete Electrical Qualified Persons training (leadership self-development)
- Promote use of toolbox talks

**Leadership Accountability**:
- Ensure training systems are effective across organization
- Allocate resources for training programs
- Monitor completion rates across all sites
- Remove barriers to training participation
- Model continuous learning (complete own EQW training)
- Promote toolbox talks as cultural norm

---

### 🛡️ Safety Culture & Regulatory Excellence

**Meets Level (3) Requirements**:
- [[Keep all environmental & compliance reporting current]]
- [[Meet Permit Obligations]]
- 1 Safety Near Miss (acceptable at leadership level recognizing organizational scale)

**Leadership Interpretation**:
- **Current Reporting**: All sites in portfolio have up-to-date regulatory submissions
- **Permit Compliance**: Zero permit violations across organization
- **Near Miss Acceptance**: At scale of multiple sites, 1 near miss demonstrates strong proactive culture (better than zero near miss reports which might indicate under-reporting)

## Strategic Leadership Behaviors

### Culture Setting
**"Champion a culture of safety excellence"**
- Visible commitment to EHS in all communications
- Resource allocation demonstrates EHS priority
- Recognition programs for safety excellence
- Immediate response to incidents showing leadership care
- Integration of EHS into business planning and reviews

### Cross-Functional Collaboration
**"Collaborate with cross-functional teams"**
- Partner with HR on safety training and incident support
- Work with Facilities on environmental compliance
- Engage Finance on EHS budget and ROI
- Coordinate with Legal on regulatory strategy
- Align with Customer Success on EHS as competitive advantage

### Strategic Planning
**"Provide strategic leadership"**
- Set EHS vision and multi-year goals
- Allocate budget for EHS programs and resources
- Hire and develop EHS talent (like David Nuckolls)
- Integrate EHS into operational reviews
- Anticipate regulatory changes and prepare organization

### Operational Resilience
**"Ensure full regulatory compliance and operational resilience"**
- Build systems that prevent compliance lapses
- Create redundancy in critical EHS processes
- Develop succession planning for EHS roles
- Invest in tools and technology for EHS management
- Monitor leading indicators, not just lagging metrics

## Common Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge: Multiple Sites with NOVs
**Root Cause**: Systemic issue, not site-specific
**Leadership Solution**:
- Conduct root cause analysis across sites
- Identify common control failures
- Implement standardized procedures
- Provide targeted training
- Increase oversight until resolved

### Challenge: Low Observation Participation
**Root Cause**: Culture doesn't value proactive safety
**Leadership Solution**:
- Model behavior (leaders do observations)
- Recognize and reward participation
- Simplify observation process
- Share findings and corrective actions
- Integrate into performance reviews

### Challenge: Audit Items Not Closing
**Root Cause**: Competing priorities, resource constraints
**Leadership Solution**:
- Clarify expectations and deadlines
- Allocate resources (people, budget, time)
- Remove barriers and roadblocks
- Track progress in operational reviews
- Escalate when support needed from other functions
