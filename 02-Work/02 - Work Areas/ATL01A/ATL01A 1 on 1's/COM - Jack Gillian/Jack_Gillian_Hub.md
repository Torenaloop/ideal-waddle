## Performance Framework

### 2025 EHS Goals
Jack is evaluated on a **5-point scale** (see [[2025 EHS Goals - COMs]]):

**1 = Below** | **2 = Nearly Met** | **3 = Meets** | **4 = Exceeded** | **5 = Outstanding**

### Key Performance Criteria

#### Incident Response
- **Meets**: 24-hour notification + designated occ health clinic referral
- **Outstanding**: Corrective action plan in motion within 30 days

#### Environmental Audit
- **Below**: 25-50% Q1 completion
- **Nearly Met**: 75% Q1 completion
- **Outstanding**: 100% Q1 completion

#### NOVs (Notices of Violation)
- **Below**: 2 or more NOVs
- **Meets**: 1 NOV without financial penalty
- **Outstanding**: Zero NOVs + controls integrated into procedures

#### SOSPES Observations
- **Below**: Zero observations
- **Meets**: 2 observations + team participation
- **Outstanding**: Monthly observations + team participation

#### Training
- **Meets**: All new team members complete EQW within 60 days + All team complete EHS Learning Path
- **Outstanding**: Above + proactive development initiatives
