### 3️⃣ SOSPES Items

**Topic**: Safety Observation Program status and progress

**Context**:
**SOSPES** = STACK's Safety Observation Program and Engagement System
- Key metric in [[2025 EHS Goals - COMs]]
- COMs expected to conduct 2+ observations per year (Meets)
- Outstanding performance = Monthly observations + team participation
- Proactive safety culture indicator

**Typical Discussion Points**:
- Number of observations completed (Jack's personal + team)
- Quality and findings from recent observations
- Positive behaviors observed vs. hazards identified
- Follow-up on corrective actions from observations
- Team participation rates
- Barriers to observation completion

**Performance Targets** (from EHS Goals):
- **Below**: Zero observations
- **Nearly Met**: 1 observation
- **Meets**: 2 observations + team participation
- **Exceeded**: Quarterly observations + team
- **Outstanding**: Monthly observations + team

**Best Practices**:
- Schedule dedicated time for observations (don't wait for "spare time")
- Involve team members in conducting observations
- Focus on both hazards AND safe behaviors (recognition)
- Close loop on corrective actions
- Share learnings in toolbox talks

### 5️⃣ Training

**Topic**: Team training completion status and needs

**Context**:
From [[2025 EHS Goals - COMs]], training requirements include:
- **EQW Training**: All new team members complete Electrical Qualified Worker training within 60 days of onboarding
- **EHS Learning Path**: All team members complete EHS Learning Path in LMS
- **Annual Audit Participation**: Sites actively participate in annual safety audit

**Typical Discussion Points**:
- Training completion rates by team member
- New hires needing onboarding training
- Overdue training items
- LMS access issues or technical problems
- Specialized training needs (confined space, fall protection, etc.)
- Scheduling challenges (coverage while people attend training)
- Certifications expiring soon

**David's Support**:
- Provide training resources and schedules
- Troubleshoot LMS issues
- Arrange specialized training (instructors, vendors)
- Track completion for organizational reporting
- Follow up on overdue items

**Performance Impact**:
- **Meets Standard**: All team members complete EHS Learning Path AND all new members complete EQW within 60 days
- **Below Standard**: Training gaps, overdue items, new hires not qualified on time

**Common Challenges**:
- Scheduling training around 24/7 operations
- Ensuring adequate coverage during training
- Keeping track of multiple certifications and expiration dates
- Motivating team members to complete online training
