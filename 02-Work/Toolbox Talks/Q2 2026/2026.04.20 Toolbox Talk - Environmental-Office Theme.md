


**TOOLBOX TALK – OFFICE ERGONOMICS & WORKSTATION SETUP**

Many of us spend long hours at desks, in front of monitors, or working on laptops—whether in offices or support spaces within data centers. Over time, poor workstation setup and posture can lead to discomfort, fatigue, and even long-term injuries. Small adjustments to how we sit, type, and view our screens can make a big difference in preventing strain and keeping us comfortable and productive throughout the day.

**What Creates Risk?**  
Office-related discomfort often builds gradually and can be easy to overlook. Common risks include:

- **Poor posture:** Slouching or leaning forward for extended periods
    
- **Improper monitor height:** Screens too high or too low causing neck strain
    
- **Repetitive movements:** Typing and mouse use without proper support
    
- **Extended sitting:** Limited movement leading to stiffness and reduced circulation
    
- **Laptop use without accessories:** Causing awkward wrist, neck, and shoulder positions
    

These issues can lead to eye strain, headaches, back pain, and repetitive stress injuries if not addressed.

**Practical Ways to Improve Your Setup**

- **Adjust your chair:** Keep feet flat on the floor, knees at hip level, and back supported
    
- **Position your monitor:** Top of the screen at or slightly below eye level, about an arm’s length away
    
- **Keep wrists neutral:** Use a keyboard and mouse positioned at elbow height
    
- **Take movement breaks:** Stand, stretch, or walk every 30–60 minutes
    
- **Use proper equipment:** Consider a laptop stand, external keyboard, or ergonomic mouse if needed
    
- **Reduce eye strain:** Follow the 20-20-20 rule—every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds
    

A well-set-up workspace helps prevent injuries, improves comfort, and supports better focus throughout the day. Small changes now can prevent bigger issues later.
