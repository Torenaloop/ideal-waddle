**TOOLBOX TALK – STRESS & FATIGUE MANAGEMENT**

Stress and fatigue can build up quickly, especially in fast-paced environments like data centers where focus and precision are critical. April is a great time to reset and recognize how ongoing stress and lack of rest can impact both mental wellbeing and job performance. When left unmanaged, these factors can lead to errors, reduced awareness, and increased risk of incidents. Taking small steps to manage stress and fatigue helps protect both your health and the safety of those around you.

**What Creates Risk?**  
Stress and fatigue don’t always look the same for everyone, but common workplace contributors include:

- **High workloads or tight deadlines** that create pressure and mental strain
    
- **Extended shifts or irregular schedules** that disrupt sleep routines
    
- **Constant connectivity**, making it hard to mentally disconnect from work
    
- **Monotonous or highly detailed tasks** that require sustained concentration
    
- **Poor sleep quality**, leading to reduced alertness and slower reaction times
    

Over time, these factors can reduce focus, impair decision-making, and increase the likelihood of mistakes or near misses.

**Practical Ways to Manage Stress & Fatigue**

- **Prioritize quality sleep:** Aim for consistent, uninterrupted rest whenever possible
    
- **Take short breaks:** Step away periodically to reset your focus and reduce mental strain
    
- **Stay hydrated and eat regularly:** Fueling your body supports energy and concentration
    
- **Set boundaries:** Disconnect from work communications when off-shift to recharge
    
- **Speak up early:** If you’re feeling overwhelmed or fatigued, communicate with your team or leader
    
- **Look out for each other:** Changes in mood, focus, or behavior can be early warning signs
    

Managing stress and fatigue isn’t just about feeling better—it’s about staying sharp, making good decisions, and keeping our operations safe and reliable.
