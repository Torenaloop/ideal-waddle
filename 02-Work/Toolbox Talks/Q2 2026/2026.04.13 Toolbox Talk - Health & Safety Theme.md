

**TOOLBOX TALK – SLIPS, TRIPS, AND FALL PREVENTION**

Slips, trips, and falls are among the most common workplace incidents—even in clean, well-managed environments like data centers and office spaces. These incidents can happen quickly and often result from small, overlooked hazards. Whether you’re walking the data floor, moving between rooms, or navigating office areas, staying aware of your surroundings plays a key role in preventing injuries. A simple misstep can lead to strains, sprains, or more serious injuries that impact both safety and operations.

**What Creates Risk?**  
Slips, trips, and falls are often caused by everyday conditions, including:

- **Wet or slippery floors** from spills, cleaning, or weather conditions
    
- **Loose cables or clutter** in walkways and work areas
    
- **Uneven surfaces** or changes in flooring height
    
- **Poor lighting** that makes hazards harder to see
    
- **Rushing or distraction**, especially during busy tasks or transitions
    

These hazards can exist in both technical spaces and office environments, making awareness essential at all times.

**Practical Ways to Prevent Incidents**

- **Keep walkways clear:** Secure cables and remove clutter from paths
    
- **Address spills immediately:** Clean up or report hazards right away
    
- **Wear appropriate footwear:** Choose shoes with good traction
    
- **Use handrails on stairs:** Especially when carrying items
    
- **Stay focused:** Avoid distractions like phones while walking
    
- **Report hazards:** If something doesn’t look safe, speak up so it can be fixed
    

Preventing slips, trips, and falls comes down to awareness, housekeeping, and taking a moment to slow down. Looking out for these everyday hazards helps keep everyone safe and operations running smoothly.
