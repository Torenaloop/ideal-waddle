# **NVA02D/E Campus Manager 1:1 – EHS Monthly Framework**

---

## **1. Site Pulse (Start Here)**

- What’s been top of mind the last 4 weeks?
    
- Any operational changes, pressures, or concerns?
    
- Biggest current safety risk?
    

---

## **2. EHS Performance (Look Back)**

- Incidents / near misses → trends? repeat issues?
    
- Root causes addressed or just closed out?
    
- Open audit / inspection actions status
    

📌 Ref: OSHA **29 CFR 1904**, **1910 / 1926**

---

## **3. Current High-Risk Exposure**

Focus discussion on:

- Electrical / LOTO (**1910.147 / NFPA 70E**)
    
- Work at Height (**1926 Subpart M / 1910**)
    
- Confined Space (**1910.146 / 1926**)
    
- Lifting / Rigging
    
- Are **JHAs / MOPs / SOPs** being followed?
    

---

## **4. Contractor & SIMOPS**

- Contractor performance / recurring issues?
    
- Alignment with **GC Site-Specific Safety Plan**
    
- Any SIMOPS conflicts between construction & ops?
    
- Following STACK APs + EHS Guidelines v6?
    

---

## **5. Culture & Leading Indicators**

- Are people speaking up / using Stop Work Authority?
    
- Real engagement or “checkbox” activity?
    
- Quality of safety observations / toolbox talks?
    

---

## **6. Program Gaps (Challenge the System)**

- What’s working well?
    
- What’s creating friction or slowing work?
    
- Any gaps in training, permits, or procedures?
    

---

## **7. Forward Look (Next 4–8 Weeks)**

- Upcoming high-risk work:
    
    - Energizations / shutdowns
        
    - Major lifts
        
    - New contractors
        
- Pre-planning complete? (JHAs, MOPs)
	- Lift plans - compressors replacements on 02D
	- "ditto" for 02E
	- Arc flash barrier in the RPPs (subfeed panels, series of branch circuit breakers) - arc jumped between phases
    
- **Change Management needed?**
    

---

## **8. Support & Escalation**

- Where does the campus need EHS support?
    
- Anything requiring escalation?
    

---

## **9. Close – Commitments**

- Top 2–3 priorities before next 1:1
    
- Owners + follow-ups
