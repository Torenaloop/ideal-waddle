## Overview

- **Prepare** - Pre-planning, communications, setting expectations, sharing what’s to come.
- **Intro** - Create a safe space to learn. Share “purpose, process, payoff”.
- **Activity** - Keep entire class on time & on task.
- **Fun** - Fun along the way without becoming an entertainment event.
- **Conclude** - Wrap up module, “Test out for Understanding” or “TOFU”.

---

The **Pilot** role focuses on the <u>**“A”**</u> in the AKSM model.
