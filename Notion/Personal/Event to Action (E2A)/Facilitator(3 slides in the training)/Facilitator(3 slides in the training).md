---
> The **Facilitator** provides learners a forum to demonstrate and apply what they have learned.

---

> [!tip] 💡
> Should only occur AFTER having given knowledge.

> [!tip] 💡
> Facilitator’s goals should be:
> - 80%: Learners speaking
> - 20%: Trainer(s) speaking

## Overview

- **Facilitator ?’s** - why, when, who, what if, how
- **“Tell me more” prompts** - alternative perspectives, different opinions
- **“Give me an example” prompts** - flip classroom on the learners to provide examples
- **Active Listening** - listening to what is & is not being said
- **Economy of words** - facilitator replies are clear & content focused

---

The **Facilitator** role focuses on the <u>**“A”**</u> ,<u>**“K”, **</u>and** **<u>**“S”**</u> in the AKSM model.
