**Facts**

- 74k lawnmower injuries annually in US
- 1/4 of the injuries involve the hand, wrist and fingers
- 1/4 of hand injuries involving lawnmowers result in amputation and shorting of hand, fingers or wrist

**Best Practices** 

- Never let kids play around/near around a mower or around anyone mowing a lawn to avoid shrapnel or being caught in mowers path.
- Do not use substances that would impair your ability to react to changing conditions
- Avoid cutting in damp or wet conditions after a rain storm. Damp grass is much more likely to clog the mower, setting up for a dangerous condition
- If clogged, stop engine, remove keys and make sure any brakes or safety mechanisms are in place. 
- To unclog the mower, do not use your hands, feet or any other part of your body. Use a broom handle or long stick to remove/unclog the grass.
- Pay caution to mowing on hills to avoid possible tip over of the mower.
