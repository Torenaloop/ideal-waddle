## Overview

- **Content, Testing, Questioning** - Present clearly/concisely with limited rabbit holes.
- **Examples** - Example of content & skills in action (NOT story time).
- **Activity Set up** - I.E.D.D.T. flawlessly for every activity.
- **Verbal communication** - Projection, clear speeds, varied pace, no verbal fillers or hedges.
- **Non-verbal communication** - Used as a tool to engage, not as a distraction to learners.

---

### <u>Additional Skills:</u>

1. Present content as written- focus on knowledge transfer
2. Effectively use media - useful visual, audio, & kinesthetic movement
3. Knowledge-based questions - black & white, no gray areas
4. Assessing learning gaps - ex: Test out for understanding

Avoid stacking questions and only ask 1 question at a time.
