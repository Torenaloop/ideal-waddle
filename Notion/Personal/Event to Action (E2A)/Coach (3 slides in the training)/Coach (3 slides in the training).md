> [!tip] 💡

## Overview

- **Role play** - concise & clear instructions for activity
- **Feedback** - provide targeted & actionable feedback
- **Shaping** - helping learner find all elements of an answer
- **Self discovery** - promote self-feedback & shape as needed
- **Inspire** - provide encouragement, recognize, & build confidence
