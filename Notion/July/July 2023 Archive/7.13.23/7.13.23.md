> [!note]+ ==“Can a Jira project be linked to a Notion Page”==
> Yes, it is possible to link a Jira project to a Notion page. You can add a Jira project to Notion by following these steps¹:
> 
> 1. Create accounts on both platforms.
> 2. Go to the Notion Jira integration page.
> 3. Click "Connect to Jira."
> 4. Enter your Jira username and password.
> 5. Click "Authorize."
> 6. Select the Jira projects that you want to integrate with Notion.
> 7. Click "Save."
> 
> Alternatively, you can embed a Jira board or project into Notion by copying the link from Jira and pasting it into Notion. Then select "Paste as database" to create an empty database with a preset list of properties².
> 
> I hope this helps!
>
