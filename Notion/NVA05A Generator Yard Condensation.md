# Description

The condensate drains in the generator yard are creating a potential slip/trip hazard due to water discharged from the CAPS HVAC units serving the MERs. The standing water is leading to algae growth, creating a slippery surface and increasing the risk of incidents.

Looking ahead to winter, the volume of water may also result in ice buildup, requiring consistent salting to maintain a safe working environment.

I am reaching out to PCM services (John Kile) to evaluate the area and propose a long-term solution to ensure safety and cleanliness in the yard.
