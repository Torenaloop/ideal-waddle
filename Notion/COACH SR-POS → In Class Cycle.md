<!-- Column 1 -->
### Self Reflection

Facilitator to ask the learner:

- What went well?
- What would you like to improve?

<!-- Column 2 -->
### Praise

Facilitator to provide an observed example of:

- What went well?
- The impact of doing the identified behavior well.

<!-- Column 3 -->
### Opportunity

Facilitator to provide an observed example of:

- What is the area of opportunity.
- Impact of the identified opportunity if it goes unchanged.
- Provide a suggestion on how to solve the opportunity.

<!-- Column 4 -->
### Suggestion

Facilitator will provide a suggestion to correct:

- Follow up on feedback to address any questions the learner may have.
- Ask for commitment.
