<!-- Column 1 -->
**Introduce** - The activity that learners will participate in during the allotted time.

**Do** - The act of doing the activity.

<!-- Column 2 -->
**Explain** - Explain the details/role & outcomes of the activity.

**Debrief** - Conduct formal debrief of the overall activity along with global feedback.

<!-- Column 3 -->
**Demonstrate** - Demo what the activity will look like & clarify any questions.

**Transition** - Transition to next topic, ensure a tie back to what is learned and a tie forward to what will be learned.
