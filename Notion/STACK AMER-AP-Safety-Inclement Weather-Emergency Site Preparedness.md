# Purpose

1. Provide baseline of emergency preparedness in the event the site needs to be continuously staffed for an extended period.
2. **Detailed Process and Policy**
    1. Overview
        1. If the site must me continuously staffed for multiple days in an emergency, the site will need provisions so that employees can maintain the site for the duration of the event.
        2. For emergency actions related to the event, the EOP associated with that event should be followed. This document covers provisions required for the site to be staffed for an extended period.
        3. Examples when the emergency provisions should be used:
            1. Wildfires in the area that limits access to the site.
            2. Earthquake in the area that limits access to the site.
            3. Heavy rain or snow/blizzard that would limit access to the site.
            4. Protest that limit access to the site.
            5. Any other reason that the facility must be staffed continuously for an extended period.
    2. Implementation
        4. Sites shall work with their management team to procure the supplies listed in this document at the earliest opportunity and the supplies should be audited semi-annually to verify emergency preparedness.
        5. When an emergency is identified, the supplies should be audited to verify preparedness. The site shall discuss the status of supplies as well as applicable EOP(s) that will be needed to maintain the site during the event with site and regional leadership. First Aid Kits should also be verified prior to the event. Onsite management will develop schedules and turnover as applicable.
    3. Minimum Supplies to Procure:
        6. Bedding for two people. Cots, pillows, and multiple sets of linen for each bed.
        7. Five gallons of water, assumption for one-half gallon per person for five days.
        8. Meals to support two people for 5 days. Fifteen MREs suggested for long term storage. Snacks with a long shelf life are also recommended. Disposable dinning products for meals.
        9. Hygiene products to last a minimum of five days. Toothpaste, Toothbrush, Comb, hand sanitizer, shampoo/bodywash, disposable razors/shaving gel, and sanitary products.
        10. Storage for the emergency supplies such as a Westward Jobsite Chest with Casters.
    4. Directives to employees that will staff the site in the emergency.
        11. Bring overnight bag with multiple changes of clothes to last the duration of the emergency.
        12. Bring computer, cell phone, and charge cables.
        13. Verify personal responsibilities are or will be taken care of for the duration of the emergency.
        14. Consider arriving to the site in public transportation/taxi/ride share to prevent damage to personal vehicles.
