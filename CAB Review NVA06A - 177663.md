## Gap Analysis and Recommendations

- ✅ **Complete/Aligned:** The JSA broadly aligns with the MOP’s major work phases: opening/switching, live-dead-live testing, removal of the damaged harmonic filter, installation of the new harmonic filter, and closing/energization.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The MOP identifies specific LOTO actions for **HF-P3-110-FB**, **ACC-1-07-FB**, and **ACC-1-08-FB**, but the JSA only lists “Opening/CKT” and does not clearly include equipment identification, LOTO application, LOTO verification, recorded LOTO identifiers, or unexpected energization controls.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The JSA’s Live-Dead-Live row lists only “arc flash.” It should also include electrical shock, exposed conductors, incorrect meter reading, test lead failure, and the need to use a properly rated/calibrated meter with live-dead-live verification at the MOP’s listed test points.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The MOP includes removal of terminations, conduit connections, platform tie-ins, and the damaged harmonic filter. The JSA combines this into one removal row and does not provide enough controls for termination removal, conduit work, dropped hardware, sharp edges, glycol residue, or material-handling hazards.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The MOP includes transporting the new harmonic filter through the loading dock and setting it in place. The JSA install row partially covers lifting support, but it does not address the travel path, loading dock movement, spotters, pinch/crush zones, struck-by hazards, or exclusion zone controls.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The MOP includes torqueing connections, tying in conduit, tying in power connections, verifying CTs, and checking torque marks. These are not specifically captured in the JSA and should be added or incorporated into more detailed install tasks.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** The MOP specifies detailed switching PPE, including **40 cal/cm² arc-rated clothing**, arc flash suit or arc-rated shirt/pants, face shield with balaclava, Class 0 gloves with leather protectors, hard hat, safety glasses/goggles, hearing protection, and leather work shoes. The JSA only lists Class 0 gloves, arc flash gear, and safety shoes, and should be updated to match the MOP where switching/energization is performed.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** Startup/commissioning is not adequately covered in the JSA. The MOP states the harmonic filter becomes energized and ready for configuration/commissioning, and vendor commissioning follows manufacturer recommendations. The JSA should include controls for personnel clearances, covers/guards installed, restricted access, abnormal-condition stop work, and manufacturer/vendor requirements.
    
- ⚠️ **Missing or Incomplete:** Housekeeping and debris/tool control should be added. The MOP includes clean walk and checking torque marks, while the JSA only mentions “cuts and debris” without specific housekeeping or tool-accountability controls.
    
- ✅ **No Clearly Irrelevant Hazards Identified:** The JSA hazards listed—arc flash, electrical shock/burns, cuts/debris, and strains—are relevant to the MOP. The issue is that they are too broad and incomplete, not that they are unrelated.
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Replace generic task names such as “Opening/CKT,” “Install new HF-P3-110,” and “Closing/CKT” with task descriptions tied to the MOP’s actual physical work: LOTO/switching, live-dead-live testing, termination/conduit removal, equipment removal, equipment transport, setting/landing unit, torque/conduit/power tie-in, CT verification, clean walk, LOTO removal, energization, and commissioning support.
    
- 💡 **Recommendation:** Update the JSA controls to use action-oriented language, such as: “Apply LOTO and verify zero energy with a calibrated meter,” “Use rated lifting equipment and spotters; keep personnel clear of pinch/crush zones,” “Use calibrated torque tools and mark completed torque points,” and “Confirm all covers, tools, and personnel are clear before energization.”
