EHS Assessment Rollout — East Region Sites

Purpose:
Discuss the rollout of the standardized Crit Ops EHS Assessment framework (Pilot 4) across our East region sites. This meeting will cover the initiative overview, logistics, site-level responsibilities, and target due dates.

Agenda:
• Overview of the Pilot 4 assessment framework — what it is, how it works in Asana, and why we're scaling it
• East region site scope — confirm which sites are in the initial rollout (ATL01A, NAL01A, and any additional East region facilities)
• Roles & responsibilities — what site-level participation looks like for each audit cycle
• Portfolio structure — how site projects will roll up into regional portfolios for visibility and reporting
• Timeline & due dates — target dates for template deployment, first audit completion, and portfolio standup
• Open questions and next steps

Context:
We've built a ~100-task EHS audit template in Asana with standardized custom fields (Priority, Audit Type, Verification Method, Regulatory Reference, Frequency, Status) that are now in our org-level library. The goal is to deploy this consistently across all Operations sites using Rule Bundles and Asana Portfolios so leadership has real-time, cross-site compliance visibility without manual reporting. This conversation is the first step in socializing the initiative across the East region.

The big picture: instead of managing audits in spreadsheets site by site, we'll have one standardized template that deploys automatically, with everything rolling up into a portfolio dashboard. I want your input on the logistics and sequencing before we start
