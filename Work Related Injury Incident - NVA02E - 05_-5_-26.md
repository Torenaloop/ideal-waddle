**From:** Daniel Stevenson

**Subject:** Work Related Injury Incident 5/5/26

Team –

 

Timeline of Events – COT Left Thumb Injury

 

Date of Incident: Yesterday

Approximate Time of Incident: 8:08 AM

Location: NVA02D, near the exterior exit to the generator yard

Task Being Performed: Floor tape replacement

 

8:08 AM – Injury Occurred

At approximately 8:08 AM, a COT sustained a laceration to his left thumb while using a cutter blade during floor tape replacement activities near the exterior exit to the generator yard at NVA02D. Root Cause of Injury was a failure to wear cut resistant gloves while using the cutter blade.

 

Shortly After Incident – Notification to Management

Following the injury, the COT proceeded to the Critical Operations office and notified me that he had cut his finger and wanted to seek medical attention at Urgent Care.

 

Initial Response – First Aid Support Provided

Another COT and I retrieved supplies from the First Aid Kit located in the Critical Operations office. Without making direct contact with the injury, we provided the injured COT with gauze and a bandage so he could apply pressure to the wound.

 

Medical Escort Provided

I informed the COT escorting the injured COT to create a SOSPES injury form using the details provided by the injured COT. An escort was arranged to transport the injured COT to UVA Urgent Care for evaluation and treatment.

 

UVA Urgent Care Evaluation

Upon arrival and evaluation at UVA Urgent Care, the medical staff determined they were unable to treat the injury due to the severity of the laceration.

 

Transfer to Inova Urgent Care

The COT was then transported to Inova Urgent Care, where his left thumb was treated and bandaged. Medical guidance was provided indicating that the injury would likely require skin graft surgery to heal properly.

 

Approximately 11:00 AM – Return to Site

The COT returned to NVA02D at approximately 11:00 AM with his left thumb bandaged.

 

Post-Incident Work Status

Upon returning to site, the COT did not perform any further physical work or labor. For the remainder of the workday, his duties were limited to escorting a vendor only.
